Alpharetta might be the easiest place in metro Atlanta to do Christmas with kids. Within a few miles you have three free tree lightings, an outdoor ice rink, a mile of free lights, a snow day, a winter parade and at least a dozen chances to see Santa. Here is the 2026 family plan, with dates, times and what costs money, sorted so you can pick the best fit for your kids' ages.

Top picks by age

  • Toddlers and preschoolers: Free Pictures with Santa downtown (Dec. 5), Snow Day at City Center (Dec. 5), Story Time with Santa at Avalon and Noon Year's Eve on Dec. 31.
  • Elementary-age kids: Avalon on Ice, the Lighting of Avalon (Nov. 22), Lights on the Loop and the Alpharetta Winter Parade (Dec. 12).
  • Tweens and teens: evening skating at Avalon, the Lights on the Loop themed weekends and a drive-thru light show at Lanier Islands.

Tips: at the free downtown photos on December 5, skip green clothing because the photographer uses a green screen, and leave pets at home. Photos are printed on site and emailed. For Avalon's ticketed Santa visits, book early weekend slots as soon as they open; December Saturdays go first. Staying at The Hotel at Avalon? Its Santa Tuck-Ins add-on runs November 20 to December 23.

Ice skating at Avalon

Avalon on Ice runs from November 22, 2026 through January 18, 2027. Tickets are $14 for kids 12 and under and $18 for adults, with skates included. Weekday sessions run 3pm to 9pm, Saturday 10am to 10pm and Sunday noon to 7pm, and the rink stays open 10am to 10pm over Thanksgiving week, winter break (Dec. 20 to Jan. 3) and Fulton County school holidays. Every skater needs a waiver, and gloves make a big difference for small hands. Full details in our Avalon on Ice guide.

Christmas lights kids will love

  • Lights on the Loop (free): a one-mile walk-through light display on the Alpha Loop between Northwinds Parkway and Old Milton Parkway, nightly 5:30pm to 11pm, December 4, 2026 to January 4, 2027. On the themed weekends (Dec. 4 and 5, Dec. 18 and 19), the Lights & Delights Village adds characters, vendors and treats. Strollers work well on the paved trail.
  • Three tree lightings (free): The Hamilton on Nov. 20, downtown on Nov. 21 and Avalon on Nov. 22.
  • Magical Nights of Lights at Lanier Islands in Buford: a drive-thru show, November 20 to January 3, 5pm to 10pm, priced per vehicle. Perfect for little ones who fall asleep in the car.
  • Garden Lights, Holiday Nights at the Atlanta Botanical Garden: November 14 to January 10, timed tickets, kids 2 and under free.

Dates, prices and parking for each are in our Christmas lights near Alpharetta guide.

Free family events in December

  • Snow Day at Alpharetta City Center, Saturday, Dec. 5, 11am to 1pm: snow on the Town Green, live music and craft stations.
  • Alpharetta Christmas Market, Saturday, Dec. 5, 9am to 4pm, at Innovation Academy, 125 Milton Ave.
  • Friendship Ring of Kids Holiday Display, November 21 to December 31: the downtown sculpture at Innovation Academy gets lights and winter hats. A great free photo stop.
  • Menorah lightings: Avalon on Sunday, Dec. 6, 5pm to 6pm; Chanukah at Halcyon on Dec. 6 with children's entertainment; and downtown on the Town Green on Thursday, Dec. 10, 5pm to 6pm.
  • Makers Market at Avalon, Saturday, Dec. 12, 10am to 6pm.
  • Alpharetta Winter Parade, Saturday, Dec. 12, 5:30pm to 7pm, along Canton Street downtown.

Holiday meals with kids

  • Brunch with the Grinch at HOBNOB in Halcyon, Saturdays Dec. 12 and 19, with Grinch photos.
  • Breakfast with Santa at Avalon, Dec. 5 at Chick-fil-A and Dec. 6 at Oak Steakhouse.
  • Kids eat free at Cherry Street Brewpub on Tuesdays, 5pm to 9pm, and at HOBNOB Sunday through Thursday.

Noon Year's Eve for little ones

Kids who will not make it to midnight can count down at Noon Year's Eve at The Hotel at Avalon on Thursday, December 31, with live music, face painting, crafts and a giant balloon drop at noon. Last year's party ran 10am to 12:30pm, with $20 tickets for everyone over age 2, bounce houses and a kids-only bubbly bar; check Avalon's events page for 2026 ticket details. Avalon on Ice stays open 10am to 10pm that day.

A sample Saturday

  1. 10am: Free Pictures with Santa at the Alpharetta Welcome Center (Dec. 5).
  2. 11am: Snow Day on the Town Green, then lunch downtown. See our downtown restaurant guide.
  3. 3pm: skate at Avalon on Ice, then a scoop at Jeni's.
  4. 6pm: walk Lights on the Loop, which starts at 5:30pm and is free.

Tips for parents

  • Book ticketed Santa visits, Breakfast with Santa, Brunch with the Grinch and Noon Year's Eve as soon as they open.
  • Parking is free at Avalon's decks and Halcyon's deck, but fills on event nights; see our Avalon parking guide.
  • Avalon has a curfew: under 17 needs a parent after 8pm.
  • Bring layers. Skating, the parade and Lights on the Loop are all outdoors.
