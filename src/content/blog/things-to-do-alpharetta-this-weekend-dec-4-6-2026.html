The first weekend of December is one of the busiest of the year in Alpharetta. From Friday, Dec. 4 through Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026, Lights on the Loop switches on for the season, Snow Day returns to the Town Green, Santa poses for free photos downtown, a Christmas market fills Milton Avenue and Avalon lights its menorah for Hanukkah. Most of it is free. Here's the plan, with times, prices and addresses.

Quick answer: the weekend at a glance

  • Fri., Dec. 4, 5:30 p.m.: Lights on the Loop "Switch On The Lights" celebration on the Alpha Loop, 2500 Northwinds Pkwy. Free. Holiday Music Showcase runs 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. Friday and Saturday.
  • Sat., Dec. 5, 8 to 10 a.m.: Breakfast with Santa at Avalon ($30 adults, $25 kids) and at Dave & Buster's (9 to 10 a.m.).
  • Sat., Dec. 5, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.: Alpharetta Christmas Market at Innovation Academy, 125 Milton Ave. Free admission.
  • Sat., Dec. 5, 10 a.m. to noon: Free Photos with Santa at the Alpharetta Welcome Center, 178 S. Main St.
  • Sat., Dec. 5, 11 a.m. to 1 p.m.: Snow Day at Alpharetta City Center. Free.
  • Sun., Dec. 6, 5 to 6 p.m.: Menorah Lighting at Avalon with Chabad of North Fulton. Free.
  • Sun., Dec. 6, 6 p.m.: Handel's Messiah at St. Aidan's Episcopal Church in Milton.

Planning further out? Our Christmas and holiday events guide covers the whole season, and the events calendar is updated daily.

Friday: Lights on the Loop switches on

The headline event this weekend is opening night of Lights on the Loop, the Alpha Loop Foundation's month-long holiday light trail. The display covers a one-mile stretch of the Alpha Loop between Northwinds Parkway and Old Milton Parkway, and it's free.

  • Opening ceremony: the "Switch On The Lights" celebration starts at 5:30 p.m. Friday, Dec. 4.
  • Holiday Music Showcase: Dec. 4 and 5, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m., with the Alpharetta Symphony, Alpharetta City Band, local musicians and a holiday DJ.
  • Lights & Delights Village: behind 2500 Northwinds Pkwy. on themed weekends, with artisan vendors, food and non-alcoholic drinks for sale, seasonal characters and photo spots.
  • Parking: free at 2500 and 2520 Northwinds Pkwy. An ADA-accessible ramp is behind 2520 Northwinds Pkwy.
  • Good to know: leashed pets are welcome, the event is alcohol-free, and portable restrooms are only available on themed weekends. Organizers encourage a free Eventbrite registration for the themed weekends.

If you miss opening weekend, the lights stay on nightly from 5:30 to 11 p.m. through Jan. 4, 2027. For more displays around the area, see our Christmas lights near Alpharetta roundup.

Want a grown-up night out instead? UP on the Roof at 33 S. Main St. is running its Elf'd UP Christmas pop-up, with kitschy decor and holiday cocktails, listed from 3 to 11 p.m. all three days. Over at Roaring Social, 35 Milton Ave., the funk and soul band Bumpin' the Mango plays Friday from 8 p.m.

Saturday morning: Santa, a Christmas market and snow

Saturday is the day to bring the kids. The events are close together downtown, so you can stack two or three.

Breakfast with Santa at Avalon (8 to 8:45 a.m. or 9:15 to 10 a.m.). Avalon's Saturday seatings are a breakfast combo at Chick-fil-A with a Santa visit and holiday surprises. Tickets are $30 for adults and $25 for kids. Sunday's seating, 8:30 to 10 a.m. at Oak Steakhouse, is $60 for adults and $36 for kids. Tickets went on sale Nov. 5, so check availability early.

Breakfast with Santa at Dave & Buster's (9 to 10 a.m.). Dave & Buster's at 6500 North Point Pkwy. opens early for a breakfast banquet, a keepsake Santa photo, letter writing and crafts. Spots are limited, and guests with play tickets can hit the arcade floor with Santa.

Alpharetta Christmas Market (9 a.m. to 4 p.m.). Crafts, food, decor, jewelry and other gifts at Innovation Academy, 125 Milton Ave. Admission is free. For more shopping, see our holiday markets guide.

Free Photos with Santa (10 a.m. to noon). The Alpharetta Convention and Visitors Bureau hosts free individual or family photos with Santa at the Alpharetta Welcome Center, 178 S. Main St., Suite 200. Photos are printed on site and emailed to you. Two tips: don't wear green (the photographer uses a green screen), and leave pets at home. Questions go to 678-297-2811.

Snow Day at ACC (11 a.m. to 1 p.m.). The Town Green at Alpharetta City Center, 2001 Commerce St., turns into a winter wonderland with snow, live music, craft stations and photo spots. Free admission.

Between stops, the Friendship Ring of Kids holiday display at the Alpharetta Community Center, 175 Roswell St., is a quick free photo op (9 a.m. to 5 p.m.), and the Alpharetta Arts Center at 238 Canton St. has its "Wonder" winter exhibition. Need lunch? Our downtown Alpharetta restaurants list is all walkable.

Saturday afternoon and night

  • Avalon on Ice: Saturday hours are 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Tickets are $18, or $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. Reservations are recommended; walk-ups are welcome but not guaranteed. More in our Avalon on Ice guide.
  • Santa at Avalon: Santa's cottage in Palmer Plaza is open 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Friday and Saturday (with breaks 1 to 2 p.m. and 4 to 5 p.m.). Appointments were fully booked when we checked, but walk-ins can join a text waitlist. Packages start at $40. See our Santa photos guide for other options.
  • Avalon Express: the kids' train rides along the Boulevard from 2 to 8 p.m. for $5 per person.
  • Once Upon A Merry Maze: a Christmas-themed maze at Once Upon a Maze, 1000 North Point Circle, from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Saturday and noon to 4 p.m. Sunday.
  • La Candela Flamenco: "Once Upon a Winter in Spain" at The Spotlight Community Stage, 1000 North Point Circle, at 7 p.m.
  • Live music: Band X plays hits from the '70s to today at Roaring Social from 8 p.m.

Staying over? The Hotel at Avalon offers Tuck-ins with Santa from 7 to 10 p.m., when Santa and an elf knock on your room door with milk and cookies.

Sunday: Menorah lighting and Messiah

Menorah Lighting at Avalon (5 to 6 p.m.). Chabad of North Fulton helps light Avalon's menorah on the Boulevard in front of The Cape, with treats for guests. It's free. Hanukkah 2026 starts the evening of Friday, Dec. 4, so this lands on the third night. Our Hanukkah in Alpharetta guide lists the other lightings, including downtown on Dec. 10.

Handel's Messiah (6 p.m., doors 5:30). St. Aidan's Episcopal Church, 13560 Cogburn Rd. in Milton, presents a full Messiah with choir, orchestra and soloists, conducted by Trey Clegg. Parking is free; tickets are on Eventbrite.

Earlier in the day: Breakfast with Santa at Oak Steakhouse (8:30 to 10 a.m.), Avalon on Ice from noon to 7 p.m., and Fermented on Canton Street hosts a Natural Wine Experience Christmas Showcase from 2 to 5 p.m. ($50 for 20 wines plus charcuterie).

Tips for the weekend

  • Go early on Saturday. The Welcome Center photos, the Christmas Market and Snow Day are all within a few blocks downtown between 9 a.m. and 1 p.m.
  • Lights on the Loop parking is at 2500 and 2520 Northwinds Pkwy., not downtown.
  • Check before you go. Lights on the Loop themed events run rain or shine except in heavy rain or severe weather, and times can change, so confirm on each organizer's site.
  • Next weekend: the Alpharetta Winter Parade is Saturday, Dec. 12.
