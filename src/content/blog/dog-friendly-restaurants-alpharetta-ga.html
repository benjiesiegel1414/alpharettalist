Alpharetta is an easy town to eat out with a dog. Between Avalon's dog park, Downtown's patios and a handful of breweries and beer gardens that welcome pups, you can make a whole day of it. Here are the dog-friendly restaurants and patios around Alpharetta, the rules to know, and a few tips for a smooth meal with your four-legged friend.

A quick note on the rules: Georgia's food code keeps dogs (other than service animals) out of restaurant dining rooms, so "dog friendly" almost always means the patio. Policies can change with the weather, the crowd or the manager on duty, so ask the host before you sit down.

Downtown Alpharetta

Truck & Tap (30 Milton Ave) is the classic downtown dog hangout: a mostly outdoor craft beer bar with a different food truck parked out front each day. BringFido lists seven dog-friendly tables. It opens at 3pm on weekdays and noon on weekends.

Smokejack Southern Grill & BBQ (29 S Main St) has dog-friendly outdoor seating in front of its historic brick building, and its pulled pork and ribs make for a very interested dog. Next door, Currahee Brewing (25 S Main St) has a patio where many locals bring dogs; reviewers report staff bringing water, but also that a barking dog may be asked to leave, so bring a calm pup.

Downtown also has the Town Green at City Center, where you can grab takeout and sit on the grass. See our downtown restaurant guide for more.

Avalon

Avalon is dog friendly: leashed dogs are welcome in the common areas, there is a fenced dog park behind Building 1000, and the concierge keeps water and dog treats on hand. Patios listed as dog friendly on BringFido include:

  • Barleygarden Kitchen & Craft Bar: 60-plus craft beers, burgers and a dog-friendly patio.
  • Ted's Montana Grill: 16 dog-friendly outdoor seats on Avalon Boulevard.
  • Rumi's Kitchen: dogs welcome at patio tables.
  • Colletta: five pet-friendly patio tables.
  • Crú Food & Wine Bar: 11 outdoor tables open to dog owners.

Halcyon

Halcyon welcomes leashed dogs in its common areas, with a few specific rules: leashes no longer than six feet, no more than two animals per person, owners 18 or older, and no dogs inside restaurants or food stores unless the business allows it. Order from the Market Hall or Char Pizzeria and eat on the Village Green, then walk the Big Creek Greenway, whose trail extension starts near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway. Dogtopia and Chewy Vet Care are on site too.

Around town

  • Lazy Dog Restaurant & Bar near North Point Mall on Haynes Bridge Road is built around dogs: it puts out water bowls on the patio and has a dog menu with grilled chicken or hamburger with rice. The chain notes that its Alpharetta location serves dog meals to go.
  • Ippolito's Family Style Italian (12850 Highway 9 N) has five pet-friendly outdoor tables, per BringFido.
  • Jerusalem Bakery & Grill (4150 Old Milton Pkwy) has four pet-friendly outdoor tables and a 100% halal menu.
  • Nahm Fine Thai Cuisine (5310 Windward Pkwy) sets out pet-friendly outdoor tables in nice weather; reservations are recommended.
  • Cherry Street Brewpub at Halcyon has a beer garden with live music, a good bet for a dog-friendly afternoon. Ask about the patio first.

Patio etiquette

  • Keep your dog leashed and close to your table, out of the server's path.
  • Bring a collapsible water bowl in case the restaurant does not have one.
  • Exercise your dog before dinner so it settles down. A walk on the Big Creek Greenway or the Alpha Loop works well.
  • Skip the busiest hours, like Friday and Saturday at 7pm, if your dog gets anxious in crowds.
  • Georgia summers are hot. Check the pavement with your hand and pick a shaded table.
  • If your dog barks or will not settle, take it outside for a minute. Patio privileges depend on it.
