Not cooking this year? Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and plenty of Alpharetta restaurants are open with turkey plates, three-course menus and full dinner menus. If you'd rather eat at home, several spots and grocery stores have heat-and-serve feasts you can pre-order now. Here's who is open, what it costs and the order deadlines to know.

Quick answer: Thanksgiving dinner in Alpharetta (2026)

  • UP on the Roof (downtown): open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., three-course Thanksgiving menu $49 per person.
  • Ruth's Chris Steak House Alpharetta: open 11:30 a.m. to 9 p.m., three-course prix fixe $52, kids 12 and under $21.
  • South City Kitchen Avalon: three-course Thanksgiving menu 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., $79 per adult, $39 per child.
  • Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar (Halcyon): open 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., four-course dinner $115 per adult, $20 per child.
  • Eddie V's: Thanksgiving dinner served 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., $52 per adult, $20 per child.
  • To-go deadlines coming up fast: Ruth's Chris Feast Bundle by Nov. 20, Ruth's sides by Nov. 23, Whole Foods and The Fresh Market by Nov. 24.

Thanksgiving is one of the busiest restaurant days of the year, so book now and call ahead to confirm hours.

Thanksgiving dinner in downtown Alpharetta

UP on the Roof at 33 South Main Street, right across from the Town Green, is open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Thanksgiving Day. The $49 three-course menu (before tax and tip) starts with a Caesar or beet salad, then an oven-roasted turkey plate with cornbread dressing, gravy and cranberry sauce plus a side, and ends with an almond cake topped with apple and cranberry compote. The full dinner menu is also available for anyone who'd rather have steak or seafood, and Wild Turkey bourbon shots are $8 that day only. Reservations are recommended.

Little Alley Steak at 102 S. Main St. posted on Instagram that all three of its locations, including Alpharetta, are open on Thanksgiving Day from noon to 9 p.m. Reserve ahead.

CRU Food & Wine Bar is also on the city tourism site's list of Alpharetta restaurants open for Thanksgiving. Call for hours and the menu. Planning a walk around downtown before or after dinner? See our best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta guide and the Downtown Alpharetta page.

Thanksgiving dinner at and near Avalon

South City Kitchen Avalon is serving a three-course Thanksgiving menu from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at all of its locations, including Avalon. It's $79 per adult and $39 per child, and reservations are highly encouraged, according to Rough Draft Atlanta.

Eddie V's Prime Seafood serves Thanksgiving dinner from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. The traditional dinner is $52 for adults and $20 for kids: slow-roasted sliced turkey, brioche sage stuffing, mashed potatoes, green beans, roasted butternut squash and cranberry chutney. A pumpkin pie tartlet with pecan ice cream is $13 extra, and the full menu is available too.

Oak Steakhouse (listed as Oak Steakhouse Atlanta, in Alpharetta) posted on Instagram that it's open 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Thanksgiving with a holiday menu. Kona Grill and Cooper's Hawk Winery & Restaurant also appear on Awesome Alpharetta's open-on-Thanksgiving list, so check with them for times.

Avalon itself gets festive that week, with Avalon on Ice open by then. Browse the Avalon guide for parking and more.

Steakhouses and a four-course feast

Ruth's Chris Steak House Alpharetta (770-777-1500) is open 11:30 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Thanksgiving. The $52 three-course prix fixe includes your choice of Caesar salad, house salad or seafood gumbo, then oven-roasted turkey breast with gravy, sausage-herb stuffing, garlic mashed potatoes, honey-glazed carrots and cranberry relish, and pumpkin cheesecake with vanilla ice cream. Kids 12 and under get a smaller turkey plate for $21. The full steak menu is available all day, and the holiday menu can't be modified.

Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar at Halcyon is open 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. with a four-course holiday dinner for $115 per adult or $20 per child, per Rough Draft Atlanta. Reservations are highly encouraged. Make a day of it with our Halcyon guide.

Thanksgiving breakfast before the big meal

The Original Pancake House is on Awesome Alpharetta's list of restaurants open on Thanksgiving, which makes it an easy morning stop with out-of-town family or after a turkey trot. Call ahead for holiday hours. For more morning ideas this holiday weekend, see our best brunch in Alpharetta list.

Thanksgiving dinner to go: order deadlines

Hosting but not cooking? These options serve Alpharetta. Prices and deadlines can vary by location, so double-check when you order.

  • Ruth's Chris Feast Bundle: a heat-and-serve turkey breast dinner for four is $195 with stuffing, gravy, cranberry relish, salad, sides and pumpkin cheesecake. Order by Friday, Nov. 20. Sides to go start at $49; order by Monday, Nov. 23.
  • Firebirds Wood Fired Grill at The Atwater, across from Avalon (2665 Old Milton Pkwy.), is taking pre-orders for a Thanksgiving Feast. Availability is limited, so order online soon.
  • Whole Foods Market Avalon: order at least 48 hours ahead, no later than Nov. 24, for pickup Nov. 20 to 26. Atlanta-area sample prices: Classic Roast Turkey Dinner for 8 at $170, turkey breast dinner for 4 at $90 and a sides-only package for 4 at $60.
  • Publix Deli: heat-and-serve turkey dinners start around $70 for eight people (prices vary by store). Order at least 48 hours before your pickup time.
  • The Fresh Market: order by Nov. 24 at 2 p.m. for pickup Nov. 23 to 26. Meals start at $80 for 3 to 5 people.
  • Sprouts: fully prepared meals from $49.99 to $129.99. Order by noon on Tuesday, Nov. 24.
  • Cracker Barrel (there's an Alpharetta location): the Heat 'n' Serve Feast for 8 to 10 is $190, or $120 for 4 to 6. Order 24 hours ahead for pickup Nov. 21 to 29.

Tips for booking Thanksgiving dinner out

  • Book this week. Prime 1 to 4 p.m. tables go first. OpenTable lets you search Alpharetta restaurants open on Thanksgiving by time.
  • Ask about big groups. Maximum party sizes vary, and some places offer private rooms.
  • Know the menu rules. Most holiday prix fixe menus don't allow substitutions, but almost every spot above also serves its regular menu.
  • Plan the rest of the week. Our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide covers turkey trots and kid-friendly ideas, and our Black Friday 2026 hours post has North Point Mall, Avalon and Halcyon hours. The holiday season starts right after, so bookmark our Christmas events guide.
