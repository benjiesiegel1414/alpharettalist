Looking for Christmas lights near Alpharetta this year? You have plenty of choices: a free mile-long light trail on the Alpha Loop, three tree lightings in one weekend and some of metro Atlanta's biggest light shows within an easy evening drive. Here are the 2026 dates, hours and prices for each, plus tips for picking the right one for your crew.

Quick answer: 2026 Christmas lights near Alpharetta

  • Lights on the Loop (Alpharetta): Dec. 4, 2026 to Jan. 4, 2027, nightly 5:30 to 11 p.m. Free.
  • The Hamilton Alpharetta tree lighting: Friday, Nov. 20, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. Free.
  • Season of Celebration tree lighting (downtown Alpharetta): Saturday, Nov. 21, 5 to 8 p.m. Free.
  • Lighting of Avalon: Sunday, Nov. 22, 2 to 7:30 p.m., tree lights at 7 p.m. Free.
  • Garden Lights, Holiday Nights (Atlanta Botanical Garden): Nov. 14, 2026 to Jan. 10, 2027, nightly until midnight (last entry 11 p.m.). Timed tickets.
  • Magical Nights of Lights (Lanier Islands, Buford): Nov. 20, 2026 to Jan. 3, 2027, 5 to 10 p.m. Drive-thru, priced per vehicle.

Lights on the Loop: Alpharetta's free light trail

The big one in town is Lights on the Loop, a walk-through display along a one-mile stretch of the Alpha Loop between Northwinds Parkway and Old Milton Parkway. It runs nightly from Dec. 4, 2026 through Jan. 4, 2027, from 5:30 to 11 p.m., and admission is free. The Alpha Loop Foundation, a nonprofit, hosts it as its largest fundraiser of the year.

  • Opening night: the "Switch On The Lights" celebration is Friday, Dec. 4 at 5:30 p.m.
  • Holiday Music Showcase: Dec. 4 and 5, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m., with the Alpharetta Symphony, Alpharetta City Band, local musicians and a holiday DJ.
  • Pet-Friendly Weekend with Llamas on the Loop: Dec. 18 and 19, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. Leashed pets are welcome, but keep them at least 6 feet from the llamas.
  • Parking: free at 2500 and 2520 Northwinds Parkway. An ADA-accessible ramp to the trail is behind 2520 Northwinds Parkway.
  • Good to know: it's an alcohol-free event, and portable restrooms are only set up on the themed weekends. Free Eventbrite registration is encouraged for the themed weekends.

On themed weekends, the Lights & Delights Village behind 2500 Northwinds Parkway adds artisan vendors, seasonal food and drinks, family entertainment and holiday characters for photos. There's also a $10 raffle for an e-bike donated by an Alpharetta company, with the winner drawn Dec. 19.

Three tree lightings in one weekend

The weekend before Thanksgiving is when Alpharetta flips the switch on the season. All three are free:

  • Friday, Nov. 20: The Hamilton Alpharetta hotel hosts its annual Christmas tree lighting at 25 Milton Ave., 5:30 to 8:30 p.m.
  • Saturday, Nov. 21: the city's Season of Celebration at the City Hall main stage in Downtown Alpharetta, 5 to 8 p.m. Expect a 50-foot tree, live holiday music, snow flurries, strolling characters and a visit from Santa. Bring a portable chair.
  • Sunday, Nov. 22: the Lighting of Avalon, 2 to 7:30 p.m., with festive music and free family activities before Santa leads the countdown to the 40-foot tree at 7 p.m. Parking fills fast: use Avalon's free decks or valet, or park at Gwinnett Technical College across the street and take the shuttle.

Avalon on Ice opens the same day, so you can make it a skate-and-lights evening. Our Avalon on Ice guide has rink hours and prices. Once the trees are lit, downtown and Avalon are both easy, free places to stroll the lights on any December night, with plenty of spots for dinner nearby (see our downtown Alpharetta restaurants guide).

Garden Lights, Holiday Nights in Midtown

If you only do one ticketed show, Atlanta Botanical Garden's Garden Lights, Holiday Nights is the region's classic. It's in its 16th year and won ABC's "The Great Christmas Light Fight Heavyweights Edition" in 2023. The 2026 run is Nov. 14, 2026 through Jan. 10, 2027, and the garden now stays open nightly until midnight, with last entry at 11 p.m.

  • What's new: expanded displays along Flower Walk, the return of the Imaginary Worlds mosaiculture dragon to the Skyline Garden and new choreography for Nature's Wonders, the garden's curtain of light and sound.
  • Tickets: timed entry, with General Admission, Premium and Premium Plus tiers. Your time controls when you enter, not how long you stay. Kids 2 and under are free.
  • Save money: Night Light Tickets after 9:30 p.m. are available every night, starting at $19.95 for adults, according to 365 Atlanta's ticket guide. Garden members save $5 per ticket.
  • Parking: the shared SAGE deck costs $20 per vehicle (card only) and fills on busy nights, so build in extra time.
  • Weather: the show is rain or shine. Add Flex Tix at checkout if you want the option to change your date.

Buy only from the garden's own site. It warns that it can't guarantee tickets from resellers.

Magical Nights of Lights at Lanier Islands

Prefer to stay warm in the car? Magical Nights of Lights at Lanier Islands Resort in Buford is a drive-thru show with miles of animated, sparkling displays. It runs Nov. 20, 2026 through Jan. 3, 2027, from 5 to 10 p.m., and pricing is per vehicle. Tune in to holiday music, take it at your own pace, and if you want to stretch the evening out, the resort's Game Changer venue becomes a holiday headquarters with firepits, games and a holiday pop-up bar. The resort is at 7000 Lanier Islands Parkway; call 770-945-8787 with questions.

More drive-thru options north of Atlanta

Holiday Lights of Georgia, which runs the long-running drive-thru Holiday Lights at Veterans Park in Canton (7345 Cumming Hwy.), says it's bringing shows to four locations this year: Canton, Woodstock, Ellijay and Rome. New for 2026 is a "Golden Ticket" season pass good at all four. Check its website for this year's dates and prices before you go.

Tips for a better light night

  • Go early in the week. Weekends and the nights closest to Christmas are the busiest everywhere.
  • Book ahead for ticketed shows. Popular Garden Lights dates can sell out.
  • Dress in layers. Lights on the Loop and Garden Lights are outdoor walks, and it gets chilly after dark.
  • Pair it with dinner or brunch. Try our best brunch in Alpharetta list for the morning after, or browse Alpharetta restaurants.
