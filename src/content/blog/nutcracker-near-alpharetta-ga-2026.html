Few holiday traditions fill north Fulton calendars like The Nutcracker. In 2026, Alpharetta families can choose from a hometown studio production, a big-stage show in Sandy Springs that opens the day after Thanksgiving, the full Atlanta Ballet run at Cobb Energy Centre, a 35th-anniversary staging up GA 400 in Cumming, and a touring show at the Fox. Here are the dates, showtimes, venues and ticket details we could confirm, plus tips for picking the right one for your crew.

Quick answer: Nutcracker near Alpharetta in 2026

  • Roswell Dance Theatre, Byers Theatre (Sandy Springs): Nov. 27 to Dec. 6, 2026. Ten shows, tickets $30 to $55 plus fees.
  • Atlanta Ballet, Cobb Energy Centre: Dec. 5 to 26, 2026, with live orchestra. 19 performances, including a 1 p.m. show on Christmas Eve.
  • Sawnee Ballet Theatre, FoCAL Center (Cumming): Dec. 5, 6, 12 and 13, 2026. The production's 35th year.
  • Nutcracker! Magical Christmas Ballet, Fox Theatre: Dec. 23 and 24, 2026. A touring show with acrobatics and puppetry.
  • Metropolitan Ballet Theatre (Alpharetta): the hometown production usually lands in mid-December. Check the company for 2026 dates.

Roswell Dance Theatre at City Springs (Nov. 27 to Dec. 6)

The closest big-stage option is Roswell Dance Theatre's Nutcracker, presented with the Tolbert Yilmaz School of Dance at City Springs' Byers Theatre, 1 Galambos Way in Sandy Springs. The cast mixes professional guest artists with company students and dancers of all ages from the school. It opens the day after Thanksgiving, which makes it an easy add to a long holiday weekend.

2026 showtimes:

  • Friday, Nov. 27: 2 p.m. and 7 p.m.
  • Saturday, Nov. 28: 11 a.m. and 4 p.m.
  • Sunday, Nov. 29: 1 p.m. and 5 p.m.
  • Friday, Dec. 4: 7 p.m.
  • Saturday, Dec. 5: 2 p.m. and 7 p.m.
  • Sunday, Dec. 6: 2 p.m.

Four shows (Nov. 28 at 11 a.m. and 4 p.m., Dec. 5 at 2 p.m. and Dec. 6 at 2 p.m.) include Character Meet and Greets, with early lobby access for photos and autographs. That is a big draw for younger kids.

Tickets: $30 to $55 plus box office fees, sold only through the Sandy Springs Performing Arts Center. The company specifically warns about resale sites charging inflated prices, so buy from the official box office link. Groups of 10 or more can get discounts by calling the Byers Theatre box office at 770-206-2022.

Good to know: the show runs about two hours with a 20-minute intermission. Doors open an hour before each show, event parking in the underground deck is $10, and valet is about $15 for most performances. Bags are limited to 12 x 6 x 12 inches.

Atlanta Ballet at Cobb Energy Centre (Dec. 5 to 26)

For the full-scale version, Atlanta Ballet's Nutcracker runs Dec. 5 to 26, 2026 at the Cobb Energy Performing Arts Centre, 2800 Cobb Galleria Parkway. This is Yuri Possokhov's choreography with Tchaikovsky's score performed live by the Atlanta Ballet Orchestra. The performance runs 2 hours and 8 minutes, including intermission.

2026 showtimes:

  • Saturday, Dec. 5: 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.
  • Sunday, Dec. 6: 2 p.m.
  • Friday, Dec. 11: 7:30 p.m.
  • Saturday, Dec. 12 and Sunday, Dec. 13: 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.
  • Friday, Dec. 18: 7:30 p.m.
  • Saturday, Dec. 19 and Sunday, Dec. 20: 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.
  • Tuesday, Dec. 22 and Wednesday, Dec. 23: 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.
  • Thursday, Dec. 24 (Christmas Eve): 1 p.m.
  • Saturday, Dec. 26: 2 p.m.

Tickets: sold through Ticketmaster from the Cobb Energy Centre and Atlanta Ballet sites. Prices vary by date and seat, so compare a few performances. The weekday matinees during winter break (Dec. 22 and 23) can be a nice option once school is out.

Good to know: on-site parking is $20 per vehicle, credit card only, and there is no prepaid parking. Doors open 90 minutes before showtime, bags larger than 11 x 13 inches are not allowed, and there is no coat check, so plan to keep jackets with you. The venue also sells a Skyline Lounge add-on with pre-show and intermission access, hors d'oeuvres and a bar.

Sawnee Ballet Theatre in Cumming (Dec. 5 to 13)

Heading north instead of south? Sawnee Ballet Theatre celebrates its 35th annual Nutcracker in 2026 at The FoCAL Center, 1150 Dahlonega Highway in Cumming.

  • Saturday, Dec. 5 and Saturday, Dec. 12: 7:30 p.m.
  • Sunday, Dec. 6 and Sunday, Dec. 13: 2 p.m.

The show runs about two hours, including a 15-minute intermission, and is recommended for ages 3 and up. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Tickets are sold online through the company and at the FoCAL Center box office; prices were not posted on the show page when we checked. Photos and video are not allowed during the performance.

Nutcracker! Magical Christmas Ballet at the Fox (Dec. 23 and 24)

If you want a different spin, the touring Nutcracker! Magical Christmas Ballet comes to the Fox Theatre, 660 Peachtree St. in Midtown, on Dec. 23 and 24, 2026. Now in its 34th year, it bills itself as the largest touring production of its kind in North America, with an international cast, acrobatics and puppetry. Midtown Alliance's listing shows a 3 p.m. performance on Dec. 23; check the Fox box office for the full schedule and prices.

The hometown option: Metropolitan Ballet Theatre

Metropolitan Ballet Theatre, the ballet school and company on North Main Street, has staged a full-length Nutcracker for more than two decades. Last year's run was in mid-December at the Innovation Academy Theater on Milton Avenue, with a Sunday matinee paired with a Sugarplum Storytime for kids before the show. The company had not posted 2026 performance details on the pages we could read, so check its site or call before planning around it. Smaller studios, including Core Dance and Acrobatics Studios, also put on holiday productions each December.

Which Nutcracker should you pick?

  • Shortest drive and lowest ticket price: Roswell Dance Theatre at Byers Theatre, a straight shot down GA 400 to Sandy Springs.
  • The full professional production with live orchestra: Atlanta Ballet at Cobb Energy Centre.
  • Thanksgiving weekend plans: Roswell Dance Theatre's Nov. 27 to 29 shows. Pair one with our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide and Black Friday hours.
  • Little ones meeting Clara and the Sugar Plum Fairy: one of the Character Meet and Greet shows at Byers Theatre.
  • Christmas Eve tradition: Atlanta Ballet's 1 p.m. matinee on Dec. 24, or the touring show at the Fox.

Tips before you buy

  • Buy from the official box office. Resale sites often list these shows well above face value.
  • Compare dates. Prices and seat choices can differ a lot between a weekend matinee and a weeknight show.
  • Arrive early. Doors open an hour before curtain at Byers Theatre and 90 minutes before at Cobb Energy Centre, which leaves time for parking, security and lobby photos.
  • Check bag limits. Both Byers Theatre and Cobb Energy Centre restrict bag sizes and screen guests.
