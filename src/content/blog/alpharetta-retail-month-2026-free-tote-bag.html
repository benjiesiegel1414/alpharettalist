November is Retail Month in Alpharetta, and shopping local comes with a perk this year. Spend $150 or more at qualifying Alpharetta shops between Nov. 1 and 30, 2026, bring your receipts to the Alpharetta Welcome Center downtown, and you can pick up a free Shops of Alpharetta tote bag. Here's how the giveaway works, which receipts count, and where to shop in each of the city's four retail districts.

How the Retail Month tote bag giveaway works

The promotion comes from the Alpharetta Convention & Visitors Bureau, which runs the city's tourism arm, Awesome Alpharetta. Here are the basics:

  • Spend: $150 or more at eligible Alpharetta retailers, shown on your receipts.
  • Dates: receipts must be dated Nov. 1 through Nov. 30, 2026.
  • Pick up: bring your receipts to the Alpharetta Welcome Center, Park Plaza, 178 S. Main St., Suite 200, in downtown Alpharetta.
  • Limit: one tote per household, while supplies last.

"Retail Month is an opportunity to celebrate the businesses that make Alpharetta a destination for shopping, dining and discovery," Janet Rodgers, president and CEO of the Alpharetta Convention & Visitors Bureau, told Appen Media.

Welcome Center hours (plan around the weekend)

One thing to know before you go: the Welcome Center is a weekday stop. Its posted hours are Monday through Thursday, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. A virtual information kiosk is available on Saturdays from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., but if you shop on a weekend, hang on to your receipts and drop by during the week. Since supplies are limited, earlier in the month is the safer bet. You can call the Welcome Center at 678-297-2811 with questions.

Which receipts count (and which don't)

The giveaway is meant to spotlight local shops and boutiques. Eligible purchases come from stores in Downtown Alpharetta, along North Main Street, at Avalon or inside North Point Mall.

Receipts from these big-box and chain stores are not eligible: Target, Costco, TJ Maxx, REI, Ross, HomeSense, Lowe's, Home Depot, Sam's Club, Walgreens, CVS and Best Buy. If you're unsure about a store, ask the Welcome Center staff before you count on it.

Where to shop: Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is the walkable, small-town side of Alpharetta shopping, and it's where you'll pick up your tote anyway. Retail Month organizers point shoppers to The Pink Valise, Clothes Horse men's apparel on Milton Avenue and Mountain High Outfitters for outdoor gear. A few more local favorites from our directory:

  • Sis + Moons, an eclectic Milton Avenue shop with home goods, local art, vintage finds, candles and clothing.
  • The Red Hound, a gift and home decor shop at Alpharetta City Center.
  • Comeback Vinyl for new and used records, a natural stop for the music fan on your list.
  • Alpharetta Outfitters, a fly fishing and outdoor shop on South Main Street.
  • Kilwins on Market Street for fudge and chocolates to round out a gift basket.

Where to shop: Avalon

Avalon is about a mile from downtown, and you can walk or bike between the two on the Alpha Loop. Its Boulevard mixes fashion, jewelry, books and home decor with plenty of places to eat. Retailers highlighted for Retail Month include Anthropologie, Alo Yoga, Kendra Scott, Free People, Lilly Pulitzer, Posman Books and Watches of Switzerland. You'll also find Crate & Barrel and lululemon there.

Tip: pair a shopping afternoon with dinner from our Alpharetta restaurants guide, and keep an eye on the holiday events roundup for Avalon's tree lighting and ice rink.

Where to shop: Windward and North Main Street

Head north for a different kind of treasure hunt. Queen of Hearts, a large antique and home furnishings market on North Main Street, is a great place to find one-of-a-kind gifts and decor. For riders, Greenhawk carries equestrian accessories in the Windward area.

Where to shop: North Point Mall area

North Point Mall brings the department stores, including Von Maur, Dillard's and Macy's. Upstairs, The Scene Art Gallery, run by the nonprofit Alpha Arts Guild, sells work by Georgia artists, which makes for a memorable local gift. The North Point area also has specialty shops like Cavender's Boot City nearby. Just remember that REI receipts are on the excluded list for the tote. Curious about the mall's future? Read our post on the North Point Mall redevelopment.

More local shopping dates this month

  • Saturday, Nov. 14: the final Alpharetta Farmers Market of the season, 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. on the City Center Town Green, with local farmers, bakers, makers and artisans.
  • Saturday, Nov. 21: the Artist Holiday Market at the Alpharetta Arts Center, 238 Canton St., 10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., with handmade jewelry, ceramics, seasonal items and wearable art.
  • Saturday, Nov. 28: Small Business Saturday, the national shop-small day after Black Friday. It falls inside Retail Month, so those receipts count toward your tote too if they're from eligible shops.
