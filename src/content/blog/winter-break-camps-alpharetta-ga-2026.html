Fulton County Schools lets out for winter break on Friday, Dec. 18, and kids don't go back until Tuesday, Jan. 5. That's two full weeks plus a teacher workday for working parents to cover. The good news: Alpharetta has a solid lineup of short holiday camps, from $45 city dance mornings to week-long rock band camps, and many of them let you book one day at a time. Here are the 2026 dates, hours, ages and prices we confirmed, plus a few no-camp ideas for the days in between.

Quick answer: winter break camps in Alpharetta (2026)

  • Fulton County Schools winter break: Mon., Dec. 21, 2026 through Fri., Jan. 1, 2027. Mon., Jan. 4 is a teacher workday (no school for students), and students return Tues., Jan. 5.
  • Cheapest single days: City of Alpharetta dance camps ($45 residents) and gymnastics or ninja camps ($60 residents), all half-day mornings.
  • Full-day options: School of Rock Alpharetta (9 a.m. to 3 p.m., $449 a week), Building Character theater camp (10 a.m. to 4 p.m., $269 for Dec. 28 to 30) and Snapology in Johns Creek (9 a.m. to 4 p.m., $79 a day).
  • Art camp downtown: Kidcreate at the Alpharetta Arts Center, Dec. 28 to 30, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., $195 for residents.
  • Jan. 4 coverage: Snapology ($79) and a Kidcreate mini camp at the Arts Center ($80 residents) both run on the teacher workday.

Winter break 2026 dates for Fulton County Schools

According to the 2026-27 Fulton County Schools calendar, the first semester ends Fri., Dec. 18. Winter break runs Mon., Dec. 21 through Fri., Jan. 1. Mon., Jan. 4 is a professional development day with no school for students, and the second semester starts Tues., Jan. 5. If your kids attend a private school or a school outside Fulton County, double-check their calendar, because break dates can differ.

Most camps below skip Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day, so plan on the camp weeks being three days long: Dec. 21 to 23 and Dec. 28 to 30.

Art camps at the Alpharetta Arts Center

Kidcreate Studio runs city art camps at the Alpharetta Arts Center on Canton Street in downtown Alpharetta:

  • New Year Break: Winter Magic Makers, Dec. 28 to 30, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., ages 5 to 12. $195 for residents, $292.50 for non-residents.
  • Laughter Art Mini Camp, Mon., Jan. 4, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., ages 5 to 12. $80 for residents, $120 for non-residents. Handy for the teacher workday.

Since the Arts Center is right downtown, a half-day camp pairs well with lunch nearby. Our list of the best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta has ideas.

Music and theater camps

School of Rock Alpharetta (5970 Atlanta Hwy.) has two Holiday Rock Camps for ages 7 to 17 with no experience needed: Dec. 21 to 24 and a second session listed as Dec. 28 to Jan. 1, both 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. for $449. Campers learn bass, drums, guitar, keys or vocals in a band and play a show for families at the end of the week. School of Rock's site lists a Black Friday code, ROCKBLACKFRIDAY, for 25% off select camps through Nov. 28.

Building Character runs its performing arts winter camp Dec. 28 to 30, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., at Atlanta Dance Theatre (4150 Old Milton Pkwy., Suite 210). It costs $269, is for ages 5 to 12, and covers on-camera acting, hip-hop, stage combat and musical theater, with a presentation at the end. More options are in our dance studios and kids activities directories.

STEM and LEGO camps

Snapology of Alpharetta holds its school break camps at Homeschool Haven Collective, 10700 Abbotts Bridge Rd. in Johns Creek, a short drive from Alpharetta. Each day pairs robotics and coding in the morning with themed LEGO building in the afternoon, for ages 5 to 12, from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

  • Dec. 21 to 23: Winter holiday robotics plus winter and holiday LEGO themes.
  • Dec. 28 to 30: Creature Creator robotics plus a Pokemon-inspired building theme.
  • Jan. 4: One-day camp with robotics and Minecraft-inspired building.

Days are $79 each or $219 for a three-day block. Early drop-off from 7:30 a.m. ($15 a day) and late pick-up until 6 p.m. ($20 a day) are available by advance reservation. Snapology also lists 50% off a sibling's registration.

No camp? Easy days out over break

  • Noon Year's Eve at Avalon (Dec. 31): A family party at The Hotel at Avalon from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. with live music, face painting, crafts and a balloon drop at noon. Tickets are $20 for everyone over age 2.
  • Avalon on Ice: The rink at Avalon is open through the holidays. Hours and prices are in our Avalon on Ice guide.
  • Holiday Houseplant Swap (Dec. 21): The city hosts a swap at the greenhouse at Old Rucker Farm (900 Rucker Rd.) from 9 to 11 a.m., with registration through the city's site.
  • Parks and lights: Burn off energy at Wills Park, then check our Christmas lights guide and Christmas with kids in Alpharetta for evening ideas.

Tips for booking winter break camp

  • Book early. Holiday camps are short and small. One city sewing workshop already showed just 2 spots left when we checked.
  • Read refund rules. School of Rock, for example, offers a 50% refund up to 15 days before camp and no refund within 14 days.
  • Pack lunch. Most camps here ask for a nut-free lunch, snacks and a labeled water bottle.
  • Check residency pricing. City camps cost about 50% more for non-residents, so confirm your address qualifies.
