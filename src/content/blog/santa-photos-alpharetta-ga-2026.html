Here are the 2026 dates, hours, prices and booking tips for every Santa photo spot in and around Alpharetta, so you can pick the one that fits your kids, your budget and your schedule.

Santa at Avalon: hours, prices and walk-ins

Santa's North Pole-inspired cottage sits in Palmer Plaza at Avalon, 400 Avalon Blvd., from Monday, Nov. 23 through Christmas Eve. You can book an appointment online or join as a walk-in: stop by the Santa House, the team adds your name to the list and texts you when it's your turn. Avalon notes walk-in spots aren't guaranteed and weekends are busier, so weekdays are your best bet without a reservation.

Regular Santa hours at Avalon:

  • Sundays: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. (breaks noon to 1 p.m. and 3 to 4 p.m.)
  • Mondays: noon to 6 p.m. (break 3 to 4 p.m.)
  • Tuesday to Thursday: noon to 8 p.m. (break 3 to 4 p.m.)
  • Friday and Saturday: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. (breaks 1 to 2 p.m. and 4 to 5 p.m.)

Holiday hours: closed Thanksgiving. Extended 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. hours on Sunday, Dec. 20 and on Dec. 22 and 23. Christmas Eve runs 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., walk-ups only.

Avalon Santa photo prices:

  • $40: Santa Photo Experience with 5 digital photos
  • $45: Photo Experience plus prints (one 6x8, one 5x7 and eight wallets)
  • $45: Photo Experience plus eight printed 4x6 Christmas cards (three designs to choose from)
  • $50: The Value Bundle, Avalon's most popular, with the digital photos, prints, cards and wallets

Cancel at least 48 hours ahead to avoid losing your payment; no refunds are given for later cancellations or no-shows. Make a night of it with a skate at Avalon on Ice and dinner on the Boulevard. Monday-night Story Time with Santa ($15) is already sold out, and story time doesn't include formal portraits.

North Point Mall: The North Point Pole

North Point Mall, 1000 North Point Circle, opened its new North Point Pole Santa set on Nov. 21, and photos run through Dec. 24. Sessions are scheduled online through the mall's photo partner, Souvenir Photograph, and the mall warns that Santa's calendar fills up fast.

There are three ways to visit, all with the same package pricing ($64.99 for Package A+, $54.99 for A, $49.99 for B and $39.99 for C):

  • Traditional Santa Photos: the classic visit with a real-bearded Santa, for first-timers and yearly regulars alike.
  • Santa Paws: bring your dog, cat or other four-legged family member for a pet photo with Santa.
  • Silent Santa: a quiet, sensory-friendly session held before the mall opens, with a calmer atmosphere and a gentle pace.

Pick your session type first on the booking page to see open times. If you're also shopping, our Black Friday hours guide has the mall's holiday weekend schedule.

Free Santa photos downtown on Dec. 5

The best deal in town is the Alpharetta Convention and Visitors Bureau's Free Pictures with Santa on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 10 a.m. to noon at the Alpharetta Welcome Center, 178 S. Main St., Suite 200, in downtown Alpharetta. Individuals and families each get a free photo, printed on site and emailed right away.

Two rules to know: don't wear green, because the photographer uses a green screen, and leave pets at home. Afterward, grab lunch at one of the spots in our downtown Alpharetta restaurant guide.

Halcyon Holiday Magic: Santa plus characters

Just up GA-400, Halcyon welcomes Santa on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 11 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Santa, the Grinch, Mickey and Minnie and other characters are all available for visits and photos with no appointment needed. There's also a Northside Hospital teddy bear check-up station, live local music, face painting, balloon artists and crafts.

It's ticketed to keep lines and traffic down: $20 per child (children of any age need a ticket), accompanying adults are free, and all ticket proceeds go to Family Promise. It's rain or shine, with characters moving indoors if the weather turns. Want to bring your camera to another character brunch? HOBNOB's Brunch with the Grinch is Saturday, Dec. 12, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at HOBNOB Neighborhood Tavern. More ideas are in our Halcyon guide.

Breakfast and brunch with Santa

  • Brunch with Santa at UP on the Roof, Sat., Nov. 28: brunch runs 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., with Santa visiting from 11:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. at UP on the Roof downtown. Reserve through the restaurant. See our best brunch in Alpharetta list for more.
  • Breakfast with Santa at Dave & Buster's, Sat., Dec. 5, 9 to 11 a.m.: at Dave & Buster's, 6500 North Point Pkwy. Tickets include a breakfast banquet, soft drinks and a photo with Santa before the doors open to the public; an upgraded ticket adds game play. Everyone attending needs a ticket, and pricing varies by location.
  • Breakfast with Santa at Avalon, Dec. 5 and 6: at Chick-fil-A on the 5th and Oak Steakhouse on the 6th. Both are sold out this year.
  • Tuck-Ins with Santa: by appointment with a room booking at The Hotel at Avalon.

Tips for a smooth Santa visit

  • Book early for weekends. Avalon and North Point both say weekends fill first. Weekday afternoons are the calmest.
  • Plan around Santa's breaks. At Avalon, Santa steps away to feed the reindeer at set times, so don't arrive right at 3 p.m. on a weekday.
  • Christmas Eve is walk-up only at Avalon and ends at 5 p.m. North Point's last day is also Dec. 24.
  • Bringing the dog? Choose Avalon's Tuesday-night Pet Portraits or North Point's Santa Paws, not the free downtown event.
  • Little ones nervous? North Point's Silent Santa sessions are built for a quieter visit.
