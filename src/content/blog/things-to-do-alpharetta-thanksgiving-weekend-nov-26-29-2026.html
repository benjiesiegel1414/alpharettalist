Thanksgiving weekend in Alpharetta runs Thursday, Nov. 26 through Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026, and with Fulton County Schools out all week, it's one of the easiest weekends of the year to get out as a family. Avalon on Ice has long holiday hours, Santa is taking visits at Avalon, the Alpharetta Symphony plays its hometown holiday concert on Saturday, and Black Friday and Small Business Saturday bring the crowds to Avalon, North Point Mall, Halcyon and Canton Street. Here's the day-by-day plan with times, prices and addresses.

Quick answer: Thanksgiving weekend at a glance

  • Thu., Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving): Avalon on Ice open 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Santa at Avalon, North Point Mall and Halcyon shops are closed.
  • Fri., Nov. 27 (Black Friday): Avalon on Ice 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.; Santa at Avalon 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.; North Point Mall 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.; Halcyon 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.; Once Upon A Merry Maze at North Point noon to 5 p.m.
  • Sat., Nov. 28 (Small Business Saturday): Brunch with Santa at UP on the Roof, 11 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.; An Alpharetta Holiday with the Alpharetta Symphony at Innovation Academy, 3 to 5:30 p.m.; skating 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Sun., Nov. 29: Avalon on Ice noon to 7 p.m.; Santa at Avalon 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.; Halcyon shops 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • All weekend: Elf'd UP Christmas rooftop at UP on the Roof (3 to 11 p.m. Thursday through Sunday) and the free Friendship Ring of Kids holiday display downtown.

Still planning the big meal? See our Thanksgiving dinner guide and what's open on Thanksgiving for grocery hours and trash pickup changes.

Thursday: Thanksgiving Day

Most of Alpharetta takes the day off, but there's one big exception. Avalon on Ice, the Rockefeller-inspired outdoor rink in the Plaza at Avalon, lists holiday hours of 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, which includes Thanksgiving. Tickets are $18 for general admission and $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. A post-dinner skate is a fun way to walk off the pie. Reservations guarantee a time slot; walk-ups are welcome but not guaranteed.

Santa takes Thanksgiving off: Avalon lists Santa's cottage as closed for the holiday. North Point Mall and the shops at Halcyon are closed too. If you're out downtown in the evening, UP on the Roof at 33 S. Main St. lists its Elf'd UP holiday pop-up from 3 to 11 p.m. on Thanksgiving. Want a morning run first? The Gobble Wobble 5K at Halcyon is covered in our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide.

Friday: Black Friday shopping, skating and Santa

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27. The short version on hours:

  • North Point Mall, 1000 North Point Circle: the mall's hours page lists regular Friday hours of 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. and notes that department store, restaurant and theater hours may vary, so anchor stores may open earlier.
  • Halcyon: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Black Friday.
  • Avalon, 400 Avalon Blvd.: regular hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday, and store hours may vary.

Full details, deals and parking tips are in our Black Friday 2026 hours guide.

Santa at Avalon reopens Friday in his North Pole-inspired cottage in Palmer Plaza. Avalon lists Friday and Saturday hours of 10 a.m. to 8 p.m., with reindeer-feeding breaks from 1 to 2 p.m. and 4 to 5 p.m. Photo packages start at $40 for five digital photos, and the $50 Value Bundle adds prints and Christmas cards. Appointments are the way to go on a holiday weekend; walk-ins get added to a list and texted when it's their turn, but Avalon says weekends are busier.

Once Upon A Merry Maze is a good pick for little kids who need a break from shopping. The storybook-style maze at North Point Circle opened its Christmas version on Nov. 13, with twinkling lights and themed rooms, and Awesome Alpharetta lists it from noon to 5 p.m. on Friday. Book tickets on its website.

For grown-ups, Friday night has options: The Edwin Hamilton Trio plays The Velvet Note at 7:30 p.m., comedian Arnez J. headlines a special event at Helium Comedy Club at 7 p.m., and the MANDY band plays Roaring Social from 8 to 11 p.m.

Saturday: Small Business Saturday, Santa brunch and the Symphony

Saturday, Nov. 28 is Small Business Saturday, and downtown Alpharetta is the place for it. Awesome Alpharetta points shoppers to Canton Street boutiques like Alpharetta Outfitters, DressUp and Sis & Moon's. Pair it with lunch from our best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta list.

Brunch with Santa at UP on the Roof (11 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.). The rooftop's annual Santa brunch serves brunch favorites, specials for kids and holiday drinks for all ages, plus $25 bottomless mimosas for the adults. Reservations are made through UP on the Roof. More morning ideas are in our best brunch in Alpharetta guide.

An Alpharetta Holiday (3 to 5:30 p.m.). The Alpharetta Symphony, the city's hometown orchestra, plays its holiday concert at Innovation Academy, 125 Milton Ave. Expect themes from holiday movies, carols you're invited to sing along with and a classical piece or two. Check the Symphony's site for tickets.

Also on Saturday:

  • Avalon on Ice: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Once Upon A Merry Maze: 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Elf'd UP: 3 to 11 p.m. at UP on the Roof. Wear your tackiest sweater.
  • Live music: Toni Byrd at The Velvet Note at 7:30 and 9:30 p.m. ($42), and Davis & The Love at Roaring Social from 8 to 11 p.m.

Sunday: an easy last day

Sunday, Nov. 29 is the slow day before everyone heads back to work and school. Avalon on Ice runs noon to 7 p.m., Santa at Avalon is in from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. (breaks from noon to 1 p.m. and 3 to 4 p.m.), and Halcyon shops are open 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. The Merry Maze is listed from noon to 4 p.m.

If you'd rather be outside, walk off the leftovers on the Big Creek Greenway, or swing by the Friendship Ring of Kids sculpture outside the Alpharetta Community Center at 175 Roswell St. It's dressed up with lights, holiday decor and winter hats through the season, and it's free.

Art lovers can catch the "Wonder" winter exhibition at the Alpharetta Arts Center, 238 Canton St., which is listed from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on Friday and 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Saturday, so plan that stop before Sunday.

Coming up next: Lights on the Loop and December

The weekend after Thanksgiving is when Alpharetta's December lineup really gets going. Lights on the Loop switches on Friday, Dec. 4 at 5:30 p.m. along a one-mile stretch of the Alpha Loop between Northwinds Parkway and Old Milton Parkway. It's free and open nightly from 5:30 to 11 p.m. through Jan. 4, 2027, with free parking at 2500 and 2520 Northwinds Parkway. Dec. 4 and 5 bring a Holiday Music Showcase with the Alpharetta Symphony and Alpharetta City Band.

Tips for Thanksgiving weekend

  • Book skating and Santa early. Holiday weekends are the busiest slots at Avalon, and appointments guarantee your time.
  • Check store hours the morning of. Anchor stores and restaurants often set their own Black Friday hours.
  • Trash pickup shifts. Thursday routes are collected Friday, Nov. 27, and Friday routes on Saturday, Nov. 28.
