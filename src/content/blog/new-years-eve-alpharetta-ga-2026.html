New Year's Eve 2026 falls on a Thursday, which means a long holiday weekend for most people and a lot of ways to ring in 2027 without driving into Atlanta. Alpharetta has a balloon drop for kids at noon, a rooftop party downtown, a speakeasy soirée, a jazz club countdown and two comedy shows. Here are the dates, times and ticket prices we confirmed, plus free things to do on Dec. 31 and New Year's Day.

Quick answer: New Year's Eve in Alpharetta (2026)

  • Best for little kids: Noon Year's Eve at The Hotel at Avalon, Thurs., Dec. 31, starting at 10 a.m., with a balloon drop at noon. $20 per person (ages 2 and under free). Tickets go on sale Thurs., Dec. 3.
  • Rooftop party: Champagne Dreams at UP on the Roof, 6 p.m. to 2 a.m., 21+. Tickets from $75 (early bird through Dec. 20).
  • Dress-up soirée: The Roaring Countdown at Roaring Social, 21+. General admission is $150 per person plus tax and gratuity, with a bar package, bites, bowling and a champagne toast.
  • Live jazz: The Velvet Note, early show 7 to 9 p.m. or late show 9:30 p.m. to 12:30 a.m. with a midnight toast. $99 to $119.
  • Comedy: Helium Comedy Club's New Year's Eve with a Secret Headliner, 7:30 and 10 p.m.
  • Free: Lights on the Loop on the Alpha Loop, nightly 5:30 to 11 p.m. through Jan. 4, 2027.
  • Skating: Avalon on Ice is open 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. on both New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.

Noon Year's Eve at Avalon (best for families)

If your kids won't make it to midnight, this is the one. Avalon's Noon Year's Eve, presented by Awesome Alpharetta, takes over the ballroom at The Hotel at Avalon (9000 Avalon Blvd.) on Thursday, Dec. 31. Avalon lists it from 10 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., while Awesome Alpharetta lists 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., so plan on wrapping up around lunchtime.

Expect a DJ, face painting, New Year's crafts, bounce houses, a photo booth, complimentary snacks and a kids-only "bubbly bar," all leading up to a countdown and giant balloon drop at noon. Mimosas and a Chick-fil-A pop-up are available for purchase.

  • Tickets: $20 per person. Every adult and every child over 2 needs a ticket. Avalon says sales open Thursday, Dec. 3, so set a reminder.
  • Parking: Use Avalon's free parking decks or surface lots. There's no validation for this event.
  • Tip: Leave the stroller at home if you can. Stroller parking is limited.

Make a day of it with lunch nearby (our best brunch in Alpharetta list has options) and a skate afterward at Avalon on Ice.

Champagne Dreams at UP on the Roof

UP on the Roof (33 S. Main St.) turns its rooftop above downtown Alpharetta into a ticketed New Year's Eve party called Champagne Dreams, from 6 p.m. to 2 a.m. Tickets include heavy hors d'oeuvres and food stations from 6 to 8 p.m. (the restaurant mentions chilled seafood, a wagyu carving station, pasta and sweets), a live DJ starting at 9 p.m. and a sparkling toast at midnight. Drinks and bottle service are extra, and there are zero-proof cocktails too.

Prices climb as the date gets closer:

  • Early bird: $75 through Dec. 20
  • Advance: $85 from Dec. 21 to 30
  • Last chance: $99 on Dec. 31 until 3 p.m.
  • At the door: $125, if any are left

It's 21+ with a valid photo ID. Note that UP on the Roof has no regular dinner service on Dec. 31, so a dinner reservation won't get you in. It reopens New Year's Day from 3 to 10 p.m. for football, food and drinks.

The Roaring Countdown at Roaring Social

For a dressier night, Roaring Social, the speakeasy-style lounge and boutique bowling spot in downtown Alpharetta, is hosting The Roaring Countdown: A New Year's Eve Soirée. Live music comes from Justin, which the venue calls one of North Atlanta's top high-energy dance bands.

General admission is $150 per guest (before tax and gratuity) and includes a bar package, food, bowling, party favors and a midnight champagne toast. GA guests share common spaces with limited seating. VIP packages are available by inquiry, and the top-tier package already has a waitlist. It's a 21+ event and the dress code is "dress to impress."

New Year's Eve at The Velvet Note

Prefer a seated, grown-up evening? The Velvet Note (4075 Old Milton Pkwy.), Alpharetta's intimate jazz listening room, has Atlanta vocalist Lavahi on Dec. 31. She blends soul and jazz, and Velvet Note regulars may know her from hosting the club's Open Mic Jam. Choose the candlelit early show from 7 to 9 p.m. or the late show from 9:30 p.m. to 12:30 a.m. with a midnight champagne toast. Tickets run $99 to $119. Reserve at the club's website or call (855) 583-5838. Last year's New Year's Eve shows sold out, so don't wait long.

Comedy at Helium

Helium Comedy Club (3070 Windward Plaza, near Windward Parkway) is doing two New Year's Eve shows, at 7:30 p.m. and 10 p.m., with a "secret headliner." The club says it hand-picks a touring comic and doesn't reveal who it is until they walk on stage. A couple's package includes two premium seats, a $90 food and drink credit, gratuity and ticket protection. There's a two-item minimum per person in the showroom. See the Windward area guide for dinner nearby.

Free and low-cost things to do on Dec. 31

  • Lights on the Loop: The free walk-through light display along a mile of the Alpha Loop between Northwinds Parkway and Old Milton Parkway is on nightly from 5:30 to 11 p.m. through Jan. 4, 2027. Free parking is at 2500 and 2520 Northwinds Pkwy. Leashed pets are welcome, and the event is alcohol-free. More in our Christmas lights near Alpharetta guide.
  • Avalon on Ice: The rink is open 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day. Skating is $18, or $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. Book a time slot ahead, since walk-ups aren't guaranteed. Details in our Avalon on Ice guide.
  • A low-key dinner out: Plenty of local spots are a good fit for an early dinner before the countdown at home. Browse our best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta or the full restaurant directory, and call ahead, because holiday hours vary.

Worth the drive: an early countdown with fireworks

If you want fireworks but your kids need an early bedtime, Stone Mountain Park hosts a Kids' Early New Year's Eve Celebration on Dec. 31. The park is open noon to 9 p.m., with a Christmas Drone and Light Show (including fire drones) at 8 p.m. and a countdown with music, a "magical snowfall" and fireworks at 9 p.m. It's included with a Stone Mountain Christmas attractions ticket or annual membership. Heads up: attractions close right after the 9 p.m. countdown, and there's no midnight countdown.

New Year's Day in Alpharetta

Friday, Jan. 1, 2027 is a good day to take it easy. A few options we confirmed:

  • Skate: Avalon on Ice, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Football and food: UP on the Roof is open 3 to 10 p.m.
  • Halcyon: Cherry Street Brewing's holiday "Brewville" celebration at Halcyon runs through New Year's Day. See our Halcyon guide for more.
  • Lights: Lights on the Loop stays up through Jan. 4.

School's still out, too. Fulton County Schools' winter break runs through Jan. 1, Jan. 4 is a teacher workday, and students return Tuesday, Jan. 5. If you need coverage, see our winter break camps roundup.

Tips for New Year's Eve in Alpharetta

  • Buy early. The Velvet Note's New Year's Eve shows sold out last year, Roaring Social says availability is limited, and UP on the Roof's prices go up after Dec. 20.
  • Plan your ride home. Downtown parties run until 2 a.m. Book a rideshare or pick a designated driver before you head out.
  • Bring ID. The evening parties at UP on the Roof and Roaring Social are 21+ and check IDs at the door.
  • Dress warm. Rooftops, the rink and the Alpha Loop are outdoors, and late December nights get cold.
