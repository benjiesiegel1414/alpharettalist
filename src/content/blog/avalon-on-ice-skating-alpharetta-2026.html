Avalon on Ice, Alpharetta's Rockefeller-inspired outdoor rink, opens for the season on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026 and runs through Monday, Jan. 18, 2027. Tickets are $18 for adults and $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. Here's everything you need to know about hours, tickets and the best times to go, plus why Avalon is now the main place to skate in Alpharetta.

Avalon on Ice 2026 at a glance

  • Dates: Nov. 22, 2026 through Jan. 18, 2027
  • Where: The Plaza at Avalon, 400 Avalon Blvd., Alpharetta, GA 30009
  • Price: $18 general admission, $14 for children 12 and under. Skate rental is included.
  • Tickets: on sale now (sales opened Nov. 3). Reservations are recommended, and walk-ups are welcome if there's room.
  • Questions: Avalon Concierge at 770-765-1000

Hours and holiday hours

Regular rink hours for the 2026-27 season:

  • Monday to Friday: 3 to 9 p.m.
  • Saturday: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Sunday: noon to 7 p.m.

Avalon stretches the hours to 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. on these days:

  • Opening day, Sunday, Nov. 22: 3 to 10 p.m.
  • Thanksgiving week, Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Winter break, Sunday, Dec. 20 to Sunday, Jan. 3: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., including New Year's Eve and New Year's Day
  • All Fulton County public school holidays: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.

Those long school-break days are a lifesaver if you have kids home and need a plan. If you're mapping out the whole week, our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide has more ideas.

Tickets, waivers and the fine print

Avalon asks guests to book a skating time slot in advance. Walk-ups are allowed, but there's no guarantee you'll get on the ice at the time you show up, especially on weekend evenings and holiday afternoons.

  • Waiver: every skater needs to complete the online waiver after booking. Walk-ups have to fill it out too, so doing it on your phone in line saves time.
  • Refunds: tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable, but you can reschedule to another date if space is available. Handy if a cold rain rolls in.
  • Groups: group pricing is available by emailing avalon@icerinkevents.com (there are no group rates Friday through Sunday).

Opening weekend: the Lighting of Avalon

The rink opens the same day as the Lighting of Avalon on Sunday, Nov. 22, Avalon's free holiday kickoff. The festivities run from 2 to 7:30 p.m. with a skating show, face painting, crafts and a photo booth, and the 40-foot tree lights up at 7 p.m. after a countdown led by Santa. Expect big crowds and busy parking that day, so carpool or ride-share if you can.

Santa sticks around, too. Starting Nov. 23, he's at his cozy cottage along the Boulevard for photos by appointment through Christmas Eve, so you can pair a skate with a Santa visit. See every date on our Alpharetta events calendar.

What happened to The Cooler?

If you've lived here a while, you probably remember skating at The Cooler, the indoor rink that opened in 1997 and was home base for local hockey players and figure skaters for decades. Its ice closed on March 31, 2025, and the building was converted into the Atlanta National Pickleball Club.

The owner told FOX 5 the decision came down to high operating costs and pickleball's growing popularity, saying the change would let the facility "serve more people." The move drew pushback from the skating community, and an online petition opposing it gathered thousands of signatures. Whatever your take, the practical result is that Avalon's seasonal rink is now the go-to spot for a casual skate in Alpharetta.

Tips for a great skate

  • Go on a weeknight for elbow room. Weekday sessions start at 3 p.m., and a school-night skate is usually calmer than a Saturday evening.
  • Book ahead for holidays. Thanksgiving week and winter break are the rink's longest days, and also its most popular.
  • Dress in layers. It's an outdoor rink, so gloves and a warm hat make a big difference for little hands and first-timers.
  • Make a night of it. Grab dinner at South City Kitchen or Oak Steakhouse, or keep it sweet with a scoop at Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams. Browse more in our Alpharetta restaurant guide.
  • Staying over? The Hotel at Avalon is a short walk from the rink.
