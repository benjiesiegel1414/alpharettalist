Hanukkah 2026 begins at sundown on Friday, Dec. 4 and runs eight nights, with the final candles lit on Friday, Dec. 11. That puts the whole Festival of Lights right in the middle of Alpharetta's busiest holiday stretch. Here's when each night falls, where to catch a free public menorah lighting in Alpharetta and nearby, and how to turn a lighting into a full evening out.

Quick answer: Hanukkah 2026 in Alpharetta

  • Dates: first candle Friday, Dec. 4, 2026 (before Shabbat); eighth and final night Friday, Dec. 11.
  • Menorah Lighting at Avalon: Sunday, Dec. 6, 5 to 6 p.m. on the Boulevard in front of The Cape, with Chabad of North Fulton. Free.
  • Menorah Lighting on the Town Green, downtown Alpharetta: listed for Thursday, Dec. 10, 5 to 6 p.m. Free. (One listing shows Dec. 7, so double-check before you go.)
  • Marcus JCC menorah lighting, Dunwoody: Tuesday, Dec. 8, 5 to 6 p.m. at Zaban Park. Free, no registration.
  • Marcus JCC "Light Up the Alcove": Sunday, Dec. 6, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. Free, registration required.

Hanukkah 2026 dates, night by night

Hanukkah begins on the eve of the 25th of Kislev on the Hebrew calendar, so the dates move every year. In 2026 the calendar from Chabad.org lays out like this:

  • Night 1: Friday, Dec. 4. Light one candle before the Shabbat candles, which are lit 18 minutes before sunset.
  • Night 2: Saturday, Dec. 5. The menorah is lit after Shabbat ends and Havdalah is recited.
  • Night 3: Sunday, Dec. 6 (the Avalon lighting).
  • Night 4: Monday, Dec. 7.
  • Night 5: Tuesday, Dec. 8 (the Marcus JCC lighting).
  • Night 6: Wednesday, Dec. 9.
  • Night 7: Thursday, Dec. 10 (the downtown Town Green lighting).
  • Night 8: Friday, Dec. 11. Light all eight candles before the Shabbat candles.

Because the holiday starts and ends on a Friday, two of the eight nights have earlier lighting times than usual. Sunset in early December comes around 5:30 p.m. in Alpharetta, so plan Friday family gatherings for the late afternoon.

Menorah Lighting at Avalon: Sunday, Dec. 6

The biggest public Hanukkah event in Alpharetta happens on the third night at Avalon. Guests gather on the Boulevard, in front of The Cape, from 5 to 6 p.m. for a traditional menorah lighting ceremony. Chabad of North Fulton helps light the menorah and brings special treats for guests. Admission is free.

A few tips for the evening:

  • It's a Sunday in peak holiday season, so arrive a little early to find parking.
  • The lighting wraps up at 6 p.m., which leaves plenty of time for a skate at Avalon on Ice or dinner on the Boulevard. The Hotel at Avalon sits right on the plaza if you're hosting out-of-town family.
  • Avalon notes that photos and video may be taken at the event for publicity.

Menorah Lighting on the Town Green: Thursday, Dec. 10

Downtown gets its own community menorah lighting on the Town Green at Alpharetta City Center. Awesome Alpharetta's holiday guide lists it for Thursday, Dec. 10 from 5 to 6 p.m., the seventh night of Hanukkah, with free admission. A separate event listing on the same site shows Monday, Dec. 7, so confirm the date on the Awesome Alpharetta calendar the week of the event.

The Town Green is steps from Milton Avenue, Canton Street and the City Center restaurants, so it's easy to make a night of it. Our guide to the best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta has plenty of options within a short walk.

More Hanukkah events near Alpharetta

  • Marcus JCC Hanukkah Menorah Lighting and Celebration: Tuesday, Dec. 8, 5 to 6 p.m. at MJCCA Zaban Park, 5342 Tilly Mill Road in Dunwoody. Free and open to the community, no registration required.
  • Light Up the Alcove: A Hanukkah Drop-In Celebration: Sunday, Dec. 6, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the Marcus JCC. Free, but registration is required. Pair it with the Avalon lighting that evening for a full Hanukkah Sunday.
  • Chabad of North Fulton: the congregation behind Avalon's lighting is based at 10180 Jones Bridge Road in Johns Creek, with satellite offices in Roswell and Alpharetta. In past years its grand menorah has traveled to a different North Fulton spot on several nights of Hanukkah, including a 2024 lighting at Milton's Broadwell Pavilion in Crabapple. Call 770-410-9000 for this year's schedule.

Make a night of it

Hanukkah overlaps with almost every big holiday event in town this year, which makes it easy to plan eight nights of outings:

  • See the lights: the eight nights line up with Lights on the Loop and the neighborhood displays in our guide to Christmas lights near Alpharetta. Kids who love the menorah usually love a light walk, too.
  • Skate: Avalon on Ice runs all through Hanukkah, and the rink is a short walk from the Dec. 6 menorah lighting.
  • Shop for gifts: the Makers Market at Avalon on Saturday, Dec. 12, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., brings local artists and makers to Avalon the day after the last candle. Earlier, the Artists Holiday Market at the Alpharetta Arts Center runs Saturday, Nov. 21, 10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Both are free and on our events calendar.
  • Weekend brunch: the two Sundays bracketing Hanukkah are perfect for a family meal. See our best brunch in Alpharetta list.

Quick planning tips

  • Outdoor lightings happen right at sunset, and December evenings get cold fast. Bring layers and gloves for little ones.
  • Public lightings are free and open to everyone, whether you celebrate Hanukkah or just want to learn about it.
  • Event details can shift. Check Avalon's event page, the Awesome Alpharetta calendar or the Marcus JCC site the week of the event.
  • Hosting at home? Friday, Dec. 4 and Friday, Dec. 11 both require lighting before Shabbat, so plan dinner around an early lighting.
