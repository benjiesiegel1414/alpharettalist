Parking at Halcyon is free in the six-level deck, with premium paid spots for people who want to park right next to the action and 15-minute curbside spaces for to-go pickups. Here is how parking works, Halcyon's regular and 2026 holiday hours, and how to get there.

Where to park

  • Parking deck (free): six levels, with accessible spaces on every level. This is the best choice for dinner, shopping or an evening on the green.
  • Premium paid spots: in the event lot and along Halcyon Way, for immediate access to the property.
  • Curbside (15 minutes): designated spots for picking up to-go orders.
  • Accessible parking: along Halcyon Way and on every level of the deck.
  • Rideshare: the Uber and Lyft pickup and drop-off area is in the surface lot.

Parking questions go to Halcyon's parking operator at hmeters@lazparking.com

Directions

Address: 6365 Halcyon Way, Alpharetta, GA 30005. Phone: (470) 223-3665.

  • From Alpharetta and Atlanta: take GA 400 north to exit 12, McFarland Parkway. Halcyon is just off the exit, along Halcyon Way near Ronald Reagan Parkway.
  • From Cumming: take GA 400 south to exit 12.
  • From Avalon: about 15 minutes north via GA 400.
  • By bike or on foot: the Big Creek Greenway trail extension connects near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway.

Amenities and rules

  • Restrooms: public and family restrooms are inside the Market Hall, across from the Village Green. There is also an ATM.
  • Wi-Fi: free in common areas on the Halcyon-Public network.
  • Strollers: Halcyon does not lend strollers or wheelchairs, so bring your own.
  • Smoking: Halcyon is smoke-free, including vaping, except a designated area on the fourth level of the parking deck.
  • Pets: leashed dogs are welcome. See Halcyon with your dog.
  • Skateboards: not allowed.
  • After hours: Halcyon asks visitors to leave the property and not park or loiter after closing.
