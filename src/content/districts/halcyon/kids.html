Halcyon is an easy, low-key outing with kids. There is a grassy Village Green with a sandbox and yard games, two kids eat free nights, plenty of quick food and dessert, and a wooded trail right next door. Here is how to plan a family visit.

Before you go

  • Parking: free in the six-level deck. See our Halcyon parking guide.
  • Restrooms: public and family restrooms are inside the Market Hall, across from the Village Green.
  • Strollers: Halcyon does not lend strollers or wheelchairs, so bring your own.
  • Hours: 10am to 9pm Monday through Saturday and 11am to 6pm Sunday.
  • Rules: Halcyon asks that children not be left unattended, and unaccompanied minors are asked to leave during regular school hours, outside lunch.

Where kids can play

The Village Green is the main attraction, with a sandbox, yard games and open grass. It is busiest on weekend evenings and during events, and quieter on weekday afternoons. For a longer outing, the Big Creek Greenway trail extension near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway is a 12-foot-wide paved and boardwalk path, good for strollers, scooters and little bikes.

Kids eat free

  • Cherry Street Brewpub: Tuesdays, 5pm to 9pm, one free kids meal with an adult entrée over $15, dine-in only. Sunday is its Family Fun Day.
  • HOBNOB: kids eat free Sunday through Thursday.

Easy family meals

Char Pizzeria, Never Enough Thyme, Chick-fil-A, Five Guys and The Original Hot Dog Factory are all easy with kids. In the Market Hall, Gu's Dumplings is a hit with dumpling fans (closed Mondays). For a fun group dinner with older kids, Iron Age lets everyone cook at the table. All 22 spots are in our Halcyon restaurant guide.

Treats

  • Kilwins for ice cream, fudge and caramel apples.
  • Playa Bowls for acai and smoothie bowls.
  • Sweet Tuna for poke if the kids are adventurous.

Activities and classes

  • Tutu School: ballet classes for young children, (678) 658-1219.
  • X-Golf: indoor golf bays, a good rainy-day plan with older kids.
  • Piggy Jo's: a children's boutique for clothes and gifts.
  • Family races: kids fun runs with the Great Chicken Chase on November 21 and the Gobble Wobble on Thanksgiving morning.

Holidays with kids

Santa officially arrives at Halcyon Holiday Magic on Saturday, December 5, 2026. Chanukah at Halcyon on December 6 has children's entertainment and a giant menorah. HOBNOB's Brunch with the Grinch is on December 12 and 19, and Chick-fil-A hosts a ticketed Dinner with Santa on December 12. See Halcyon holidays 2026.
