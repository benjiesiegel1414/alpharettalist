Halcyon is the 135-acre walkable district at GA 400 exit 12, just north of Alpharetta, and it has become a favorite for dinner, a Trader Joe's run, a round of indoor golf or a walk on the Big Creek Greenway. It feels more relaxed than Avalon, with a grassy Village Green at its center and a calendar full of leagues, live music and family events. This guide covers everything you need: hours, free parking, every restaurant, happy hours, brunch, holiday events and tips for kids and dogs.

Halcyon at a glance

  • Address: 6365 Halcyon Way, Alpharetta, GA 30005. It has an Alpharetta mailing address but sits just over the line in southern Forsyth County, off GA 400 at exit 12 (McFarland Parkway).
  • Hours: Monday through Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday 11am to 6pm. Restaurants and X-Golf keep their own hours.
  • Parking: free in the six-level deck, with premium paid spots in the event lot and along Halcyon Way. Full parking guide.
  • Phone: (470) 223-3665 for the property office.
  • Amenities: public and family restrooms and an ATM in the Market Hall, free Wi-Fi in common areas (network: Halcyon-Public) and a rideshare pickup area in the surface lot.
  • What's here: 22 restaurants and food stops, Trader Joe's and local boutiques, X-Golf, an Embassy Suites hotel, loft offices and homes.

Where to eat

Dining is Halcyon's biggest draw. Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar and Ocean & Acre are the top picks for a nice dinner, Iron Age brings all-you-can-eat Korean barbecue, and Eclipse di Luna pairs Spanish tapas with live music most nights. HOBNOB and Cherry Street Brewpub are the casual hangouts, and the Market Hall holds quick favorites like Gu's Dumplings and Il Bottegone. See all 22 in our Halcyon restaurant guide, plus happy hours and weekly deals and brunch.

Shops and services

Trader Joe's anchors the shopping. Around the green are boutiques including Bluemercury, Lizard Thicket, Willow Jane, Nina + Leigh and Piggy Jo's, plus Buff City Soap, RW Design & Exchange, BBQGuys and the wine market Rise & Wine. Everyday services include Chewy Vet Care, Dogtopia, Optima Eye Care, Tutu School and The Forum Athletic Club. See the full list with phone numbers.

Things to do

X-Golf has indoor golf simulator bays and a bar. The Village Green hosts cornhole and bocce leagues, a sandbox and yard games, and community events through the year. A 1.5-mile trail extension near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway connects to Forsyth County's Big Creek Greenway. Restaurants add trivia on Mondays, music bingo on Thursdays and live music on weekends. More in things to do at Halcyon.

One change to know: the CMX CinéBistro theater has closed, and Halcyon says a new concept is planned for the space. For a movie, head to Regal Avalon.

Families and dogs

Kids love the Village Green, and parents love that Cherry Street and HOBNOB run kids eat free nights. Family restrooms are in the Market Hall, but bring your own stroller. See Halcyon with kids.

Dogs are welcome on a leash of six feet or less, up to two per person, but not inside restaurants or food stores unless the business allows it. See Halcyon with your dog.

Where to stay

Embassy Suites Alpharetta Halcyon is on Market Street inside the district, so you can walk to dinner and the Greenway. For hotels closer to Avalon and Downtown Alpharetta, see our Avalon hotels guide.

Halcyon vs. Avalon

Both are walkable districts with free garage parking, chef-driven restaurants and a central gathering space. Avalon is bigger, with 75-plus stores from Apple to CHANEL, a 12-screen theater, a hotel and conference center and Avalon on Ice. Halcyon is smaller and more neighborhood-scaled, with Trader Joe's, local boutiques, a grassy village green, weekly leagues and direct access to the Big Creek Greenway. For a big night out or holiday skating, start at Avalon. For a laid-back dinner, a playground stop and a walk in the woods, pick Halcyon.

Tips for visiting

  • Head straight to the free deck; curbside spots are limited to 15 minutes.
  • Book weekend dinners at Cattle Shed, Ocean & Acre and Iron Age.
  • Sunday center hours end at 6pm, though many restaurants stay open until 9pm.
  • Halcyon is smoke-free, except a designated area on the fourth level of the parking deck, and glass is not allowed in common areas.
