Deals by day of the week

Monday

  • Cattle Shed: chef's cut steak dinner for $38, all day.
  • HOBNOB: $13 beer and burger.
  • X-Golf: $5 margaritas.
  • Cherry Street: trivia at 7:30pm (21 and up).
  • Eclipse di Luna: flamenco music, 6pm to 9pm.

Tuesday

  • CT Cantina: $2 tacos, all day.
  • Cattle Shed: wine flights.
  • HOBNOB: taco and margarita deal.
  • X-Golf: $6 Tito's cocktails.
  • Cherry Street: Kids Eat Free, 5pm to 9pm, with an adult entrée over $15 (dine-in only).
  • Eclipse di Luna: live band, 6:30pm to 9:30pm.

Wednesday

  • Cattle Shed: half off select bottles of wine.
  • HOBNOB: $8 to $10 featured whiskey.
  • Rise & Wine: a free 187 mL bottle with a $25 purchase.

Thursday

  • HOBNOB: ladies night from 5pm and music bingo at 7pm.
  • Iron Age: 50% off drinks for ladies with a dinner purchase.

Friday and Saturday

  • HOBNOB: live music at 8pm.
  • Eclipse di Luna (Saturday): flamenco guitar 12:30pm to 3:30pm, salsa and merengue 7:30pm to 10:30pm, then a DJ until 1:15am.

Sunday

  • Ocean & Acre: live music, 1pm to 4pm.
  • Eclipse di Luna: flamenco guitar, 12:30pm to 3:30pm and 5pm to 8pm.
  • Cherry Street: Family Fun Day with occasional pop-up activities.

Kids eat free

  • Cherry Street Brewpub: Tuesday, 5pm to 9pm, one free kids meal with an adult entrée over $15, dine-in only.
  • HOBNOB: Sunday through Thursday.

Tips

  • Deals change with the season, so confirm with the restaurant before you go.
  • Happy hour pricing is usually limited to the bar area or certain items; ask your server.
  • Do not drink and drive. Rideshare pickup is in Halcyon's surface lot.
  • For happy hours elsewhere, browse the Alpharetta restaurant directory.
