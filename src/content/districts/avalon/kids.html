Avalon is one of the easiest outings in Alpharetta with kids. Strollers are free to borrow, there is a children's play area and a train ride, almost every restaurant has a kids menu, and dessert is never more than a block away. Here is how to plan a family visit, including the rules parents and teens should know.

Before you go

  • Parking is free in the decks; see our Avalon parking guide. Valet ($12) helps if you are juggling car seats.
  • Borrow a stroller or wheelchair for free at the concierge, (770) 765-1000, subject to availability.
  • Restrooms are listed among Avalon's amenities, and Avalon has a Family Lounge for a break.
  • Center hours are 10am to 9pm Monday through Saturday and noon to 7pm Sunday.

Places to play

  • Children's play area: soft outdoor play equipment for little ones.
  • Avalon Express train: a train ride kids love, especially during the holidays.
  • The Plaza: open space for running around, and the home of Avalon on Ice in winter.
  • Squirrel Scavenger Hunt: free maps and clue sheets at the concierge turn a stroll into a game.
  • Photo booth: one of Avalon's listed amenities, good for a quick family picture.

Kid-friendly places to eat

Most Avalon restaurants welcome kids. These are the easiest picks:

Chick-fil-A is on site too. For a nicer family dinner, Colletta has wood-fired pizza and pasta that please most kids, and South City Kitchen's weekend brunch starts at 8am.

Treats

  • Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams for scoops and waffle cones.
  • Five Daughters Bakery for croissant-style donuts.
  • Caffè Antico for gelato.
  • Le Macaron for colorful French macarons.
  • Boba Craze for bubble tea with older kids.

Activities for older kids

  • Movies at Regal Avalon.
  • Group game rooms at Immersive Gamebox, a hit for birthday parties.
  • Browsing Posman Books and the Apple store.
  • Kids' clothes at Janie and Jack and GAP. See the full store list.

Holidays with kids

Winter is peak family season. Avalon on Ice runs November 22, 2026 to January 18, 2027 ($14 for kids 12 and under, skates included), with 10am to 10pm sessions over Thanksgiving week and winter break. Santa is at his cottage from November 23 through Christmas Eve, Story Time with Santa runs through December 22, and Breakfast with Santa is on December 5 and 6. On December 31, Noon Year's Eve at The Hotel at Avalon has a balloon drop at noon, made for kids who will not make it to midnight. Details in Christmas at Avalon.

Rules for teens

Avalon's code of conduct is strict about evenings:

  • Visitors under 17 must be with a parent or legal guardian after 8pm.
  • Visitors under 21 are not permitted after 10pm.
  • No skateboards, hoverboards, running, climbing on furniture or bike riding on sidewalks.

Drop-offs for a movie or dinner work best before 8pm, with a parent meeting them afterward.

More family ideas

For a slower-paced outing, Halcyon has a Village Green with a sandbox and kids eat free nights. Around town, our things to do in Alpharetta guide covers parks, playgrounds and the Big Creek Greenway.
