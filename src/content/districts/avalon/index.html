Avalon is Alpharetta's busiest place to eat, shop and hang out: an 86-acre walkable district on Old Milton Parkway with more than two dozen restaurants, 75-plus stores, a 12-screen movie theater, a hotel and conference center, and a plaza that turns into a skating rink every winter. This guide covers everything you need for a visit, from free parking and hours to where to eat, what to do with kids and dogs, and Avalon's 2026 holiday events.

Avalon at a glance

  • Address: 400 Avalon Blvd, Alpharetta, GA 30009, on Old Milton Parkway just west of GA 400 (exit 10).
  • Hours: Monday through Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday noon to 7pm. Restaurants set their own hours, and many stay open until 10pm or later on weekends.
  • Parking: free self-parking every day in the decks and surface lots on the north and south sides. Valet is $12. Full parking guide.
  • Concierge: (770) 765-1000 for directions, free stroller and wheelchair loans, bag check, gift cards and restaurant reservations. General line: (770) 765-2000.
  • What's here: more than 570,000 square feet of shops, 26 restaurants and dessert shops, the Regal Avalon theater, The Hotel at Avalon with 330 rooms, the 44,000-square-foot Alpharetta Conference Center, offices and 637 homes.

Where to eat

Dining is the main reason most people come. The Oak Steakhouse and Colletta dining rooms are the go-to reservations for a special night, Rumi's Kitchen is a metro Atlanta favorite for Persian kabobs, and The Cape has a sunset rooftop. For something easier, Antico Pizza, Superica and Barleygarden handle families and groups, and South City Kitchen runs one of the area's most popular weekend brunches. Dessert is never far away, from Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams to Five Daughters Bakery.

Shopping

Avalon mixes national flagships with smaller brands. The big names include Apple, CHANEL, Sephora, lululemon, Anthropologie, Free People, Madewell, J. Crew, Tory Burch, Kendra Scott, Pottery Barn, Williams Sonoma, Crate & Barrel, Arhaus and The Container Store, and Whole Foods Market anchors the grocery side. There is also an independent bookstore, Posman Books, and a Rivian showroom. Browse all of them by category in our Avalon store guide.

Things to do

Beyond shopping and dining, Avalon has the 12-screen Regal Avalon theater, private game rooms at Immersive Gamebox, spa time at The Woodhouse Day Spa, workout studios like Club Pilates and [solidcore], and a plaza with seating, a children's play area and the Avalon Express train. The Makers Market local artist fair pops up through the year, and the paved Alpha Loop links Avalon to Downtown Alpharetta in about a mile. More ideas in things to do at Avalon.

Holidays at Avalon

Avalon goes all out from late November through New Year's. The free Lighting of Avalon is Sunday, November 22, 2026, when Santa counts down to the 40-foot tree at 7pm. Avalon on Ice, a Rockefeller-inspired outdoor rink, opens the same day and runs through January 18, 2027, with tickets at $18 for adults and $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. Santa's cottage, Breakfast with Santa, a menorah lighting, the Jingle Jog and a family-friendly Noon Year's Eve round out the season. Dates and details are in our Christmas at Avalon guide and the full Avalon on Ice guide.

Families and dogs

Avalon is easy with kids: strollers are free to borrow at the concierge, there is a children's play area and a family lounge, and plenty of restaurants have kids menus. Parents should know the code of conduct, though: visitors under 17 must be with a parent or guardian after 8pm, and visitors under 21 are not allowed after 10pm. Read our Avalon with kids guide.

Dogs are welcome too. Keep them leashed, clean up after them, and use the fenced dog park behind Building 1000. The concierge even keeps water and treats on hand. See Avalon with your dog for patios and tips.

Where to stay

The Hotel at Avalon puts you right on the Boulevard, with South City Kitchen downstairs and a direct connection to the conference center. EVEN Hotel Alpharetta is next door on Old Milton Parkway, and the Courtyard Alpharetta Avalon Area is a few minutes away. Compare them in our Avalon hotels guide.

Tips for visiting

  • Skip the street meters for anything longer than a quick errand; the decks are free.
  • Book dinner ahead on Friday and Saturday, especially at Oak Steakhouse, Colletta, Rumi's Kitchen and The Cape.
  • Weeknights are the calmest time for Avalon on Ice; weekend evenings sell out.
  • On big event nights like the Lighting of Avalon, the decks fill early, so carpool or use rideshare.
  • Want a quieter alternative? Halcyon, 15 minutes north at GA 400 exit 12, has a similar walkable setup on a smaller, more relaxed scale.
