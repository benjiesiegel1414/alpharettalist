Avalon has more than 570,000 square feet of shops, from Apple and CHANEL to local boutiques and a Whole Foods Market. Below is every store on Avalon's retail directory, sorted by category, plus hours, shopping services and tips for a smoother trip.

Store hours

Most Avalon stores follow the center's general hours: Monday through Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday noon to 7pm. Some keep their own schedules. Whole Foods Market is open daily from 7am to 10pm, Anthropologie closes at 8pm, and Crate & Barrel opens at 9am on Saturdays. Holiday hours usually stretch later in December.

Women's fashion and boutiques

Aerie and OFFLINE by Aerie, Altar'd State, American Threads, Anthropologie, Banana Republic, Boden, EVEREVE, Everything But Water, Free People, GAP, J. Crew, J. Jill, J. McLaughlin, Lena Larose, Lilly Pulitzer, Madewell, Reformation, Tory Burch and Urban Outfitters.

Men's and outdoor

Bonobos, Faherty, High Country Outfitters, Johnnie-O, Onward Reserve, Orvis, Peter Millar, Tecovas, Tommy Bahama and Vineyard Vines. Many of these carry women's lines too.

Activewear

Alo Yoga, Athleta, lululemon and Vuori. Several studios on the property, including Club Pilates and [solidcore], are steps away if you want to try out the gear.

Kids

Janie and Jack for children's clothing, plus the kids sections at GAP and Posman Books. See our Avalon with kids guide for more.

Jewelry, watches and accessories

Brilliant Earth, Cappai Designs, gorjana, Kendra Scott, Mayors, Watches of Switzerland, Sunglass Hut and Tumi.

Beauty and personal care

CHANEL, Jo Malone London and Sephora for shopping, and AYA Skin, Boardroom Styling Lounge, Drybar, Lux Nail Lounge, Rowan, SHINE Medical Aesthetics, Van Michael Salon and The Woodhouse Day Spa for services.

Home and furniture

Arhaus, The Container Store, Crate & Barrel, LOVESAC, Pottery Barn, Tempur-Pedic, West Elm and Williams Sonoma. With this many home stores in one walkable stretch, Avalon is one of the easiest places north of Atlanta to compare furniture in person.

Tech, books, gifts and more

  • Apple on the Boulevard, with Genius Bar appointments.
  • Posman Books, an independent bookstore with gifts.
  • Makers Market, a shop for local makers' goods, plus the outdoor Makers Market artist fair on select dates.
  • Fount for leather goods and tech accessories.
  • Rivian, the electric vehicle maker's showroom.
  • LensCrafters, with Peachtree Eye Associates inside for eye exams.
  • PNC Bank and a drive-up ATM.

Groceries

Whole Foods Market at 2800 Old Milton Parkway anchors the south side of Avalon and is open daily from 7am to 10pm. For other groceries, Trader Joe's at Halcyon is about 15 minutes north.

Shopping services

  • Bag check at the concierge during property hours, so you can drop bags and keep shopping.
  • Free stroller and wheelchair loans from the concierge, subject to availability.
  • Avalon gift cards sold at the concierge from $5 to $500, with a $3 activation fee. They work at Avalon's stores and restaurants.
  • Free phone charging, water and umbrellas at the concierge when available.
  • Concierge: (770) 765-1000.

Shopping tips

  • Weekday mornings are the quietest time to shop. Saturday afternoons and December evenings are the busiest.
  • Park free in the north or south deck closest to the stores you want; the Boulevard is easy to walk, but parking on the right side saves steps with heavy bags.
  • During the holidays, the Makers Market artist fair on December 12 and the Santa cottage make a shopping trip more festive. See Christmas at Avalon.
  • Store lineups change. If a store matters to you, call ahead or check Avalon's directory before you go.
