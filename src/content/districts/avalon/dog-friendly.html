Yes, Avalon is dog friendly. Leashed dogs are welcome in the common areas, there is a fenced dog park on the property, and the concierge keeps water and dog treats on hand. Here are the rules, where to go with your dog and how to plan a meal out together.

The rules

  • Keep dogs leashed in all common areas.
  • Clean up after your pet. Avalon's code of conduct asks every owner to do this.
  • Stores decide for themselves. Some retailers welcome leashed dogs and some do not, so ask at the door. Dogs cannot go in restaurant dining rooms, so plan on a patio.
  • No skateboards or bikes on sidewalks, which also makes the Boulevard calmer for nervous dogs.

Avalon's dog park

Avalon has a fenced dog park behind Building 1000, according to its code of conduct. It is the place to let your dog stretch its legs before or after a walk down the Boulevard. The concierge, (770) 765-1000, can point you to it and offers water and dog treats.

Eating out with your dog

Barleygarden Kitchen & Craft Bar is known for its dog-friendly patio, with more than 60 craft beers, burgers and sandwiches. Many other Avalon restaurants have outdoor seating along the Boulevard, including Crú Food & Wine Bar, whose patio is one of the best for people-watching. Patio policies can change with the weather and the crowd, so ask the host before you sit down.

Quick options work well too: grab a bowl from Gusto! or a scoop from Jeni's and find a bench on The Plaza.

Walks from Avalon

The paved Alpha Loop runs from Avalon to Downtown Alpharetta in about a mile, a good leashed walk with plenty to see. During the holidays, the free Lights on the Loop display along the Alpha Loop has a Pet-Friendly Weekend with Llamas on the Loop on December 18 and 19, 2026 (5:30pm to 8:30pm); pets need to stay at least 6 feet from the llamas. For a longer, wooded walk, the Big Creek Greenway is a short drive away.

When to go with a dog

  • Best: weekday mornings and early afternoons, when the Boulevard is quiet.
  • Busier: Friday and Saturday evenings and December weekends.
  • Skip: the Lighting of Avalon on November 22, when crowds are huge.
  • Bring water on warm days. Pavement heats up fast in a Georgia summer.
