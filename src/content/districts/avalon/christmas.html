The Lighting of Avalon: Sunday, Nov. 22

Avalon's free holiday kickoff runs from 2pm to 7:30pm along the Boulevard, with a skating performance, face painting, holiday crafts and a photo booth. Avalon on Ice opens at 3pm that day. At 7pm sharp, Santa leads the countdown and the 40-foot tree lights up.

It is one of the busiest nights of the year at Avalon. The free decks fill quickly, so Avalon recommends carpooling or rideshare. Valet runs on 3rd Street, and shuttles run from Gwinnett Technical College across the street. More in our Avalon parking guide.

Avalon on Ice

The Rockefeller-inspired outdoor rink on The Plaza runs from November 22, 2026 through January 18, 2027. Tickets are $18 for general admission and $14 for kids 12 and under, and skates are included. Book a time slot ahead; walk-ups are welcome, but a slot is not guaranteed.

  • Regular hours: Monday to Friday 3pm to 9pm, Saturday 10am to 10pm, Sunday noon to 7pm.
  • Extended hours (10am to 10pm): Thanksgiving week (Nov. 23 to 27), winter break (Dec. 20 to Jan. 3, including New Year's Eve and Day) and Fulton County school holidays.
  • Every skater signs an online waiver. Tickets are non-refundable but can be rescheduled if space allows.

Santa at Avalon

From November 23 through December 24, Santa is at his cottage along the Boulevard for photos, with visits booked by ticket. Story Time with Santa runs November 23 through December 22 for younger kids. Book early for weekend slots in December, which go first.

Breakfast with Santa is on Saturday, December 5 at Chick-fil-A and Sunday, December 6 at Oak Steakhouse. Both require tickets.

Staying the night? The Hotel at Avalon offers Santa Tuck-Ins as an add-on to room bookings from November 20 to December 23.

Menorah lighting: Sunday, Dec. 6

Avalon celebrates Chanukah with a menorah lighting on the Boulevard from 5pm to 6pm on Sunday, December 6, with Chabad of North Fulton helping light the menorah and handing out treats. It is free. Halcyon holds its own Chanukah celebration the same day.

Makers Market and the Jingle Jog

The last Makers Market of the year on Saturday, December 12 (10am to 6pm) brings local artists, crafters and designers to the Boulevard, a good place to find one-of-a-kind gifts. On Sunday, December 13, the Jingle Jog is a one-mile fun run down Avalon Boulevard with Santa, followed by a holiday brunch with a Nutcracker performance at The Hotel at Avalon. Tickets are required.

Noon Year's Eve: Thursday, Dec. 31

Families with little ones can ring in 2027 early at Noon Year's Eve at The Hotel at Avalon, with live music, face painting, crafts and a giant balloon drop at noon. Tickets are required. The ice rink stays open 10am to 10pm on New Year's Eve, so you can make a day of it.

Holiday tips

  • Skate on a weeknight for a calmer rink, and book holiday-week sessions early.
  • Pair a skate with an espresso or gelato at Caffè Antico or dessert at Cafe Intermezzo.
