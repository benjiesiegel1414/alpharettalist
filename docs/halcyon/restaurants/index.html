Halcyon Restaurants: Where to Eat at Halcyon

All 22 restaurants and food stops at Halcyon, from steak and tapas to the Market Hall.

Halcyon has 22 places to eat, from a Charleston-inspired steak and wine bar to tabletop Korean barbecue, Spanish tapas with live flamenco and a Market Hall full of quick bites. Here is every one, with hours, phone numbers and our picks.

Quick picks

  • Special occasion: Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar
  • Seafood and a raw bar: Ocean & Acre
  • Groups and birthdays: Iron Age Korean Steak House
  • Live music: Eclipse di Luna
  • Watch the game: HOBNOB or Cherry Street Brewpub
  • Quick lunch: Gu's Dumplings in the Market Hall
  • Dessert: Kilwins

Dinner picks

Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar, from the owners of HOBNOB, is the special-occasion pick: premium steaks, seafood, charcuterie and cheese boards and more than 200 wines, with a menu inspired by Charleston. Happy hour runs daily from 2:30pm to 6pm, and Monday's $38 chef's cut steak dinner is one of the best deals at Halcyon.

Ocean & Acre sits in a bright, barn-like building and pairs smoked meats and seasonal produce with shareable seafood and a raw bar. It is open daily from 11am, with live music on Sunday afternoons.

Iron Age Korean Steak House is all-you-can-eat Korean barbecue at tabletop grills, with staff guiding you through bulgogi and other meats. It opens at 11am and serves until 11pm, and on Thursdays it has offered half-off drinks for ladies with dinner.

Eclipse di Luna serves paella, empanadas, patatas bravas and sangria with live music most nights, from flamenco guitar to Saturday salsa and merengue. It stays open until midnight on Friday and Saturday.

Casual and family-friendly

HOBNOB Neighborhood Tavern has a deep bourbon list, burgers from a short rib, brisket and chuck blend, live music on weekends and kids eat free Sunday through Thursday. Cherry Street Brewpub, from Forsyth County's original brewery, pours 20 house beers with a family menu, Monday trivia and a Tuesday kids eat free night. Char Pizzeria handles artisanal pizza, and Never Enough Thyme, a family-owned spot that started in Alpharetta, is known for homemade comfort food.

CT Cantina & Taqueria covers Mexican street food and margaritas, with $2 tacos all day on Tuesdays and a weekday bar-area happy hour from 2pm to 5pm. Call (770) 674-7170. Osteria Bella, from the Furfaro family, is listed as coming soon.

The Market Hall and quick bites

The Market Hall across from the Village Green is Halcyon's food hall, with public restrooms, family restrooms and an ATM inside. Gu's Dumplings serves Szechuan dumplings, noodles and stir-fry (closed Mondays), and Il Bottegone is a little Italian market with cheeses, cured meats and custom charcuterie boards.

Coffee and dessert

BRASH Coffee is the morning stop, open daily from 8am to 4pm. Kilwins handles ice cream, fudge and chocolate until 9pm most nights and 10pm on weekends, and Playa Bowls does acai and smoothie bowls.

Tips

  • Reserve Friday and Saturday dinner at Cattle Shed, Ocean & Acre and Iron Age.
  • Use the 15-minute curbside spots for takeout pickups; park free in the deck for a sit-down meal.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best restaurant at Halcyon?

Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar and Ocean & Acre are Halcyon's top sit-down picks for a nice dinner. Iron Age Korean Steak House is the fun group choice, and Gu's Dumplings in the Market Hall is the favorite casual lunch.

What is in the Market Hall at Halcyon?

The Market Hall holds quick-service food stalls including Gu's Dumplings and Il Bottegone Italian market, plus public restrooms, family restrooms and an ATM.

Which Halcyon restaurants have live music?

Eclipse di Luna has live music most nights, including flamenco guitar and Saturday salsa. HOBNOB has live music Friday and Saturday at 8pm, and Ocean & Acre on Sunday afternoons.
