Holidays at Halcyon 2026

Santa's arrival, a giant menorah, holiday pop-up bars and Halcyon's holiday hours.

OverviewRestaurantsShops and servicesThings to doParking and hoursHappy hour and dealsBrunchHolidaysWith kidsDog friendly

Halcyon's holiday season runs from mid-November through New Year's Day, with a pop-up Christmas bar, a brewery holiday takeover, a Thanksgiving 5K, Santa's arrival on the Village Green and one of the area's biggest Chanukah celebrations. Here is the full 2026 calendar and Halcyon's holiday hours.

Halcyon Holiday Magic: Saturday, Dec. 5

Halcyon officially welcomes Santa and friends to kick off the season at Halcyon Holiday Magic on Saturday, December 5, 2026. It is the main family event of the season at Halcyon, held around the Village Green. Check Halcyon's events page the week before for the schedule and any ticketed add-ons.

More time with Santa and the Grinch

  • Chick-fil-A Dinner with Santa: Saturday, December 12, with tickets sold through Halcyon's event page.
  • HOBNOB's Brunch with the Grinch: Saturdays, December 12 and 19, with photos with the Grinch. These fill up fast.

For more Santa visits around town, including Avalon's Santa cottage and Breakfast with Santa, see Christmas at Avalon and our 2026 Alpharetta holiday events guide.

Chanukah at Halcyon: Sunday, Dec. 6

Halcyon calls Chanukah at Halcyon its biggest Chanukah celebration of the year, open to the whole community, with a giant menorah lighting, a live DJ and children's entertainment. Avalon holds its own menorah lighting the same evening, from 5pm to 6pm.

Holiday pop-up bars

The Elf Upstairs at HOBNOB brings festive cocktails and over-the-top decor starting November 12. Cherry Street Brewville at Cherry Street Brewpub runs from November 23 through New Year's Day.

Thanksgiving and holiday races

Big Peach Running hosts the Great Chicken Chase 5K and Chicken Mini Fun Run on Saturday, November 21, and the Gobble Wobble 5K and Kids Fun Run on Thanksgiving morning, November 26. For more on the holiday, see our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide.

Holiday shopping at Halcyon

Halcyon's boutiques make for easy, low-stress gift shopping: Bluemercury for beauty, Willow Jane, Lizard Thicket and Nina + Leigh for clothing, Piggy Jo's for kids, Buff City Soap for stocking stuffers, and Kilwins for boxed chocolates and fudge. Pick up a bottle at Rise & Wine and a charcuterie board from Il Bottegone for holiday parties. Full list in our Halcyon shops guide.

Tips

  • Arrive early for Holiday Magic and Chanukah at Halcyon; the deck fills on event days.
  • Book Brunch with the Grinch and Dinner with Santa as soon as tickets are posted.
  • Want lights? See our Christmas lights near Alpharetta guide.

Frequently asked questions

When does Santa come to Halcyon in 2026?

Santa officially arrives at Halcyon Holiday Magic on Saturday, December 5, 2026. Chick-fil-A at Halcyon also hosts a ticketed Dinner with Santa on December 12.

Is there a menorah lighting at Halcyon?

Yes. Chanukah at Halcyon is Sunday, December 6, 2026, with a giant menorah lighting, a live DJ and children's entertainment.
