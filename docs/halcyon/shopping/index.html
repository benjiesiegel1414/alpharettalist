Halcyon Shops and Services

Trader Joe's, local boutiques, a wine market and the everyday services at Halcyon.

OverviewRestaurantsShops and servicesThings to doParking and hoursHappy hour and dealsBrunchHolidaysWith kidsDog friendly

Halcyon's shopping is built around everyday stops rather than big-name flagships: a Trader Joe's, a cluster of local boutiques, a wine market and a set of useful services from a vet to a ballet school. Here is every store and service at Halcyon, with phone numbers.

Groceries and wine

  • Trader Joe's: 1605 Halcyon Days Trail, open daily 9am to 9pm. (470) 762-3171.
  • Rise & Wine: a husband-and-wife wine market overlooking the Village Green, with weekday happy hour from 4pm to 7pm. Closed Mondays. (470) 299-2619.
  • Il Bottegone: Italian market in the Market Hall with cheeses, cured meats and charcuterie boards. (678) 694-1331.

Shopping tips

  • Trader Joe's is busiest on weekend mornings; weekday mornings are calmer.
  • Pair a boutique stop with a glass at Rise & Wine or a charcuterie board from Il Bottegone.
  • For bigger-name shopping, Avalon's 75-plus stores are 15 minutes south.
  • Store lineups change, so check Halcyon's directory or call ahead before a special trip.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Trader Joe's at Halcyon?

Yes. Trader Joe's anchors Halcyon's shopping. Call (470) 762-3171 for store hours.

What stores are at Halcyon?

Halcyon's shops include Trader Joe's, Bluemercury, Lizard Thicket, Willow Jane, Nina + Leigh, Piggy Jo's, Buff City Soap, RW Design & Exchange, BBQGuys and Rise & Wine.
