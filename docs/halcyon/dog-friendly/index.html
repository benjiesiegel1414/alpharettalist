Halcyon with Your Dog

The rules, the best patios and the dog services on site at Halcyon.

OverviewRestaurantsShops and servicesThings to doParking and hoursHappy hour and dealsBrunchHolidaysWith kidsDog friendly

Yes, Halcyon is dog friendly. Leashed, well-behaved dogs are welcome in the common areas, the Big Creek Greenway is right next door, and there is a dog daycare and a vet on site. Halcyon does have specific rules, though, so here is what to know before you bring your dog.

Halcyon's pet rules

From Halcyon's rules of conduct:

  • Customers 18 or older may bring leashed, well-behaved domesticated animals to the common areas.
  • Leashes can be no longer than six feet.
  • Two animals per person, at most.
  • Never leave a pet unattended, including in a car.
  • Clean up after your pet right away.
  • Pets may not go inside restaurants or food stores unless they are service animals or the business allows it.
  • Pets must stay out of fountains, water displays and the lake.

Eating out with your dog

Because dogs cannot go inside restaurants, plan on an outdoor table. Several Halcyon restaurants have patios facing the Village Green and Town Square; ask the host whether dogs are welcome on the patio before you sit down, since each restaurant sets its own policy. For a quick bite, pick up takeout from the Market Hall or Char Pizzeria and find a spot on the green.

Walk the Big Creek Greenway

The best dog walk at Halcyon is the Big Creek Greenway. A 1.5-mile trail extension starts near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway and runs through shaded woods along Big Creek on a 12-foot-wide concrete and boardwalk path. Keep your dog leashed and bring water on warm days. Read our Big Creek Greenway guide for trailheads and parking.

Dog services at Halcyon

  • Dogtopia: daycare, overnight boarding and spa services, with webcams for owners. (770) 676-2283.
  • Chewy Vet Care: a veterinary clinic on site. (678) 264-3006.

Dog-friendly events

Halcyon hosts dog-themed events through the year, such as Doodle Dog Day, and its November 2026 chili cook-off on the Village Green benefits Furkids, a local animal rescue. Watch Halcyon's events page for the next one.

Tips

  • Weekday mornings are calmest; weekend evenings and event days are crowded.
  • Halcyon is smoke-free and glass-free in common areas, which keeps the green safer for paws.
  • For another dog-friendly outing, see Avalon with your dog, which has a fenced dog park, and our dog-friendly restaurants in Alpharetta guide.

Frequently asked questions

Are dogs allowed at Halcyon?

Yes. Customers 18 and older may bring leashed, well-behaved dogs to Halcyon's common areas. Leashes can be no longer than six feet, and each person may bring up to two animals.

Can dogs go inside restaurants at Halcyon?

No, unless they are service animals or the business allows it. Halcyon's rules also keep dogs out of fountains, water features and the lake.
