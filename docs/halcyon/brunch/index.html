Brunch at Halcyon

Six weekend brunch spots at Halcyon, with hours and what each one does best.

OverviewRestaurantsShops and servicesThings to doParking and hoursHappy hour and dealsBrunchHolidaysWith kidsDog friendly

Weekend brunch is one of the best times to visit Halcyon. Six restaurants serve brunch on Saturday and Sunday, there is live music on Sunday afternoons, and the Village Green is right there for kids who finish first. Here is where to brunch at Halcyon, with hours.

Halcyon brunch hours

Times are from Halcyon's weekly events page and each restaurant's listing, checked in November 2026. Holiday weekends can change hours, so call ahead.

Our picks

For a family brunch: Never Enough Thyme opens earliest, at 10am, and its homemade comfort food suits picky eaters. Cherry Street is roomy and family friendly, and Sunday is its Family Fun Day.

For a brunch with music: Ocean & Acre has live music on Sundays from 1pm to 4pm, and Eclipse di Luna has flamenco guitar on weekend afternoons from 12:30pm to 3:30pm, though it does not list a formal brunch.

For a celebration: Cattle Shed serves brunch the longest, until 4pm, with more than 200 wines and wine-infused cocktails.

For game day: HOBNOB opens at 10am on weekends and pairs brunch with its bourbon list and TVs.

Holiday brunch: Brunch with the Grinch

HOBNOB hosts Brunch with the Grinch, with photos with the Grinch, on Saturday, December 12 and Saturday, December 19, 2026. It is popular, so book as soon as reservations open. See Halcyon holidays 2026.

Brunch tips

  • Arrive by 11am on Sundays to beat the after-church rush, or reserve where you can.
  • Park free in the deck; the curbside spots are only 15 minutes.
  • After brunch, walk it off on the Big Creek Greenway trail extension, or let kids play on the Village Green.
  • Grab a coffee at BRASH (open 8am to 4pm) if you are waiting on a table.
  • More options around town: our Alpharetta brunch guide and brunch at Avalon.

Frequently asked questions

What time is brunch at Halcyon?

Weekend brunch runs 10am to 2pm at Never Enough Thyme, 11am to 3pm at Ocean & Acre, Cherry Street and CT Cantina, and 11am to 4pm at Cattle Shed. HOBNOB serves brunch until 4pm.
