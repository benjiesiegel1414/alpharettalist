Things to Do at Halcyon

Indoor golf, leagues, live music, trivia, the Big Creek Greenway and the Village Green.

OverviewRestaurantsShops and servicesThings to doParking and hoursHappy hour and dealsBrunchHolidaysWith kidsDog friendly

Halcyon is built for hanging out. Beyond dinner and Trader Joe's, you can play a simulated round of golf, join a cornhole or bocce league, catch live music or trivia, let kids loose on the Village Green and walk straight onto the Big Creek Greenway. Here are the best things to do at Halcyon.

Play indoor golf at X-Golf

X-Golf is Halcyon's indoor golf simulator and sports bar, with bays for solo practice, lessons, a round with friends or a company outing, plus a full bar and shareable appetizers. It is open 10am to 10pm Monday through Saturday and 10am to 6pm on Sunday. Weekly drink specials have included $5 margaritas on Mondays and $6 Tito's cocktails on Tuesdays. Book a bay ahead for weekend nights.

Walk or bike the Big Creek Greenway

A 1.5-mile trail extension starts near the intersection of Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway and connects to Forsyth County's Big Creek Greenway, a 12-foot-wide concrete and boardwalk path through deciduous woods along Big Creek. It suits walking, running and biking. When future phases are finished, the greenway is planned to run about 16 miles in Forsyth County, from the Halcyon trailhead to the Sawnee Nature Preserve. Read our full Big Creek Greenway guide.

Spend time on the Village Green

The Village Green is Halcyon's heart, with a sandbox, yard games and room for kids to run. It hosts events through the year, from a chili cook-off benefiting Furkids to Halcyon Holiday Magic in December. Grab takeout from the Market Hall and eat on the grass.

Join a cornhole or bocce league

Halcyon runs seven-week seasonal leagues: Monday and Wednesday cornhole and Thursday bocce. The fall 2026 leagues started the week of November 16. Watch Halcyon's events page for the next season's sign-ups.

Trivia, music bingo and live music

  • Monday: trivia at Cherry Street Brewpub at 7:30pm (21 and up); flamenco at Eclipse di Luna, 6pm to 9pm.
  • Tuesday: live band at Eclipse di Luna, 6:30pm to 9:30pm.
  • Thursday: music bingo at HOBNOB at 7pm, and ladies night from 5pm.
  • Friday and Saturday: live music at HOBNOB at 8pm.
  • Saturday: salsa and merengue at Eclipse di Luna from 7:30pm, then a DJ until 1:15am.
  • Sunday: live music at Ocean & Acre, 1pm to 4pm, and flamenco guitar at Eclipse di Luna.

Full details in our Halcyon happy hour and weekly deals guide.

Run a holiday 5K

Big Peach Running hosts races at Halcyon, including the Great Chicken Chase 5K on November 21 and the Gobble Wobble 5K and Fun Run on Thanksgiving morning, November 26, 2026.

Get pampered

Book a facial at Clean Your Dirty Face, a manicure at Lush Nail Bar, a treatment at SafiraMD or a workout at The Forum Athletic Club. Phone numbers are in our Halcyon shops and services guide.

Wine, charcuterie and a picnic

Pick up a bottle at Rise & Wine and a board from Il Bottegone. Note Halcyon's rules: alcohol is limited to designated areas and containers, and glass is not allowed in common areas, so ask staff what is allowed before heading to the green.

What about movies?

The CMX CinéBistro at Halcyon has closed, and Halcyon says it is working on a new concept for the space. The nearest theaters are Regal Avalon and the AMC at North Point Mall.

More ideas

For bigger-scale shopping and entertainment, see things to do at Avalon. For the rest of the area, our things to do in Alpharetta list covers parks, live music and day trips.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do at Halcyon besides eat?

Halcyon has X-Golf indoor golf simulators, cornhole and bocce leagues on the Village Green, trivia and live music at its restaurants, direct access to the Big Creek Greenway, and community events through the year.

How do you get to the Big Creek Greenway from Halcyon?

The trailhead is near the intersection of Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway. A 1.5-mile trail extension connects there, and the greenway is a 12-foot-wide concrete and boardwalk path.
