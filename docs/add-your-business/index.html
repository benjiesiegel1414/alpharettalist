Add your business to Alpharetta List

Listings are free for Alpharetta businesses. Email us your details and we'll add or update your listing, usually within a few days.

To add a new listing, or to claim or correct an existing one, send us an email with the details below.

Email hello@alpharettalist.com

What to include

  • Business name
  • Category and type of business (for example: sushi restaurant, HVAC, pediatric dentist)
  • Street address in Alpharetta
  • Phone number and website
  • Hours of operation
  • A two- or three-sentence description of what you do
  • If you're updating an existing listing, the link to its page

Prefer to copy and paste? Put everything in the body of one email and we'll take it from there.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free to list my business?

Yes. Basic listings on Alpharetta List are free and always will be. Each listing gets its own page with your address, hours, phone, website, a description and practical tips for customers.

Which businesses can be listed?

Businesses with a location in Alpharetta, or service businesses that regularly serve Alpharetta homes and companies. That includes restaurants, shops, home services, health and wellness practices, schools, real estate, fitness, pets and family activities.

How long does it take for my listing to go live?

Usually a few days. We check the details against your website or public records before publishing so that customers can rely on what they read.

How do I correct or remove my listing?

Email us the link to your listing page and what should change. Use the "Claim this listing" link at the bottom of any listing page to start an email with the right details filled in.

Can I pay to be featured?

Basic listings and their placement aren't for sale, but we offer clearly labeled sponsorships. See our advertising page for options.

What happens next
  • We verify the business is in or serves Alpharetta
  • Your listing goes live with its own page
  • Spot an error on an existing listing? Email us the correction
