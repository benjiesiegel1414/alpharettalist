    Late November 2026 (date announced by the city)

    Noon Year's Eve at The Hotel at Avalon

    December 31, 2026

    A family-friendly early New Year's party with a balloon drop at noon, so the kids can celebrate without the midnight bedtime.

    The Hotel at Avalon, 9000 Avalon Blvd, Alpharetta, GA 30009

    Alpharetta Farmers Market (2027 season)

    Saturday mornings, April through November

    Local growers, bakers, makers and artisans on the Town Green every Saturday morning, 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., April through November.

    Town Green, Downtown Alpharetta, 2 Park Plaza, Alpharetta, GA 30009

  • AprTBA

    Alpharetta Food Truck Alley (2027 season)

    4th Thursday of the month, April through October, 5 to 9 p.m.

    Ten or more food trucks plus live music one Thursday evening a month. Seating is limited, so bring a chair or blanket; pets are not permitted.

    Old Roswell Street, Downtown, 37 Old Roswell St, Alpharetta, GA 30009
