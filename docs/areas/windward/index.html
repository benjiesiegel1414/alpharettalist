51 places in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11, from restaurants, parks and trails, health and medical, beauty and spas and local services. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Other parts of town: Downtown Alpharetta · North Point · Old Milton Pkwy · Haynes Bridge · Crabapple · North Main St · McGinnis Ferry · Roswell · Avalon · Halcyon.
