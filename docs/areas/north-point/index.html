Everything we list in North Point, with hours and phone numbers.

46 places9 categoriesFree to list

46 places in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10, from restaurants, things to do, shopping, beauty and spas and kids and classes. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.

Other parts of town: Downtown Alpharetta · Windward · Old Milton Pkwy · Haynes Bridge · North Main St · Crabapple · McGinnis Ferry · Roswell · Avalon · Halcyon.
