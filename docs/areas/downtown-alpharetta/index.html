Downtown Alpharetta, Alpharetta: restaurants, shops and services

Everything we list in this part of Alpharetta, with hours and phone numbers.

69 places11 categoriesFree to list

69 places in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green, from restaurants, parks and trails, things to do, arts and history and shopping. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Other parts of town: Windward · North Point · Old Milton Pkwy · Haynes Bridge · Crabapple · North Main St · McGinnis Ferry · Roswell · Avalon · Halcyon.

Memorial

Walk of Memories

Veterans memorial at American Legion Post 201 with nearly 8,000 engraved bricks honoring Georgians who served in every branch. Monuments mark World War II, Korea and Vietnam, and displays include a Cold War-era M60 tank, a UH-1 Huey flown in the 1968 Tet Offensive, a Navy twin 40mm gun and an Air Force T-33 trainer.
