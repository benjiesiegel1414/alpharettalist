21 places in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400, from restaurants, parks and trails, shopping, fitness and gyms and health and medical. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

Haynes Bridge Road crosses GA 400 in south Alpharetta, close to the Big Creek Greenway and Old Milton Parkway. Businesses here are mostly in shopping centers and office parks with their own parking.

Other parts of town: Downtown Alpharetta · Windward · North Point · Old Milton Pkwy · Crabapple · North Main St · McGinnis Ferry · Roswell · Avalon · Halcyon.
