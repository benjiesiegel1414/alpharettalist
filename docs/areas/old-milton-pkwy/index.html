37 places on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown, from restaurants, shopping, arts and history, health and medical and fitness and gyms. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.

Other parts of town: Downtown Alpharetta · Windward · North Point · Haynes Bridge · North Main St · Crabapple · McGinnis Ferry · Roswell · Avalon · Halcyon.
