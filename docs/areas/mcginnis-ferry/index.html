14 places along McGinnis Ferry Road on Alpharetta's east side, near the Johns Creek line, from restaurants, parks and trails, things to do, pets and vets and local services. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

McGinnis Ferry Road runs along Alpharetta's northeast edge near the Johns Creek and Forsyth County lines. It's a busy commuter route with shopping centers, medical offices and neighborhoods on both sides.
