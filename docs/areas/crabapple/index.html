18 places in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line, from parks and trails, fitness and gyms, kids and classes, restaurants and health and medical. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

Crabapple sits on Alpharetta's west side near the Milton line, known for its rolling, horse-country feel, larger lots and the historic Crabapple crossroads just over the line in Milton. Most businesses here are in small shopping centers with easy parking.

Other parts of town: Downtown Alpharetta · Windward · North Point · Old Milton Pkwy · Haynes Bridge · North Main St · McGinnis Ferry · Roswell · Avalon · Halcyon.
