North Main St, Alpharetta: restaurants, shops and services

Everything we list in North Main St, with hours and phone numbers.

17 places7 categoriesFree to list

17 places on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta, from restaurants, shopping, beauty and spas, health and medical and kids and classes. Every listing has an address and, where available, hours and a phone number.

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.

Other parts of town: Downtown Alpharetta · Windward · North Point · Old Milton Pkwy · Haynes Bridge · Crabapple · McGinnis Ferry · Roswell · Avalon · Halcyon.
