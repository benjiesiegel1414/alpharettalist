Things to Do in Alpharetta This Weekend (Nov. 20-22, 2026): Tree Lightings, Santa and Avalon on Ice

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 16, 20265 min read

This is the weekend Alpharetta flips the switch on the holidays. From Friday, Nov. 20 through Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, there are three tree lightings, a Santa parade at North Point Mall, an artists market on Canton Street and the opening of Avalon on Ice. Almost all of it is free. Here's the plan, with times, addresses and parking tips.

Quick answer: the weekend at a glance

  • Fri., Nov. 20, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m.: The Hamilton Alpharetta's Christmas Tree Lighting, 35 Milton Ave. Tree lights at 6:30 p.m. Free with an Eventbrite RSVP.
  • Sat., Nov. 21, 10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.: Artist Holiday Market at the Alpharetta Arts Center, 238 Canton St.
  • Sat., Nov. 21, noon to 3 p.m.: North Point Mall Holiday Kickoff, with a parade through the mall and Santa arriving aboard the train.
  • Sat., Nov. 21, 5 to 8 p.m.: Season of Celebration, the 50-foot tree lighting in front of Alpharetta City Hall. Free.
  • Sun., Nov. 22, 2 to 7:30 p.m.: The Lighting of Avalon, with the 40-foot tree lit at 7 p.m. and Avalon on Ice opening at 3 p.m. Free to attend; skating is $18 ($14 for kids 12 and under).

Want the whole season in one place? Our Christmas and holiday events guide runs through New Year's, and the events calendar is updated daily.

Friday: The Hamilton's tree lighting

The weekend starts downtown at The Hamilton Alpharetta, the Curio Collection hotel at 35 Milton Ave. The celebration runs 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. with live music, holiday caroling, photos with Santa, festive treats and a Hot Cocoa and Baileys Bar (cocoa for the kids, spiked versions for adults). The countdown to the tree lighting is at 6:30 p.m.

It's free, but the hotel asks guests to RSVP on Eventbrite. Make a night of it with dinner nearby: our best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta list is all within walking distance.

Rooftop fans, take note: UP on the Roof at 33 S. Main St. is running its Christmas-themed Elf'd UP pop-up, with kitschy holiday decor and seasonal cocktails, listed from 3 to 11 p.m. on both Friday and Sunday this weekend.

Saturday daytime: Santa at North Point and holiday shopping

North Point Mall Holiday Kickoff (noon to 3 p.m.). North Point Mall welcomes the season with a parade through the mall, and Santa arrives aboard the train. It's a good pick for toddlers and anyone who'd rather skip the evening crowds. The mall's Santa set, The North Point Pole, is also taking photo appointments, and the CVB recommends booking ahead because Santa's calendar fills fast.

Artist Holiday Market (10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.). Arts Alpharetta's annual market fills the Alpharetta Arts Center at 238 Canton St. with handmade jewelry, ceramics, seasonal decor and wearable art from new and returning local artists. It's an easy way to knock out a few gifts and shop local before Black Friday.

Friendship Ring of Kids holiday display (9 a.m. to 5 p.m.). Over at the Alpharetta Community Center, 175 Roswell St., the much-loved Friendship Ring sculpture gets a winter makeover, with the kids dressed in colorful winter hats. It's a free, quick photo stop between the market and dinner.

Art after dark. The Scene Art Gallery, 2070 North Point Circle, hosts its monthly third-Saturday event: a live paint-around at 4:30 p.m., then music, food and drinks from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. as a new collection is unveiled. This month's theme is "Feed Your Eyes: A Visual Feast."

Saturday night: Season of Celebration downtown

The city's own tree lighting is the big one. Season of Celebration runs 5 to 8 p.m. on the main stage in front of Alpharetta City Hall in Downtown Alpharetta, ending with the lighting of a 50-foot tree. Expect live holiday music, snow flurries, marshmallow roasting, sing-alongs, strolling characters and a visit from Santa. Bring portable chairs.

Road closures: the city is closing Park Plaza (from Academy Street to the City Hall parking deck entrance), Commerce Street, North and South Broad Street and Market Street from 6 a.m. Saturday until 1 a.m. Sunday. On-street parking is prohibited on those streets during that window, so plan to use a deck or walk in.

Sunday: The Lighting of Avalon and opening day on the ice

Avalon closes out the weekend with its free Lighting of Avalon, 2 to 7:30 p.m. Here's the schedule:

  • 2 to 3 p.m.: Atlanta Figure Skating Club performance at the rink
  • 2 to 5 p.m.: Activity Row on Avalon Boulevard, with face painting, holiday crafts and a photo booth
  • 3 p.m.: Avalon on Ice opens to the public (open until 10 p.m. on opening day)
  • 3 to 5:15 p.m.: holiday performances on the stage
  • 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.: The Big Beyond performs
  • 7 p.m.: Santa leads the countdown to light the 40-foot tree

Parking: Avalon's parking decks are free, valet is on 3rd Street, and shuttles will run from Gwinnett Technical College across the street. Avalon says parking fills quickly and recommends carpooling or ride-share.

Skating: Avalon on Ice runs Nov. 22 through Jan. 18. Tickets are $18 for general admission and $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. Reservations are recommended, and walk-ups are welcome but not guaranteed a time slot. Everyone, walk-ups included, needs to complete the waiver. Our Avalon on Ice guide has the full season of hours and tips.

Booking Santa photos for next week

Santa moves into his cottage in Avalon's Palmer Plaza on Monday, Nov. 23, and stays through Christmas Eve. Packages run from $40 (Santa Photo Experience with five digital photos) to $50 (Value Bundle with digital photos, prints and Christmas cards). Appointments are recommended; walk-ins are added to a text waitlist, and Avalon suggests weekdays for shorter waits. Pet portraits are walk-up only on Tuesdays, 6 to 8 p.m. Santa is closed on Thanksgiving. More Santa options are in our Christmas with kids guide.

Looking ahead: Thanksgiving week

Avalon on Ice runs extended holiday hours of 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, which makes it an easy outing while relatives are in town. If you're still sorting out the big meal, see our roundup of Thanksgiving dinner restaurants and to-go meals, and check Black Friday hours for Avalon, North Point and Halcyon. After Dec. 4, the mile-long Alpha Loop lights up nightly for Lights on the Loop; our Christmas lights guide has the details.

Tips for a smooth weekend

  • Arrive early for the tree lightings. The best views go to people who show up 30 to 45 minutes ahead.
  • Dress for the evening. All three lightings are outdoors after dark, so bring layers and gloves for little ones.
  • Book skating ahead for Sunday. Opening day is popular, and reservations guarantee a time slot.
  • Don't drive downtown on Saturday night if you can help it. Several streets are closed and on-street parking is off-limits.
  • Hungry? Our restaurant directory and best brunch list cover the morning after.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do in Alpharetta this weekend (Nov. 20-22, 2026)?

It's holiday kickoff weekend: The Hamilton Alpharetta's tree lighting on Friday (5:30-8:30 p.m.), the Artist Holiday Market, North Point Mall's Holiday Kickoff and the downtown Season of Celebration tree lighting on Saturday, and the Lighting of Avalon plus Avalon on Ice opening day on Sunday.

What time is the Alpharetta tree lighting in 2026?

Season of Celebration is Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, from 5 to 8 p.m. in front of Alpharetta City Hall in Downtown Alpharetta, with a 50-foot tree lighting, live music, snow flurries, marshmallow roasting and Santa. It's free.

What time is the Lighting of Avalon 2026?

The Lighting of Avalon is Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, from 2 to 7:30 p.m. Santa leads the countdown to light the 40-foot tree at 7 p.m. The event is free, and Avalon on Ice opens to the public at 3 p.m. that day.

When does Avalon on Ice open in 2026 and how much is it?

Avalon on Ice opens Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026 (3 to 10 p.m. on opening day) and runs through Jan. 18, 2027. Tickets are $18 for general admission and $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. Reservations are recommended.

Where do I park for the Lighting of Avalon?

Avalon's parking decks are free, valet is available on 3rd Street, and shuttles run from Gwinnett Technical College across the street. Avalon recommends carpooling or ride-share because parking fills quickly.

When does Santa arrive at North Point Mall in 2026?

North Point Mall's Holiday Kickoff is Saturday, Nov. 21, from noon to 3 p.m., with a parade through the mall and Santa arriving aboard the train.
