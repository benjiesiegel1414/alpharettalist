The Best Brunch in Alpharetta, GA (2026)

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 11, 20265 min read

Weekend brunch is practically a sport in Alpharetta. Between Avalon, downtown and the Windward corridor, you can go from rooftop mimosas to a slow-baked apple pancake to Tex-Mex migas without leaving town. Here are the brunch and breakfast spots locals keep going back to, with weekend hours checked against each restaurant's own website in November 2026.

Quick picks

  • Best for a celebration: South City Kitchen Avalon
  • Best rooftop: Up on the Roof
  • Best for a big group: Superica
  • Best late brunch: Delbar (served until 3:45 p.m.)
  • Best classic breakfast: The Original Pancake House
  • Best with kids and a beer garden: Cherry Street Brewpub at Halcyon

Brunch at Avalon

South City Kitchen Avalon is the dress-up option. Avalon lists its weekend hours as 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. on Saturday and Sunday, and it also serves breakfast on weekdays from 7 to 10 a.m. Expect Southern classics like buttermilk fried chicken, shrimp and grits and fried green tomatoes. It's a popular pick for birthdays and visiting family, so book ahead.

Superica, Ford Fry's Tex-Mex cantina, runs brunch Saturday and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. The room is loud in a good way, which makes it an easy call for groups and families. Reservations and online ordering are both available on its website.

Colletta on Third Street brings an Italian spin to the weekend, with brunch Saturday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. It's a nice middle ground when half your group wants eggs and the other half wants pasta.

Brunching at Avalon over the holidays? Pair it with a skate session. Our Avalon on Ice guide has rink hours and ticket prices.

Downtown Alpharetta

Up on the Roof on South Main is the rooftop brunch in town. Its site lists brunch service on Saturday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., with reservations available. On a sunny fall weekend the views over downtown are hard to beat.

Carrie's inside The Hamilton hotel serves brunch Saturday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., followed by a daily social hour from 2 to 5 p.m. If you're an early riser, the hotel's Fulton's Public House serves breakfast daily from 7 to 11 a.m.

The Southern Porch on North Main is a breakfast, brunch and lunch spot built around freshly baked biscuits and Southern comfort classics. It's a laid-back choice for a weekday morning too.

Maple Street Biscuit Company on Canton Street is the quick, casual option: hearty biscuit sandwiches you can eat in or grab before a walk around downtown.

A note on Hen Mother Cookhouse: the Michelin-recommended downtown spot is temporarily closed following a small fire, according to Rough Draft Atlanta's Nov. 5 restaurant roundup. Its Johns Creek location remains open. We'll update this guide when the Alpharetta location reopens.

Windward and Old Milton Parkway

The Original Pancake House on Windward Parkway has been an Alpharetta fixture since 2007. It's open Monday to Friday from 6:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. and Saturday and Sunday from 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. The star is the Apple Pancake, which the restaurant says takes nearly an hour to bake, so order it as soon as you sit down. The Dutch Baby is the other must-try.

J. Christopher's at Windward Plaza is the reliable weekday-and-weekend standby, open 7 a.m. to 2 p.m. on weekdays and 7 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. on Saturday and Sunday. It's a good bet when you need a table for a crowd without a long plan.

Delbar on Old Milton Parkway serves Middle Eastern brunch on Saturday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 3:45 p.m., the latest weekend brunch window on this list. Heads up for the holidays: its site lists it as closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas.

Just over the line at Halcyon

Cherry Street Brewpub at Halcyon serves brunch on Saturdays and Sundays alongside 20 craft beers on tap, a kids menu and a patio. Halcyon is an open-container development, so you can take a drink to go and stroll after you eat. It's a strong pick for families who want a relaxed, outdoorsy weekend morning.

Brunch tips for Alpharetta

  • Reserve where you can. Avalon and rooftop spots fill up fast on weekends, especially during the holidays.
  • Go early or late. Breakfast spots like The Original Pancake House and J. Christopher's open at 7 a.m. on weekends, and Delbar serves until 3:45 p.m.
  • Check holiday hours. Hours can change around Thanksgiving and Christmas. Our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide covers holiday dining plans.
  • Make a morning of it. Pair brunch with a walk on the Big Creek Greenway or a coffee from our best coffee shops list.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best brunch in Alpharetta?

Local favorites include South City Kitchen Avalon, Superica and Colletta at Avalon, Up on the Roof and Carrie's at The Hamilton downtown, Delbar on Old Milton Parkway, and The Original Pancake House and J. Christopher's on the Windward corridor.

Which Alpharetta restaurants serve brunch latest?

Delbar on Old Milton Parkway serves brunch Saturday and Sunday until 3:45 p.m. Superica and Up on the Roof both serve weekend brunch until 3 p.m.

Is there rooftop brunch in Alpharetta?

Yes. Up on the Roof on South Main Street in downtown Alpharetta serves brunch Saturday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., with reservations available.

Is Hen Mother Cookhouse in Alpharetta open?

The downtown Alpharetta location is temporarily closed following a small fire, according to Rough Draft Atlanta's Nov. 5, 2026 roundup. The Johns Creek location remains open.

Where can I get an early breakfast in Alpharetta on weekends?

The Original Pancake House on Windward Parkway and J. Christopher's at Windward Plaza both open at 7 a.m. on Saturday and Sunday. South City Kitchen Avalon starts weekend brunch at 8 a.m.
