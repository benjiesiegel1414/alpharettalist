Veterans Day 2026 in Alpharetta: Events, Free Meals and Tributes

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 6, 20265 min read

Veterans Day falls on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, and Alpharetta's tributes start days earlier. American Legion Post 201 on Wills Road hosts a car show, a flag retirement ceremony and a Veterans Day party, a downtown biscuit shop serves a free breakfast for veterans, and a long list of restaurants near town offer free meals on the day itself. Here's how to honor the veterans in your life this week, close to home.

Veterans Day week at a glance

  • Saturday, Nov. 7: 10th Annual Veterans Day Cruz-In at American Legion Post 201, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Free.
  • Sunday, Nov. 8: Flag Retirement Ceremony at Post 201, 4 to 5:30 p.m. Open to the public.
  • Monday, Nov. 9: 7th Annual Pre-Veterans Day Breakfast for veterans at Maple Street Biscuit Company downtown, 7 to 10 a.m.
  • Wednesday, Nov. 11: Veterans Day. Free food for veterans at Post 201 from 4 to 10 p.m., free meal deals around town, and free entry to every National Park.

American Legion Post 201 events

Founded in 1947, American Legion Post 201 sits on 13 acres at 201 Wills Road, just down the street from Wills Park. It's the center of Veterans Day week in Alpharetta, and most of its events are open to everyone.

  • 10th Annual Veterans Day Cruz-In (Sat., Nov. 7, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.): the Post opens its front lawn to vehicles of every kind. It's free to attend, with food, drinks and kids' activities, and proceeds support veterans and their families. Bring the car buffs in your family, or just come to look.
  • Flag Retirement Ceremony (Sun., Nov. 8, 4 to 5:30 p.m.): a local Scout troop holds its annual ceremony to properly retire worn American flags on the grassy area by the back parking lot. The public is welcome, and guests are invited into the clubroom afterward.
  • Veterans Day at Post 201 (Wed., Nov. 11, 4 to 10 p.m.): free food and a free first drink for all veterans, with hot dogs and toppings, sides and cake, plus live music in the clubroom from 7 to 9 p.m.

Any day of the year, the Post's Walk of Memories is worth a visit. The walkway has almost 8,000 bricks inscribed with the names of Georgians who served in the Army, Navy, Marines, Air Force and Coast Guard, with a collection of military equipment alongside. The Post calls it the only memorial of its kind in Georgia, honoring veterans of conflicts from the Indian Wars through Iraq and Afghanistan. Questions? Call the Post at 770-475-9023.

Free veterans breakfast downtown

For the seventh year, a local realtor is treating veterans to breakfast at Maple Street Biscuit Company, 50 Canton Street, on Monday, Nov. 9 from 7 to 10 a.m. No RSVP is needed, and to-go orders and curbside pickup are available. The host started the tradition to honor everyone who served, with a special nod to a grandfather who flew bombers in World War II and Korea. Organizers note that anyone still in line at 10 a.m. will be taken care of.

While you're downtown, stop by Brooke Street Park behind City Hall. It's home to the Alpharetta Veterans Memorial, dedicated on Veterans Day 2015. Its centerpiece is "The Defenders," a 7-foot bronze statue of two soldiers in combat gear, funded by more than $100,000 in private donations from local businesses and residents.

Veterans Day free meals and deals near Alpharetta

Plenty of national chains honor veterans and active-duty military on Nov. 11. Offers usually require proof of service, are often dine-in only and don't include tax or tip, and participation can vary by location, so call your local spot before you go. A few highlights for 2026:

  • Lazy Dog Restaurant and Bar (Alpharetta): a free Fireside Favorites entrée, a personal-size dessert and a soda for active-duty military, veterans, retirees and Reserve and National Guard members.
  • Dave & Buster's: free unlimited game play for veterans and active-duty military, plus a free dessert with an entrée purchase. Since the holiday lands on a Wednesday, friends and family get half-price games too.
  • Applebee's: a free entrée from a special menu, dine-in only, plus a $5 card for a future visit.
  • Outback Steakhouse: a free Bloomin' Onion and a beverage on Nov. 10 and 11.
  • Chili's and California Pizza Kitchen: a free entrée from a select menu.
  • Starbucks: a free tall brewed coffee for veterans, active military and their spouses.
  • Breakfast picks: a free Grand Slam at Denny's (5 a.m. to noon) and a free coffee and bagel with schmear at Einstein Bros. Bagels.
  • Quick bites: a free Dave's Single at Wendy's, a free smoothie at Smoothie King (noon to 3 p.m.), free 10 boneless wings and fries at Buffalo Wild Wings and a buy-one-get-one sub at Firehouse Subs.

Beyond restaurants, Great Clips and many Sport Clips locations offer free haircuts, Target has a 20% discount for verified military families through Nov. 11, and admission to every National Park is free for everyone that day. For more places to eat, browse our Alpharetta restaurant guide.

Veterans Day ceremonies nearby

We didn't find a city-hosted Veterans Day ceremony announced for Alpharetta this year, but neighboring communities have several free, public ones:

  • Milton Veterans Breakfast (Sat., Nov. 7, 9 a.m.): a free breakfast for local veterans and their families at Milton City Hall, 2006 Heritage Walk. Seating is limited and an advance RSVP is required through the City of Milton.
  • Georgia Veterans Day Parade (Sat., Nov. 7, 11 a.m.): the 45th annual parade runs along Peachtree Street from 15th Street to 5th Street in Midtown Atlanta, after a 9:30 a.m. observance at the Woodruff Arts Center.
  • Roswell Veterans Day Ceremony (Wed., Nov. 11, 8:30 a.m.): an honor walk and ceremony at the pond in Roswell Area Park.
  • Sandy Springs Veterans Day Tribute (Wed., Nov. 11, 11:30 a.m.): the city's 17th annual tribute at the Sandy Springs Performing Arts Center, with a JROTC color guard and patriotic music. Free and open to all.
  • Marietta Veterans Day Parade (Sat., Nov. 14, 10:40 a.m.): marching bands, floats and military vehicles timed to reach Marietta Square at 11:11 a.m., followed by a noon observance.

Frequently asked questions

When is Veterans Day 2026?

Veterans Day is Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

What Veterans Day events are in Alpharetta in 2026?

American Legion Post 201, 201 Wills Road, hosts the 10th Annual Veterans Day Cruz-In on Saturday, Nov. 7 (11 a.m. to 3 p.m., free), a Flag Retirement Ceremony on Sunday, Nov. 8 (4 to 5:30 p.m.) and a Veterans Day celebration on Nov. 11 from 4 to 10 p.m. with free food and a free first drink for veterans.

Is there a free breakfast for veterans in Alpharetta?

Yes. The 7th Annual Pre-Veterans Day Breakfast is Monday, Nov. 9 from 7 to 10 a.m. at Maple Street Biscuit Company, 50 Canton Street. Breakfast is free for veterans, no RSVP is needed, and to-go and curbside pickup are available.

Which restaurants near Alpharetta offer free meals for veterans on Veterans Day 2026?

Offers announced for Nov. 11, 2026 include a free entrée, dessert and soda at Lazy Dog in Alpharetta, free unlimited game play at Dave & Buster's, and free meals or items at Applebee's, Chili's, California Pizza Kitchen, Denny's, Wendy's, Starbucks and others. Most require proof of service, and participation varies by location.

Where is the Alpharetta Veterans Memorial?

The Alpharetta Veterans Memorial is in Brooke Street Park behind City Hall downtown. Its centerpiece is "The Defenders," a 7-foot bronze statue of two soldiers. American Legion Post 201 on Wills Road also has the Walk of Memories, with almost 8,000 inscribed bricks honoring Georgians who served.
