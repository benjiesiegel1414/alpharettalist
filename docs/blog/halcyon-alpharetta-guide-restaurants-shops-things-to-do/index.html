Halcyon Guide: Restaurants, Shops and Things to Do

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 11, 20267 min read

Halcyon is the 135-acre walkable district just north of Alpharetta, off GA 400 at exit 12, and it has quietly become one of the area's favorite places to eat, shop and hang out on the green. Here is everything you need for a visit: hours, free parking, the restaurants worth booking, the shops, what to do with kids, and Halcyon's 2026 holiday events and hours. We checked all of it against Halcyon's own site in November 2026.

Halcyon at a glance

  • Address: 6365 Halcyon Way, Alpharetta, GA 30005. It has an Alpharetta mailing address but sits just over the line in southern Forsyth County, at GA 400 exit 12 (McFarland Parkway).
  • Hours: Monday through Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday 11am to 6pm. Restaurants and X-Golf keep their own hours, and many stay open later.
  • Parking: free in the six-level parking deck. There are 15-minute curbside spots for to-go pickups and premium paid spaces in the event lot and along Halcyon Way. Rideshare pickup is in the surface lot.
  • Phone: (470) 223-3665 for the property office.
  • Good to know: free Wi-Fi in common areas, pets welcome, and public restrooms, family restrooms and an ATM inside the Market Hall.

Where to eat at Halcyon

Dining is Halcyon's biggest draw. These are the sit-down spots worth planning around.

Ocean & Acre sits in a bright, barn-like building and pairs smoked meats and seasonal produce with shareable seafood and a raw bar. It is open daily from 11am, until 10pm Monday through Saturday and 9pm on Sunday.

Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar, from the owners of HOBNOB, is the special-occasion pick: premium steaks, seafood, charcuterie and cheese boards, more than 200 wines and wine-infused cocktails, with a menu inspired by Charleston. It opens at 11am daily and serves until 10pm Tuesday through Saturday.

Iron Age Korean Steak House brings tabletop-grill Korean barbecue, with staff guiding you through sizzling bulgogi and other meats. The Duluth-based group opened in 2008 as metro Atlanta's first all-you-can-eat Korean barbecue restaurant, and it is a fun choice for groups and birthdays. It opens at 11am and serves until 11pm on weeknights.

Eclipse di Luna serves Spanish tapas favorites such as paella, empanadas, patatas bravas and pitchers of sangria, plus a daily fresh fish special. It stays open until midnight on Friday and Saturday, making it one of Halcyon's later options.

HOBNOB Neighborhood Tavern covers lunch, dinner and weekend brunch with a deep bourbon list and burgers made from a short rib, brisket and chuck blend. Trivia and live entertainment run most nights, and it is a reliable place to watch a game. In November and December it hosts an Elf-themed holiday pop-up bar and Brunch with the Grinch.

Cherry Street Brewpub is the Halcyon outpost of Forsyth County's first brewery, founded in 2012, with a family-friendly brewpub menu and house beers. CT Cantina & Taqueria handles Mexican street food and margaritas, Char Pizzeria turns out artisanal pizza, and Never Enough Thyme is a family-owned spot that started in Alpharetta, known for homemade comfort food.

The Market Hall and quick bites

The Market Hall at the center of the property is home to Gu's Dumplings, the well-known Atlanta Szechuan restaurant for dumplings, noodles and stir-fry (closed Mondays), and Il Bottegone, a little Italian market where you can grab cheeses, cured meats and bread or order a custom charcuterie board to enjoy on the green. For something quick, Halcyon also has Sweet Tuna poke bowls, Playa Bowls, Pita Mediterranean Street Food, Salata, Five Guys, Chick-fil-A, The Original Hot Dog Factory and CT Al Pastor, with BRASH Coffee Roasters for coffee and Kilwins for fudge and ice cream. Osteria Bella, from the Furfaro family, is listed as coming soon.

Shopping

Trader Joe's anchors Halcyon's shopping and is the reason many neighbors stop by every week. Around the Village Green you will find boutiques and specialty shops including Bluemercury for luxury beauty, the women's boutiques Lizard Thicket, Willow Jane and Nina + Leigh, the children's boutique Piggy Jo's, Buff City Soap, RW Design & Exchange for home decor, BBQGuys for grills and outdoor kitchens, and Rise & Wine, a husband-and-wife wine market overlooking the green. Store hours vary, and Bluemercury, for example, closes at 7pm.

Things to do

X-Golf is Halcyon's indoor golf simulator and sports bar, with bays for solo practice, lessons or a group outing, plus a full bar and shareable appetizers. It is open 10am to 10pm Monday through Saturday and until 6pm on Sunday.

The Village Green is the heart of the property, with a sandbox, yard games and room to run for kids. Cornhole and bocce leagues play there during the season, and the green hosts Halcyon's community events.

For a walk or bike ride, a 1.5-mile trail extension that starts near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway connects to Forsyth County's Big Creek Greenway, a 12-foot-wide concrete and boardwalk path through the woods along Big Creek. Dogs are welcome at Halcyon, and Dogtopia offers daycare and boarding on site.

One change to know: the CMX CinéBistro movie theater at Halcyon has closed. Halcyon says it is working on a new concept for the space. For a movie nearby, the Regal at Avalon and AMC at North Point Mall are both a short drive south.

Halcyon holidays 2026

Halcyon's holiday calendar this year includes:

  • HOBNOB's The Elf Upstairs pop-up, starting November 12, with festive cocktails and over-the-top decor
  • Cherry Street Brewville, starting November 23 and running through New Year's Day
  • Big Peach Running Gobble Wobble 5K and Kids Fun Run on Thanksgiving morning, November 26
  • Halcyon Holiday Magic on Saturday, December 5, when Santa officially arrives
  • HOBNOB's Brunch with the Grinch and Chick-fil-A Dinner with Santa (ticketed), both on Saturday, December 12

Holiday hours: Halcyon closes at 5pm on Wednesday, November 25, and is closed on Thanksgiving Day. It closes at 5pm on Christmas Eve, is closed on Christmas Day, closes at 5pm on December 31 and opens 11am to 7pm on New Year's Day. Restaurant holiday hours are set by each restaurant, so check before you go. For more ideas, see our guide to Christmas and holiday events in Alpharetta.

Halcyon vs. Avalon

Both are walkable mixed-use districts with restaurants around a central gathering space, but they feel different. Avalon is bigger on shopping, with national brands from Apple to CHANEL and a 12-screen theater, and it gets busier on weekend nights. Halcyon is more relaxed and neighborhood-scaled, with Trader Joe's, local boutiques, a grassy village green and direct access to the Big Creek Greenway. Both offer free garage parking. If you want a big night out, start at Avalon; for a laid-back dinner, a playground stop and a walk on the trail, pick Halcyon.

Tips for visiting

  • Head straight for the free parking deck instead of circling for a curbside spot. The curbside spaces are limited to 15 minutes.
  • Book ahead for Cattle Shed, Iron Age and Ocean & Acre on Friday and Saturday nights.
  • Sunday center hours end at 6pm, though many restaurants stay open until 9pm.
  • Bring the kids early on weekends while the Village Green is quieter, then grab lunch in the Market Hall.

Frequently asked questions

Is Halcyon in Alpharetta or Forsyth County?

Halcyon has an Alpharetta mailing address (6365 Halcyon Way, Alpharetta, GA 30005), but it is located just over the line in southern Forsyth County, off GA 400 at exit 12.

What are Halcyon's hours?

Halcyon is open Monday through Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday 11am to 6pm. Restaurants and entertainment set their own hours, and many stay open later.

Is parking free at Halcyon?

Yes. Parking is free in the six-level parking deck. There are also 15-minute curbside spots for to-go orders and premium paid spaces in the event lot and along Halcyon Way.

What restaurants are at Halcyon?

Halcyon restaurants include Ocean & Acre, Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar, Iron Age Korean Steak House, Eclipse di Luna, HOBNOB Neighborhood Tavern, Cherry Street Brewpub, CT Cantina & Taqueria, Char Pizzeria, Never Enough Thyme and Gu's Dumplings, plus quick spots like Sweet Tuna, Five Guys and Chick-fil-A.

Is the movie theater at Halcyon still open?

No. CMX CinéBistro at Halcyon has closed. Halcyon says it is working to bring a new concept to the space.

Is Halcyon open on Thanksgiving and Christmas?

No. Halcyon is closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day 2026. It closes early at 5pm on November 25, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.
