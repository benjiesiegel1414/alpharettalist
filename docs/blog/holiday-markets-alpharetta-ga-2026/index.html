Holiday Markets Near Alpharetta, GA (2026): Dates, Hours and Locations

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 22, 20266 min read

Looking for handmade gifts, local makers and a cup of mulled wine this season? Here are the holiday markets in and around Alpharetta for 2026, with dates, hours, addresses and what each one costs (spoiler: almost all of them are free to walk in). Two Saturdays carry most of the action: December 5 and December 12.

Quick answer: 2026 holiday markets near Alpharetta

  • Christkindlmarket Roswell: daily Nov. 20 to Dec. 23 at Bulloch Hall, Roswell. Free.
  • Roswell UMC Holiday Market: Fri. and Sat., Dec. 4 and 5, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Free.
  • Alpharetta Christmas Market: Sat., Dec. 5, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Innovation Academy, downtown. Free.
  • Henderson Commons Holiday Market: Sat., Dec. 5, 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. on North Main Street.
  • Roswell Makers Market: Sat., Dec. 5, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the Roswell Visual Arts Center. Free.
  • Makers Market at Avalon: Sat., Dec. 12, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on the Plaza at Avalon. Free.
  • Holiday Market at the Art Center, Johns Creek: Sat., Dec. 12, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. Free.
  • Holly Jolly Market, Roswell: Sat. and Sun., Dec. 12 and 13 at You42 Studios. Free general admission.
  • Atlanta Christkindl Market, Lawrenceville: Nov. 20 to Dec. 24. Free.

Want the full holiday calendar, not just markets? Our Christmas and holiday events guide has tree lightings, the parade and more, and the events calendar stays updated.

Holiday markets in Alpharetta

Alpharetta Christmas Market (Dec. 5): the big downtown market runs 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 5 at Innovation Academy, 125 Milton Ave. Awesome Alpharetta describes crafts, food, decor, jewelry and more, with free admission. It lines up nicely with Snow Day at Alpharetta City Center the same morning (11 a.m. to 1 p.m. on the Town Green, 2001 Commerce St.), which brings live music, snow and craft stations, also free. Make a morning of it and grab lunch at one of the best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta.

Henderson Commons Holiday Market (Dec. 5): a smaller, shorter market from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Henderson Commons, 735 North Main St. Its listing promises local makers, live music and Santa photos, so it is an easy add-on to the downtown market. For other places to see Santa, check our Santa photos guide.

Makers Market Local Artist Fair at Avalon (Dec. 12): the holiday edition of this monthly fair runs 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on the Plaza at Avalon, 400 Avalon Blvd. Avalon says to expect 30+ local makers selling jewelry (including permanent jewelry), woodwork, candles, apothecary goods, art, crochet, pet accessories and plants. Admission is free and Avalon has free parking. Pair it with a skate at Avalon on Ice, then head downtown for the Alpharetta Winter Parade at 5:30 p.m. the same evening.

Already passed this year: the Artists Holiday Market at the Alpharetta Arts Center was Nov. 21, and Halcyon's one-day Holiday Artists Market was in early November. Halcyon still has Holiday Magic for kids on Dec. 5; see our Halcyon guide. North Point Mall held a Christmas Market Dec. 19 to 21 last year, but we have not seen 2026 dates announced, so check the mall's events page before you go.

Roswell holiday markets (10 to 15 minutes away)

Christkindlmarket Roswell (Nov. 20 to Dec. 23): the closest thing to a German Christmas market in north Fulton, set at historic Bulloch Hall, 180 Bulloch Ave. It is open daily: Monday to Thursday 3 to 8 p.m., Friday and Saturday noon to 9 p.m., and Sunday noon to 8 p.m. (closed Thanksgiving Day, per Roswell365). Expect vendors, gift shops, food and homemade mulled wine. Entry is free, leashed pets are welcome, and photos with Santa run Friday through Sunday from 4 to 7 p.m. with no reservation needed. Parking is limited near the hall, so use the free shuttle from City Hall and church parking lots on Mimosa. It is a short walk from the Roswell Historic District.

Roswell UMC Holiday Market (Dec. 4 and 5): 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. both days at Roswell United Methodist Church, 814 Mimosa Blvd. Free.

Roswell Makers Market (Dec. 5): 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the Roswell Visual Arts Center, 10495 Woodstock Road. It is indoors with free admission and free parking, a good pick if the weather turns.

Holly Jolly Market (Dec. 12 and 13): an indoor market at You42 Studios, 11000 Alpharetta Highway, Roswell. Hours are 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday and noon to 6 p.m. Sunday. General admission is free, and RSVPing in advance gets you a 10% food and drink coupon. Paid extras include a $15 kids' all-day adventure pass, Santa photo sessions ($30 for five minutes) and Brunch with Santa on Saturday from 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. ($40 adults, $25 ages 3 to 12, free for 2 and under). Free shows at The Block Theater include story time with Mrs. Claus and a magic show.

More on the neighborhood in our Roswell area page.

Johns Creek and Lawrenceville

Holiday Market at the Art Center, Johns Creek (Dec. 12): 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. at The Art Center, 6290 Abbotts Bridge Road, Building 700. Atlanta on the Cheap lists 25 juried vendors selling handmade items, and it is free. See our Johns Creek page for nearby spots.

Atlanta Christkindl Market, Lawrenceville (Nov. 20 to Dec. 24): if you want the full German market experience and don't mind the drive, this one sets up at 210 Luckie St. in downtown Lawrenceville. Hours are Wednesday and Thursday 3 to 9 p.m. and Friday through Sunday 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., with extra days Thanksgiving week (Nov. 23 to 25) and Dec. 21 to 23, plus Christmas Eve from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. It is closed Thanksgiving Day. Admission is free, with free market parking at the Gwinnett Justice and Administration Center, 75 Langley Drive.

Tips for market days

  • Stack Dec. 5: start at the Alpharetta Christmas Market, catch Snow Day on the Town Green at 11, then walk up North Main to Henderson Commons before it wraps at 2.
  • Stack Dec. 12: Makers Market at Avalon in the afternoon, then the winter parade downtown at 5:30 p.m.
  • Bring a tote. A reusable bag helps when you are juggling ceramics and candles, and a little cash never hurts at small booths.
  • Christkindlmarket timing: local guides suggest a Monday to Thursday visit after 3 p.m. if you want a lighter crowd, and the free shuttle saves the parking hunt.
  • After dark: Lights on the Loop glows along the Alpha Loop nightly from 5:30 to 11 p.m., Dec. 4 to Jan. 4. More ideas in our Christmas lights guide and things to do page.

Dates and hours come from organizer and event listings and can change with weather, so it is worth a quick check before you head out.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Alpharetta Christmas Market in 2026?

Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Innovation Academy, 125 Milton Ave. in downtown Alpharetta. Admission is free.

When is the holiday Makers Market at Avalon?

Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on the Plaza at Avalon, 400 Avalon Blvd. Avalon says 30+ local makers take part, and it is free to attend.

What are the Christkindlmarket Roswell hours for 2026?

It runs daily Nov. 20 to Dec. 23 at Bulloch Hall: Monday to Thursday 3 to 8 p.m., Friday and Saturday noon to 9 p.m., Sunday noon to 8 p.m. It is closed Thanksgiving Day and admission is free.

Is there parking at the Roswell Christkindlmarket?

Parking near Bulloch Hall is limited, so the market runs a free shuttle during open hours with stops at Roswell City Hall and church parking lots on Mimosa.

Are holiday markets near Alpharetta free?

Most are. The Alpharetta Christmas Market, Makers Market at Avalon, Christkindlmarket Roswell, Johns Creek Art Center market and Atlanta Christkindl Market are all free to enter. Holly Jolly Market is free to enter with paid extras like Santa brunch.
