Moving to Alpharetta, GA: What to Know Before You Move

By Alpharetta List staffPublished October 31, 20268 min read

Alpharetta keeps landing on "best places to live" lists for a reason: a genuinely walkable downtown, the Avalon district, top-tier parks and trails, and one of the strongest suburban job markets in the Southeast. If you're considering a move, here's the practical picture.

The Technology City of the South

Alpharetta's economic identity is tech. The city is home to hundreds of technology companies, including a large share of metro Atlanta's biggest tech employers, a legacy of fiber infrastructure the city began investing in decades ago. Tech Alpharetta, the city's innovation arm, has operated a startup incubator downtown since 2015. Major employers with large Alpharetta operations include Fiserv, ADP, Verizon, Equifax, Jackson Healthcare, Morgan Stanley and UPS Supply Chain Solutions. The practical upshot for movers: many residents work in Alpharetta itself rather than commuting into Atlanta.

The lay of the land

Alpharetta's best-known areas each have a distinct feel:

  • Downtown Alpharetta: the walkable heart, with Main Street shops, the Town Green and the farmers market. Homes within walking distance command a premium.
  • Avalon and the Old Milton corridor: upscale shopping and dining, with luxury apartments and townhomes in and around the district.
  • Windward: the established master-planned community on the north side, built around a lake and golf, convenient to GA 400 exit 11.
  • North Point: the retail corridor along GA 400 exit 10, anchored by the mall, which the city approved for a major mixed-use redevelopment in 2026.
  • Crabapple: the west side near the Milton line, with a small-town market district and newer neighborhoods.

Popular established neighborhoods include Glen Abbey, Park Bridge and Kimball Bridge-area communities, with equestrian and estate properties pushing toward Milton.

Commutes and getting around

GA 400 is the spine of life here, with exits 9 through 12 serving the city. Local trips lean on Old Milton Parkway, Windward Parkway, Haynes Bridge Road and North Point Parkway. Commuters heading into Sandy Springs, Buckhead or Midtown ride GA 400 south, and like anywhere in metro Atlanta, rush hour is real. Inside the city, the Alpha Loop and Big Creek Greenway make bike and foot trips between districts genuinely practical.

ZIP codes and city limits

Alpharetta addresses mainly use 30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022. One quirk worth knowing: 30004 extends into Milton and 30022 extends into Johns Creek, so plenty of "Alpharetta" addresses sit outside the city limits. If city services, taxes or school assignments matter to your decision, verify the actual jurisdiction of any home you're considering.

What living here is actually like

Weekends revolve around the farmers market and Town Green, Avalon, the Greenway, youth sports at Wills Park, and a concert calendar headlined by Ameris Bank Amphitheatre in the warm months. The city runs a packed events slate, from Taste of Alpharetta in May to the Wire & Wood songwriters festival in October and dueling tree lightings downtown and at Avalon each holiday season; our events calendar tracks them all. Dining is a legitimate draw, which is why our restaurant guide runs 15 deep without breaking a sweat.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Alpharetta called the Technology City of the South?

Alpharetta is home to hundreds of technology companies and a large share of metro Atlanta's top tech employers, supported by fiber infrastructure the city began investing in decades ago. Tech Alpharetta, the city's innovation arm, has run a startup incubator downtown since 2015.

What are the main areas of Alpharetta?

The best-known areas are Downtown Alpharetta around Main Street, Avalon on Old Milton Parkway, the Windward area to the north, the North Point corridor along GA 400, and Crabapple on the west side near Milton.

What ZIP codes cover Alpharetta?

Alpharetta addresses mainly use 30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022. Note that 30022 extends into Johns Creek and 30004 extends into Milton, so an Alpharetta mailing address doesn't always mean Alpharetta city limits.

How do people commute from Alpharetta?

GA 400 is the spine of Alpharetta commuting, with exits 9 through 12 serving the city. Many residents work locally at the city's corporate campuses; others ride GA 400 south toward Sandy Springs, Buckhead and Midtown.
