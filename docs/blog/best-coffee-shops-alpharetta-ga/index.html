The Best Coffee Shops in Alpharetta, GA

By Alpharetta List staffPublished October 31, 20265 min read

Alpharetta's coffee scene has grown up fast, and most of it is packed into a few walkable blocks downtown. Whether you want a serious pour-over, a place to work for a few hours or a latte that supports a good cause, here's where locals get their caffeine.

The downtown core

Valor Coffee on Milton Avenue is the one everybody names first: a hometown roaster a block off Main Street with indoor and outdoor seating and consistently excellent espresso. It's the default meeting spot for half of downtown.

Warm Waves Coffee on South Main brings a laid-back, comfortable-seating vibe with a short food menu, and it's one of the easier places downtown to settle in with a laptop.

Brewable on Roswell Street might be the most meaningful cup in the city: it's a nonprofit community cafe that employs adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities. The coffee holds its own, and the mission makes it better.

Boarding Pass Coffee on Commerce Street leans into its airline theme, complete with real Delta seats. Kids and av-geeks love it; the drinks are no gimmick.

The Nest Cafe on Canton Street blurs the line between coffee shop and bakery with lollipop waffles, croissants and beignets.

Beyond downtown

Fuel Coffee on North Main offers a roomy, industrial-style space with breakfast and lunch, a good pick when downtown's shops fill up. In the North Point corridor, Shibam Coffee does handcrafted drinks and light bites, and Vincent Bakery pairs Korean pastries with espresso and boba. At Avalon, Cafe Intermezzo plays a different game entirely: a Paris-style coffeehouse with towering dessert cases and espresso that runs late into the night.

While you're at it

Pair your coffee with gluten- and dairy-free baked goods at Argentis Bakery, gelato at Dolce Spoon, or, if you're up early enough, the legendary trailer-side bagels at BB's Bagels on McFarland Parkway, which routinely sell out before lunch. Frequently asked questions

What is the best coffee shop in Alpharetta?

Valor Coffee on Milton Avenue is the local standard-bearer, a hometown roaster a block off Main Street. Warm Waves, Boarding Pass and Brewable round out a strong downtown coffee scene.

Which Alpharetta coffee shops are good for working?

Valor Coffee and Warm Waves downtown both have comfortable seating, and Fuel Coffee on North Main offers a roomy industrial-style space with a breakfast and lunch menu.

What makes Brewable special?

Brewable is a nonprofit community cafe on Roswell Street that employs adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities, so your latte supports a good cause.
