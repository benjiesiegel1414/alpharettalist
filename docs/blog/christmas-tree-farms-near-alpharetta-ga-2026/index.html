Christmas Tree Farms Near Alpharetta, GA (2026): Cut Your Own and Fresh Fraser Firs

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 24, 20266 min read

Thanksgiving weekend is when most Alpharetta families go get the tree, and you don't have to drive to the mountains to cut your own. There's a family farm right in Milton, a long-running choose-and-cut farm just off GA 400 in Cumming, and a big North Georgia favorite a little farther up 400. Here are the 2026 dates, hours, tree types and Santa times for the best Christmas tree farms and lots near Alpharetta, including which ones are open on Thanksgiving Day.

Quick answer: Christmas tree farms near Alpharetta (2026)

  • Christmas at Little River Farms (Milton): Closest choose-and-cut farm. Tues. to Sun., closed Mondays and Thanksgiving Day. Free Santa visits Sat., Nov. 28 (10 a.m. to 1 p.m.) and Sun., Nov. 29 (1 to 4 p.m.).
  • Scottsdale Farms (Milton): Fresh-cut trees from 2 to 13 feet, including Fraser, Noble and Nordmann fir, plus flocked trees. Mon. to Sat. 8 a.m. to 9 p.m., Sun. 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Closed Thanksgiving Day.
  • Bottoms Christmas Tree Farm (Cumming): More than 5,000 choose-and-cut trees. Open Thanksgiving Day, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Season runs Nov. 21 to Dec. 19. Free Santa photos Nov. 28 and Dec. 5.
  • Kinsey Family Farm (off GA 400, Gainesville address): Choose-and-cut cypress, pre-cut firs in water and living trees. Closed Thanksgiving, open Nov. 27 to Dec. 19. Last tractor run to the fields is 5:30 p.m.

Christmas at Little River Farms (Milton)

If you want the cut-your-own experience without a long drive, this is the one. Little River Farms has been a family farm in Milton since the 1950s, and its Christmas setup includes trees grown right on the property plus classic pre-cut Fraser firs up to 12 feet tall. Field varieties include Green Giants, Blue Ice cypress and Eastern cedar. You pick the tree and the staff cuts it, then they'll wrap it and tie it to your car.

There's also a holiday gift shop (decor, kitchenware, jewelry and kids' clothing), a hot cocoa bar, Christmas-themed cocktails for the grown-ups and outdoor games for kids. Santa visits are free and first come, first served, and you can take photos with your own camera.

  • Address: 14505C Batesville Road, Alpharetta (Milton). Pull into the Wildflower Barn entrance at the corner of Batesville Road and Taylor Road, not the main Little River Farms entrance.
  • Thanksgiving week hours: Tues., Nov. 24, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.; Wed., Nov. 25, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; closed Thurs., Nov. 26; Fri., Nov. 27, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Sat., Nov. 28, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Sun., Nov. 29, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Regular schedule: Open Tuesday through Sunday, closed Mondays. The farm posts weekly hours and inventory updates on its website and Instagram.
  • Santa: Sat., Nov. 28, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., and Sun., Nov. 29, 1 to 4 p.m.

Pair it with lunch in nearby Crabapple or Milton, both a short drive away.

Scottsdale Farms (Milton)

Scottsdale Farms' Enchanted Christmas Tree Forest is the easy, all-weather option. The garden center brings in fresh-cut trees from the mountains of North Carolina and Oregon throughout the season, so the stock keeps turning over. Sizes run from 2 feet to about 13 feet, and varieties include Fraser fir, Noble fir, layered Noble fir, Nordmann fir, Silvertip fir, White Pine and Douglas fir. They also carry white and blue flocked trees, fresh wreaths and garland, and custom greenery arrangements.

  • Address: 15639 Birmingham Hwy., Milton.
  • Hours: Mon. to Sat. 8 a.m. to 9 p.m.; Sun. 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Closed Thanksgiving Day.
  • Bonus: The Cafe at Scottsdale Farms serves coffee and brunch on site, so you can make a morning of it.

The late closing time makes Scottsdale a good weeknight pick for families who can't get out before dark.

Bottoms Christmas Tree Farm (Cumming)

Bottoms is the classic Forsyth County choose-and-cut farm, with more than 5,000 trees in the fields and a barn full of fresh-cut Fraser firs from North Carolina. The farm's motto sums it up: they provide the saw, you provide the saw power. Leashed dogs are welcome, and the farm participates in Trees for Troops.

  • Address: 5880 John Burruss Road, Cumming. From GA 400, take GA 369 west about 5 miles and turn left onto John Burruss Road.
  • Season: Nov. 21 to Dec. 19, 2026.
  • Thanksgiving week: Mon. to Sat., 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., including Thanksgiving Day.
  • After Thanksgiving week: Mon. to Fri., 3 to 6 p.m.; Sat., 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.; closed Sundays.
  • Free Santa photos: Sat., Nov. 28 and Sat., Dec. 5, both 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.
  • Phone: Barn 770-887-2728, mobile 706-429-3173.

Note that Bottoms is closed on Sundays and only open late afternoons on weekdays after Thanksgiving, so Saturday is your best shot at daylight in the fields.

Kinsey Family Farm (off GA 400 north)

Kinsey is the full farm-day experience: a wagon ride out to the fields, marshmallow roasting by the fire, hot chocolate and holiday music in the barn. It has a Gainesville mailing address but sits about 4 miles east of GA 400 on Jot-em Down Road. The farm grows about 800 trees in its fields for families to cut, all tagged with height and price. The field trees this year are Murray cypress and Carolina Sapphire cypress, and the barn holds pre-cut Fraser, Noble, Grand and Douglas firs standing in water. Living Norway and Blue Spruce trees are available if you want to plant one after the holidays.

  • Address: 7140 Jot-em Down Road, Gainesville. From Alpharetta, take GA 400 north about 6 miles past the Exit 17 bridge, turn right on Jot-em Down Road and go 4 miles. The farm warns that some GPS apps send people to a smaller field 2 miles away.
  • Thanksgiving weekend: Wed., Nov. 25, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.; closed Thanksgiving Day; Fri., Nov. 27 through Sun., Nov. 29, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Weekdays Nov. 30 to Dec. 11: Noon to 7 p.m. Weekends Dec. 5 and 6, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.; Dec. 12 and 13, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Final week: Weekdays Dec. 14 to 18, noon to 6:30 p.m.; last day is Sat., Dec. 19, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.
  • Daylight rule: They can't take you into the fields after dark. The last tractor run is currently 5:30 p.m.
  • Phone: 770-887-6028.

Kinsey also runs a Trees for Troops program. The last day to buy a tree for a military family is Dec. 10.

Tips for a smooth tree trip

  • Go early in the season. PickYourOwnChristmasTree.org says supplies look good this year but expects prices to be up again, maybe around 5%, and recommends getting to the farm early.
  • Bring daylight. Cut-your-own fields close at dusk, and in early December that comes before 6 p.m.
  • Measure first. Kinsey's advice is worth repeating: trees in an open field look smaller than they'll be in your living room, so trust the height on the tag.
  • Bring straps and an old blanket. Most farms will net or tie your tree, but it helps to protect the roof of your car.
  • Check social media before you go. Small farms post sell-outs, weather closures and Santa changes on Instagram and Facebook first.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I cut my own Christmas tree near Alpharetta?

The closest choose-and-cut farm is Christmas at Little River Farms in Milton (14505C Batesville Rd.), where you pick a farm-grown tree and staff cuts it. Bottoms Christmas Tree Farm in Cumming has more than 5,000 choose-and-cut trees, and Kinsey Family Farm off GA 400 north grows about 800 field trees for families to cut.

Which Christmas tree farms near Alpharetta are open on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

Bottoms Christmas Tree Farm in Cumming is open on Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Little River Farms, Scottsdale Farms and Kinsey Family Farm are all closed on Thanksgiving Day.

When does Kinsey Family Farm close for the 2026 season?

Kinsey Family Farm's Christmas season runs from Nov. 21 through Sat., Dec. 19, 2026, when it is open 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. It closes Dec. 20 until the new year. The last tractor run to the fields is 5:30 p.m.

Where can I see Santa at a Christmas tree farm near Alpharetta?

Little River Farms in Milton has free Santa visits Sat., Nov. 28 from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. and Sun., Nov. 29 from 1 to 4 p.m. Bottoms Christmas Tree Farm in Cumming has free Santa photos Sat., Nov. 28 and Sat., Dec. 5, both 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.

Where can I buy a fresh-cut Fraser fir in Alpharetta?

Scottsdale Farms in Milton (15639 Birmingham Hwy.) sells fresh-cut trees from 2 to 13 feet, including Fraser, Noble, Nordmann and Douglas fir, plus flocked trees. It is open Mon. to Sat. 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. and Sun. 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Little River Farms, Bottoms and Kinsey also sell pre-cut Fraser firs.
