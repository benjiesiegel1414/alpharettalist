What's Open on Thanksgiving 2026 in Alpharetta, GA: Stores, Groceries and Trash Pickup

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 18, 20266 min read

Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and it's the one day of the year when most of Alpharetta really does shut down. The malls close, Publix and Costco close, the library closes and trash pickup shifts by a day. But a few grocery stores, plenty of restaurants and the Avalon ice rink stay open. Here's what's open and closed in Alpharetta on Thanksgiving, so you can grab that forgotten can of cranberry sauce without driving all over town.

Quick answer: what's open on Thanksgiving in Alpharetta (2026)

  • Closed Thanksgiving Day: North Point Mall, Halcyon shops, Publix, Costco, Aldi, Trader Joe's, Walmart, Target, Home Depot, Lowe's, Best Buy, Kohl's, post offices, banks and the Fulton County library.
  • Open with short hours: Kroger (closing at 4 p.m., most pharmacies closed). Whole Foods and CVS hours vary by store.
  • Open for fun: Avalon on Ice, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., and a long list of restaurants serving Thanksgiving dinner.
  • Trash pickup: Thursday routes move to Friday, Nov. 27, and Friday routes move to Saturday, Nov. 28.
  • Schools: Fulton County Schools is out all week, Monday, Nov. 23 to Friday, Nov. 27.

Grocery stores open on Thanksgiving near Alpharetta

If you need a last-minute ingredient, shop Wednesday if you can. On Thanksgiving Day itself, here's where things stand based on 2026 holiday roundups from Yahoo Shopping and the Cincinnati Enquirer:

  • Publix: closed on Thanksgiving Day.
  • Kroger: open, but stores close at 4 p.m., and most pharmacies are closed. Normal hours return on Friday.
  • Whole Foods: modified hours that vary by store, so call Whole Foods Market at Avalon before you go.
  • Sprouts: listed at 7 a.m. to 2 p.m., though that's based on last year's schedule.
  • Trader Joe's: closed, including Trader Joe's at Halcyon.
  • Aldi and Costco: closed.

Hours can change by location, so a quick call to your store on Wednesday is the safest bet. Want someone else to do the cooking? Our Thanksgiving dinner in Alpharetta guide has heat-and-serve meals and their order deadlines.

Big-box stores and pharmacies

Walmart, Target, Home Depot, Lowe's, Best Buy and Kohl's have all confirmed they'll be closed on Thanksgiving 2026, according to Yahoo Shopping. Walmart reopens on Black Friday. Sephora and Ulta are closed too.

For a prescription or cold medicine, Walgreens says most stores are closed, but its 24-hour locations stay open. CVS hours vary by store and region. Kroger pharmacies are mostly closed, so don't count on them.

Avalon, North Point Mall and Halcyon

North Point Mall lists Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26 as closed on its hours page. Regular hours are 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through Saturday and noon to 7 p.m. Sunday, and the mall notes that department store, restaurant and theater hours may vary.

Halcyon has posted its full Thanksgiving week schedule:

  • Monday and Tuesday, Nov. 23 and 24: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Wednesday, Nov. 25: 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26: closed
  • Friday and Saturday, Nov. 27 and 28: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Sunday, Nov. 29: 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Thanksgiving morning is still busy at Halcyon, though. The Gobble Wobble 5K and Kids Fun Run starts there on Thanksgiving Day. Details are in our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide.

At Avalon, regular hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday and noon to 7 p.m. Sunday, with store hours that may vary. Santa at Avalon is closed on Thanksgiving. Restaurants set their own hours, and a few are serving holiday meals (more on that below). For Black Friday times at all three centers, see our Black Friday 2026 hours guide.

Thanksgiving trash and recycling pickup in Alpharetta

The City of Alpharetta's trash, recycling and yard waste service, run through Republic Services, shifts on only three holidays a year: New Year's Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas. When your pickup day falls on the holiday or later that same week, it moves one day later. For Thanksgiving week, that means:

  • Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday routes: no change.
  • Thursday routes: picked up Friday, Nov. 27.
  • Friday routes: picked up Saturday, Nov. 28.

Carts need to be at the curb by 7 a.m. on your service day. With all that turkey packaging, you might also need extra room. The city sells $2 bag tags for trash that won't fit in your cart; you can buy them at City Hall, 2 Park Plaza. For missed pickups, call Republic Services at 678-407-6216.

Library, post office, banks and schools

  • Alpharetta Library: The Fulton County Library System closes early at 6 p.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 25, and is closed Thursday and Friday, Nov. 26 and 27. The Alpharetta branch at 10 Park Plaza reopens Saturday at 10 a.m. Fulton County offices are also closed both days.
  • Post office: USPS post offices are closed on Thanksgiving, with no regular mail delivery. UPS and FedEx only handle urgent and critical deliveries that day.
  • Banks: Bank branches are closed, but ATMs and online banking still work.
  • Schools: Fulton County Schools is on Thanksgiving Break from Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27.

Restaurants and things to do on Thanksgiving

Not everything is closed. Several Alpharetta restaurants are serving Thanksgiving dinner, including UP on the Roof downtown (11 a.m. to 10 p.m.), Little Alley Steak (noon to 9 p.m.), South City Kitchen Avalon (11 a.m. to 7 p.m.), Eddie V's (11 a.m. to 9 p.m.) and Oak Steakhouse (11 a.m. to 8 p.m.). Book ahead, since Thanksgiving is one of the busiest restaurant days of the year. Menus and prices are in our Thanksgiving dinner guide.

Need to burn off the pie? Avalon on Ice is open 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. all Thanksgiving week, including the holiday. A walk on the Big Creek Greenway or around Wills Park is free. For the rest of the season, see our Christmas and holiday events in Alpharetta and Christmas lights near Alpharetta guides, plus the events calendar and things to do page.

Tips for Thanksgiving week

  • Get errands done early. Halcyon closes at 5 p.m. and the library at 6 p.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 25, so plan returns and pickups for Monday or Tuesday.
  • Check store-level hours. Chains like Kroger, Whole Foods and CVS set hours by location, so call the store you plan to visit.
  • Remember the trash shift. If you're a Thursday or Friday pickup, roll your carts out a day later.
  • Find a local business. Our Alpharetta business directory and restaurant guide list addresses and phone numbers for shops all over town.

Frequently asked questions

Is Publix open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. Publix is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026.

Is Kroger open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Yes, with short hours. Kroger says stores close at 4 p.m. on Thanksgiving Day and most pharmacies are closed. Normal hours return Friday, Nov. 27.

Is Avalon open on Thanksgiving?

Avalon's shops set their own hours and many close, and Santa at Avalon is closed. Avalon on Ice is open 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., and several Avalon restaurants, including South City Kitchen and Eddie V's, serve Thanksgiving meals.

Is North Point Mall open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. North Point Mall lists Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026 as closed. Regular hours resume on Black Friday.

Is trash pickup delayed for Thanksgiving in Alpharetta?

Yes, for some routes. Monday to Wednesday pickups don't change. Thursday routes are collected Friday, Nov. 27, and Friday routes on Saturday, Nov. 28. Carts go to the curb by 7 a.m.

Is the Alpharetta library open over Thanksgiving?

The Fulton County Library System closes at 6 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 25 and is closed Thursday and Friday, Nov. 26 and 27. The Alpharetta branch reopens Saturday at 10 a.m.
