Black Friday 2026 Hours in Alpharetta: Avalon, North Point Mall and Halcyon

By Alpharetta List staffPublished November 13, 20266 min read

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, and Small Business Saturday follows on Nov. 28. If you're shopping in Alpharetta that weekend, your three big stops are Avalon, North Point Mall and Halcyon, plus the independent shops downtown. Here are the hours each one has posted so far, what's closed on Thanksgiving, where to park and the holiday events that make the trip more fun.

Quick answer: Black Friday 2026 hours in Alpharetta

  • Black Friday: Friday, Nov. 27, 2026. Small Business Saturday: Saturday, Nov. 28.
  • North Point Mall: closed Thanksgiving Day. No special Black Friday hours posted yet; regular Friday hours are 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Halcyon: 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 25; closed Thanksgiving Day; 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Black Friday and Small Business Saturday; 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 29.
  • Avalon: no holiday hours posted yet. Regular hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday and noon to 7 p.m. Sunday, and store hours may vary.
  • Avalon on Ice: holiday hours of 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27.
  • Santa at Avalon: closed Thanksgiving; open 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Black Friday and Saturday.
  • Trash pickup: Thursday routes move to Friday, Nov. 27, and Friday routes move to Saturday, Nov. 28.

Department stores, restaurants and the movie theaters at each center often set their own hours, so check a specific store before you head out early.

North Point Mall Black Friday hours

North Point Mall, at 1000 North Point Circle near GA 400, has posted its holiday schedule: closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26. As of Nov. 13, the mall's hours page did not list special Black Friday hours, so plan on its regular Friday and Saturday hours of 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. unless that changes. Sunday hours are noon to 7 p.m.

Later in the season, the mall lists 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Christmas Eve, closed on Christmas Day, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. on New Year's Eve and 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on New Year's Day. The mall notes that department store, restaurant and theater hours may vary, so anchor stores may open earlier on Black Friday. Curious about what's next for the property? Avalon Black Friday hours and holiday fun

Avalon, at 400 Avalon Blvd. off Old Milton Parkway, lists regular hours of 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday and noon to 7 p.m. Sunday, and says store hours may vary. It had not posted Thanksgiving or Black Friday hours as of Nov. 13. We'll update this guide when it does, and you can call Avalon's concierge at 770-765-1000 to check a specific store.

What Avalon has posted is a packed holiday lineup for the shopping weekend:

  • Avalon on Ice: the rink in the Plaza runs Nov. 22 to Jan. 18. Its holiday hours are 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Monday, Nov. 23 through Friday, Nov. 27, then 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Saturday. Tickets are $18 for general admission and $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. Reserve ahead, especially for a holiday weekend. Our Avalon on Ice guide has more tips.
  • Santa at Avalon: Santa's cottage in Palmer Plaza opens Nov. 23 and runs through Dec. 24. It's closed on Thanksgiving, then open 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Friday and Saturday (with breaks from 1 to 2 p.m. and 4 to 5 p.m.) and 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday. Photo packages start at $40 for five digital photos. Book an appointment online or walk up; Avalon says weekdays have shorter waits.
  • The Lighting of Avalon: the season kicks off the Sunday before, Nov. 22, from 2 to 7:30 p.m., with the 40-foot tree lighting at 7 p.m.

Retailers also post in-store promotions on Avalon's Retail Happenings page. Current offers include a Tempur-Pedic sale through Dec. 1, and the page is updated through the season, so check it the week of Thanksgiving. Need a break between stores? See our Avalon neighborhood guide and the best brunch in Alpharetta.

Halcyon Black Friday hours

Halcyon, at 6365 Halcyon Way just over the line in southern Forsyth County (off GA 400 at exit 12, McFarland Parkway), is the one center that has posted its full Thanksgiving week schedule:

  • Monday and Tuesday, Nov. 23 and 24: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Wednesday, Nov. 25: 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26: closed
  • Black Friday, Nov. 27: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Small Business Saturday, Nov. 28: 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Sunday, Nov. 29: 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Halcyon has a countdown and a running list of holiday sales on its Black Friday page. Bluemercury is hosting a holiday shopping party Nov. 19 to 25, and Clean Your Dirty Face has a holiday promotion running Nov. 14 to Jan. 3. Thanksgiving morning also starts at Halcyon for many families: the Gobble Wobble 5K and Kids Fun Run is on Thursday, Nov. 26 (details in our Thanksgiving in Alpharetta guide). For where to eat after, see our Halcyon guide to restaurants, shops and things to do.

Small Business Saturday in downtown Alpharetta

Downtown's shops around City Center, Milton Avenue, Main Street and Canton Street are mostly independent, which makes Downtown Alpharetta the natural stop on Small Business Saturday, Nov. 28. A few to put on your list, with their regular Saturday hours (holiday hours are set by each store, so check before you go):

  • Clothes Horse, 28 Milton Ave.: men's apparel, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.
  • Sis & Moon's, 24 Milton Ave.: boutique gifts and clothing, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Comeback Vinyl, 1 S. Main St.: records and music gifts, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.
  • Alpharetta Outfitters, 79 S. Main St.: outdoor gear and apparel, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Kilwins, 251 Market St.: fudge and chocolates for stocking stuffers, noon to 10 p.m.

November is also Alpharetta Retail Month, so receipts from eligible local shops that weekend can count toward the city's free tote bag. Make a day of it with lunch from our best restaurants in downtown Alpharetta list. The Alpharetta Farmers Market wraps its season on Nov. 14, so it won't be open that Saturday.

More to do Thanksgiving weekend

  • Saturday, Nov. 21: the free Artists Holiday Market at the Alpharetta Arts Center, 10 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and the Season of Celebration tree lighting downtown at City Hall, 5 to 8 p.m. A good early start on gift shopping.
  • Saturday, Nov. 28: the Alpharetta Symphony's holiday concert at Innovation Academy, 3 to 5:30 p.m.
  • Saturday, Dec. 5: the Alpharetta Christmas Market at Innovation Academy, plus Halcyon Holiday Magic welcoming Santa to Halcyon.

Parking, trash day and shopping tips

  • Park free at Avalon. Avalon's parking decks are free, and valet is available. For busy events, Avalon recommends carpooling or ride-share.
  • Use bag check. Avalon's concierge offers bag check, stroller and wheelchair rentals and phone charging, handy on a long shopping day.
  • Know your trash day. Thanksgiving is one of three holidays that shift city pickup. If your normal day is Thursday, it moves to Friday, Nov. 27; Friday routes move to Saturday, Nov. 28. Monday through Wednesday routes don't change. Carts go to the curb by 7 a.m.
  • Go early or late. Halcyon is open until 9 p.m. on Black Friday and Saturday, and Avalon on Ice runs until 10 p.m., so an evening trip can beat the midday crowds.
  • Browse before you go. Our Alpharetta business directory and things to do page list hours and addresses for shops all over town.

Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving. Small Business Saturday is Saturday, Nov. 28.

Is North Point Mall open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. North Point Mall is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026. It had not posted special Black Friday hours as of Nov. 13; regular Friday hours are 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.

What are Halcyon's Black Friday hours?

Halcyon is open 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Black Friday, Nov. 27, and Small Business Saturday, Nov. 28. It closes at 5 p.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 25, is closed on Thanksgiving Day and is open 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday, Nov. 29.

Is Avalon open on Thanksgiving and Black Friday?

Avalon had not posted Thanksgiving or Black Friday hours as of Nov. 13. Its regular hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday and noon to 7 p.m. Sunday, and store hours may vary. Santa at Avalon is closed on Thanksgiving, and Avalon on Ice lists holiday hours of 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. from Nov. 23 to 27.

Is trash picked up on Black Friday in Alpharetta?

Yes. Thanksgiving shifts city pickup by one day for the rest of the week, so Thursday routes are collected Friday, Nov. 27, and Friday routes move to Saturday, Nov. 28.
