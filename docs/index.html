Shopping & dining district

Avalon

Alpharetta's 86-acre upscale mixed-use district: dozens of shops and restaurants along the Boulevard, the Regal Avalon 12 theater, The Hotel at Avalon, and a plaza with bocce, swings and fire pits. Home to a seasonal ice rink, tree lighting and Santa visits each winter.

Old Milton PkwyDetails
City Center district

Downtown Alpharetta

Alpharetta's walkable heart: 50+ shops and restaurants around Main Street, the Town Green with its splash fountain, City Hall, a self-guided arts walk of nearly 20 sculptures, and the Saturday farmers market in season.

AlpharettaDetails
Trail

Big Creek Greenway

Alpharetta's signature trail: about 9 miles of wide paved path along Big Creek for walking, biking and skating, plus mountain-bike trails and a wetland boardwalk spur. Free, dawn to dusk, with access points at Rock Mill, Haynes Bridge, Northpoint and more.

Haynes BridgeDetails
Concert venue

Ameris Bank Amphitheatre

Alpharetta's 12,000-seat outdoor concert venue in a wooded setting at Encore Park, hosting major country, rock and pop tours from spring through fall.

Encore ParkDetails

Avalon and Downtown Alpharetta

Things to do

Two walkable districts, one mile apart

Avalon's shops, restaurants and seasonal ice rink sit one mile from Downtown Alpharetta's Town Green, farmers market and 50+ storefronts, connected by the paved Alpha Loop trail.
