About Borrelli's Salon

Borrelli's Salon has served Alpharetta since 2006 from South Main Street, at the southern edge of downtown. Owner Kelli Christie, a Georgia native, leads a team of stylists who keep up with ongoing education, and the salon carries Redken along with Moroccanoil, Pureology, Color WOW and Leaf & Flower.

Services cover precision haircuts, color from root touch ups to full highlights, formal styling, extensions and custom treatments. A licensed esthetician offers facials, waxing, LED therapy and makeup, and a one hour head spa service combines water therapy, aromatherapy and an upper body massage.

In 2007 the owner added Illuminations by Borrelli's, a division focused on thinning hair and hair loss, with options ranging from toppers to full wigs, including its own silicone wig line. Consultations for that service are booked by phone at a separate number listed on the site.

Borrelli's Salon is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • The salon is open Tuesday through Saturday only, so plan Monday and Sunday needs elsewhere.
  • Hair loss consultations run through Illuminations by Borrelli's at (770) 827-2100.
  • Book the 60 minute head spa if you want a relaxation add on beyond a standard cut or color.
