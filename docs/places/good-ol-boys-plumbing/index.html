About Good Ol'Boys Plumbing

Good Ol'Boys Plumbing has served Alpharetta homes and businesses for more than 15 years, with a focus on plumbing repair and water heater service. The company handles everything from leaky faucets, toilets and hose bibs to burst pipes, leak detection and underground water and main line repairs.

Water heaters are a specialty, whether a unit needs repair or it is time for a replacement or upgrade. Technicians are typically available for same-day or next-day emergency calls, and the company backs its workmanship with a satisfaction guarantee.

Its service area includes Alpharetta, Cumming, Johns Creek, Milton and Roswell. Hours run from 6am to 8pm, and scheduled appointments can be booked by calling (678) 954-7611.
