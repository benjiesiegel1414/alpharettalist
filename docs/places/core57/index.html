About CORE57

CORE57 is a fitness and nutrition coaching studio at 394 Commerce Street in Alpharetta City Center. Founder Kolleen Riddick Losch opened it in late 2018 as an expansion of her earlier business, Core Physique. The name refers to the studio's five steps to success and seven foundations of fitness.

Clients can book one-on-one personal training, semi-private sessions or group classes that mix strength, mobility, cardio, high-intensity circuits, TRX and yoga-inspired work. Fitness assessments, goal setting and nutrition guidance are part of the program, with an emphasis on education and habits that last.

The studio also sells performance wear and hosts community events. Its Alpharetta City Center location puts it within walking distance of downtown restaurants and shops.
