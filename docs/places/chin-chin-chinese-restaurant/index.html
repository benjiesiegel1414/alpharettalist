About Chin Chin Chinese Restaurant

Chin Chin Chinese Restaurant has served Chinese cuisine in metro Atlanta since 1994, and its Alpharetta location is at 270 Rucker Road, Suite 115. The group's other restaurant is on Peachtree Road in Brookhaven. Chin Chin says it received several Creative Loafing awards of excellence between 1998 and 2020.

The menu runs from mild to spicy and light to rich, and diners can watch the chefs cook through a glass enclosed kitchen. Gluten free and MSG free options are available on request. Reviews posted on the restaurant's site mention the fried dumplings, beef curry rolls, sweet and sour chicken and eggplant, along with a good vegetarian selection.

The Alpharetta location is open every day from 11am to 10pm for dine in, and the restaurant also offers online ordering for takeout and delivery.

Chin Chin Chinese Restaurant is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. It is one of 111 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Chinese dining scene.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • Ask for the gluten free or MSG free versions of dishes.
  • Sit where you can watch the cooks work in the glass enclosed kitchen.
  • Order online ahead for faster takeout.
