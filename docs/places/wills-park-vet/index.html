About Wills Park Vet

Wills Park Vet is a general practice animal hospital in Suite 703 at 11770 Haynes Bridge Road. Dr. Drew Keller leads the practice and schedules unhurried appointments so owners have time to ask questions.

Services include wellness and preventive exams, dental care, spay and neuter surgery, in-house diagnostic lab work, a pharmacy and health certificates for pets traveling in the US or abroad. Wellness plans are tailored to each pet's age, breed and lifestyle, and same-day and drop-off appointments help busy owners.

The clinic uses gentle handling to keep visits calm, and it offers free Happy Visits so nervous pets can get to know the staff and building. Appointments and messages go through the Digitail app. Hours are 7:30am to 5:30pm on weekdays.

Wills Park Vet is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more pets and vets in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Haynes Bridge
