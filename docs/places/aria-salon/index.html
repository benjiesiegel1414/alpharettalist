About ARIA Salon

ARIA Salon sits on Milton Avenue in downtown Alpharetta, a short walk from City Center and the Main Street restaurants. The salon focuses on precision haircuts, color transformations and styling, and its stylists and estheticians also handle extensions and waxing.

Products used include Oribe, Aveda, Goldwell and Kerasilk, and the salon sells Aveda products and gift cards online. Its stated values include education, creativity and community, and the website has pages for the team, specials, press and careers.

For weddings, ARIA offers a trial run so the bride can work through the look with her stylist ahead of time, and a bridal package is available. The trial is non-refundable, and the salon asks that the wedding day appointment be secured at checkout after the trial. Evening appointments run until 8pm Tuesday through Thursday, which suits clients who work daytime hours.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • Book a Tuesday to Thursday slot if you need an evening appointment, since those days run until 8pm.
  • For weddings, schedule the trial run early and lock in the wedding day appointment at checkout.
  • Downtown parking fills on weekends, so allow a few extra minutes.
