Clean Juice

Juice Bar · Old Milton Pkwy

Get directionsCall (470) 361-2035Visit website

About Clean Juice

Clean Juice is part of a national organic juice bar franchise, and its Alpharetta store sits at 2500 Old Milton Parkway. The menu centers on organic cold-pressed juices such as Green Glow and blended smoothies, with seasonal flavors, cold brew and juice cleanse programs.

Food runs to quick, wholesome snacks, including toasts like peanut butter with banana and hemp seed. It is a counter-service stop that works for a morning smoothie on the way to work, a post-workout drink or a light lunch.

The store opens at 7am on weekdays and a little later on weekends, which makes it one of the earlier healthy options along this stretch of Old Milton Parkway.

Clean Juice is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Juice dining scene.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.

Good to know

  • It opens at 7am on weekdays, handy for a pre-work smoothie.
  • Order ahead for a quick pickup during the lunch rush.
  • Ask about juice cleanse programs if you want a multi-day plan.
