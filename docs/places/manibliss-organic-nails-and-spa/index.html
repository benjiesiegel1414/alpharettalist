About Manibliss Organic Nails & Spa

ManiBliss Organic Nails & Spa is a nail salon in Suite 103 at 670 North Main Street, with a sister location in Duluth. It centers on organic nail care, careful sanitation and higher-end products.

Manicures and pedicures are the core of the menu, with nail art and services for men as well. The salon also offers eyelash extensions, waxing and brow tinting, so guests can combine several appointments in one visit. It positions itself as affordable luxury, with a clean, relaxing setting. The salon says it uses carefully selected high-end products and serves a varied clientele.

Appointments can be booked ahead, which is the best way to secure a weekend time.

Manibliss Organic Nails & Spa is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.
