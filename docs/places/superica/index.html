About Superica

Ford Fry's Tex-Mex cantina at Avalon, known for chorizo queso fundido, sizzling fajitas and a popular Sunday brunch.

Superica is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. It is one of 106 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Tex-Mex dining scene.
