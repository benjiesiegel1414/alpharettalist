About Superica

Superica is chef Ford Fry's Tex-Mex cantina, and its Avalon location sits on 1st Street in Alpharetta. The menu covers lunch, dinner and weekend brunch, with nachos, tacos, fajitas, enchiladas and margaritas in a colorful dining room styled after the Old West. Fry's Tex-Mex cookbook is sold through the restaurant's website.

Lunch runs on weekdays, dinner starts at 3pm, and brunch is served Saturday and Sunday from 10am to 3pm. Takeaway, online ordering and catering for pickup are all available.

For groups, Superica can seat up to 30 people for dinner in a semi-private space or up to 40 on its covered patio. The patio can also be closed for a private cocktail event for up to 60 guests, and full buyouts are possible through the Rocket Farm Restaurants events team.

Superica is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Tex-Mex dining scene.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.
