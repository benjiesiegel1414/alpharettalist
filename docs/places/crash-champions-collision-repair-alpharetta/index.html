About Crash Champions Collision Repair Alpharetta

Crash Champions Alpharetta is a collision repair center on Hembree Road in north Fulton County, a few minutes from GA-400 and the Windward Parkway corridor. It is one of more than 650 Crash Champions locations nationwide.

The shop handles everyday collision repair and paintless dent repair, and it calibrates Advanced Driver Assistance Systems such as cameras, sensors and radar after a repair. It holds I-CAR Gold Class status. A service advisor reviews the damage, runs a pre scan and writes the preliminary estimate. Typical turnaround runs three to ten business days, depending on structural work and parts.

The shop works directly with most major insurers and offers on-site rental car pickup. Repairs carry a written lifetime guarantee that is honored at any Crash Champions location. Free estimates are available at the counter on weekdays.

Good to know

  • Bring your claim number to speed up the estimate.
