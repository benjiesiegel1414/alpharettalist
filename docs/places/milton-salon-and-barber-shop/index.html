About Milton Salon & Barber Shop

Milton Salon & Barber Shop was opened in October 2017 by Gus Bukhari, who has been cutting hair since 1990 and still works the chair. It sits in the Old Milton Festival plaza, two minutes from GA 400 Exit 10, across Old Milton Parkway from Georgia State University's Alpharetta campus. Look for the red sign over the green awning.

The shop is one room, with barber chairs on one side and a salon on the other. Barbers handle classic cuts, skin and taper fades, gray blending, beard line-ups and straight razor hot towel shaves, while the salon side offers women's cuts and blowouts, color, Brazilian Blowout smoothing and Japanese straightening.

Walk-ins are taken every day, including Sunday, and appointments can be booked online through Fresha. Kids are welcome and first haircuts are common. The full price list is posted on the website, and parking is free right outside the door.

Milton Salon & Barber Shop is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.

Good to know

  • Walk in any day, but book ahead on weekends since they fill up.
  • You can text the shop at the main number with quick questions.
  • Salon treatments like smoothing start with a free consultation to confirm the price.
