About Fuel Coffee

Fuel Coffee is an industrial-style cafe at 800 North Main Street, Suite 110, operating as Fuel Coffee Roaster. It serves breakfast and lunch alongside its coffee, and the website offers online ordering for pickup.

The shop opened around 2021 and is owned by Angela and David Thompson, who also acquired Thirsty Gauge Coffee, a roaster of small-batch 100 percent Arabica beans. In 2022 WhatNow Atlanta reported that Thirsty Gauge was moving its commercial roaster into a suite next door to the cafe. Fuel describes its coffee as micro-roasted to order through the week, and whole-bean blends such as High Octane and Colombian Coupe are sold for brewing at home.

It works for a morning coffee and breakfast stop along North Main Street or a casual weekday lunch meeting, and a bag of locally roasted beans makes an easy gift for coffee drinkers.

Fuel Coffee is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Coffee dining scene.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.
