About Wills Park

Wills Park on Wills Road is Alpharetta's largest park and the city's main hub for recreation. Ball players use seven grass baseball fields, a synthetic turf field and two tee-ball fields, and there are six lighted tennis courts, batting cages, cornhole boards, outdoor fitness equipment and an 18-hole disc golf course spread across the grounds.

For families, there is the Wacky World Playground plus two more playgrounds, walking trails of 1.25 and 2 miles, an arboretum with about two dozen tree species, a community garden and two Little Free Libraries. The Alpharetta Community Center and Recreation Center provide gyms and indoor activity rooms.

Wills Park Pool, renovated in 2018, has a 10-lane competition pool with diving boards and a leisure pool with zero-depth entry, play features and a water slide. The park is also home to the Waggy World dog park, the Wills Park Equestrian Center and the historic Mansell House.

Wills Park is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more parks and trails in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • Bring your own discs for the 18-hole disc golf course.
  • Three of the five picnic pavilions can be rented; the other two are first-come.
  • The pool entrance is at 1815 Old Milton Parkway.
