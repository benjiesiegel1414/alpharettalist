About MaidPro Alpharetta

MaidPro Alpharetta is a locally owned franchise of the national cleaning brand, run by owner Tom Nguyen and operating in the area since 2004. The office is on Branch Drive in Alpharetta and serves the surrounding North Fulton ZIP codes.

Cleanings follow MaidPro's 49-Point Checklist and can be booked weekly, every other week, monthly or as a one-time deep clean. The location also offers move-in and move-out cleaning, cleaning for short-term rental hosts on Airbnb and VRBO, pre- and post-holiday cleaning, and add-on services such as laundry and linen changes.

Cleaners are hired, trained, insured, bonded and employed by MaidPro rather than subcontracted. Gift cards are sold online and emailed within a day of purchase, and the office keeps weekend hours for scheduling questions.

MaidPro Alpharetta is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more home services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.

Good to know

  • Pick monthly service if you keep up with tidying and just want periodic help.
  • Short-term rental hosts can set up turnover cleans between guests.
  • Ask about add-ons like laundry or linen changes when booking a holiday clean.
