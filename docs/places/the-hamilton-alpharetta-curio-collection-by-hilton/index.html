About The Hamilton Alpharetta, Curio Collection by Hilton

The Hamilton is a boutique hotel in Alpharetta's downtown historic district and part of Hilton's Curio Collection. Its design draws on the history of the Western & Atlantic Railway, mixing classical architecture with industrial touches. Travel listings show 119 rooms across four floors.

Rooms include Waldorf Astoria beds, blackout curtains, 50-inch HDTVs, mini-fridges, Frette robes and complimentary Wi-Fi. Standard kings run 290 to 364 square feet, and studio suites have floor-to-ceiling windows. Carrie's, the patio bar, serves lunch, brunch, social hour and dinner, while Fulton's Public House handles daily breakfast. The hotel also has about 3,500 square feet of meeting space and a private lounge.

Check-in starts at 4 PM and check-out is at 11 AM. Valet drop-off is on Old Canton St and pick-up is on Milton Ave, with a public garage across the street at 97 Milton Ave. Pets are not allowed, though service animals are welcome.

The Hamilton Alpharetta, Curio Collection by Hilton is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more things to do in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
