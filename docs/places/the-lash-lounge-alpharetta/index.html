About The Lash Lounge Alpharetta - Old Alabama Square

The Lash Lounge Alpharetta is a lash and brow salon in Old Alabama Square, next to The Fresh Market. The spa-like salon is set up for relaxation, with cozy blankets and calm music, and it draws guests from Roswell and Johns Creek as well as Alpharetta.

Services include custom lash extensions, Purple Light LED lash extensions that cure the bond in under four seconds, lash lifts and tinting, brow lamination, threading and tinting. Every extension appointment begins with a one-on-one consultation to design a look, and regular fills are needed about every two weeks as natural lashes shed.

The Lash Lounge brand was founded by Anna Phillips, and its stylists finish weeks of company training before earning certification. Memberships are available for guests who keep a regular fill schedule.

The Lash Lounge Alpharetta - Old Alabama Square is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.
