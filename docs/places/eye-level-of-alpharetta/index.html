About Eye Level of Alpharetta

Eye Level of Alpharetta has offered supplemental math and English instruction since 2006 from its center on Webb Bridge Way, in the shopping area off Webb Bridge Road. Eye Level is a learning program from a South Korean education company, and this center says it has been named the brand's Franchise of the Year four times.

Programs include Eye Level Math, Eye Level English and Summit of Math, with an emphasis on critical thinking and language arts. Teachers work with students one on one, and each child follows an individualized plan rather than a fixed class pace.

Sessions are held on weekday afternoons and evenings, Tuesday through Friday, plus Saturday mornings. The center is closed Sundays and Mondays and takes a short summer break, so check dates before planning around it.
