About iFLY Indoor Skydiving

iFLY Atlanta is an indoor skydiving center at 2778 Cobb Parkway SE, just off I-75 near Cumberland Mall and the Cobb Galleria Centre. It is about a 30-minute drive from Alpharetta.

Flyers float in a vertical wind tunnel that simulates the feeling of freefall. Every first-time flyer takes a short training session with a certified instructor, who then joins them in the flight chamber. Packages include flight time, training and gear rental, with larger goggles available for people who wear glasses.

Flyers must be at least 3 years old, and there is no upper age limit. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian to sign the waiver. There is also a weight limit, so check the requirements before booking. Plan for about 90 minutes from check-in to finish.
