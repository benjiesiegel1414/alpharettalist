About Pediatric ENT of Atlanta

Pediatric ENT of Atlanta is an ear, nose and throat practice for infants, children and teens, with an Alpharetta office in Suite 275 at 3300 Old Milton Parkway. The practice is a Wellstar affiliate, and the office sits in the medical corridor along Old Milton Parkway near GA 400.

Wellstar's location page lists pediatric otolaryngologists Dr. Erik Bauer, affiliated with Wellstar since 2013, and Dr. Megan Ballard, affiliated since 2024, along with otolaryngologist Dr. Paula Harmon, who holds both DMD and MD degrees and has been affiliated since 2019. Families typically see a pediatric ENT for concerns involving the ears, nose, sinuses and throat, such as recurring ear infections, tonsils and adenoids, or breathing and hearing questions.

Many families arrive through a pediatrician referral, so check whether your insurance requires one before calling. Wellstar's page also provides guidance on appointments and cancellations, directions and insurance for the visit.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.
