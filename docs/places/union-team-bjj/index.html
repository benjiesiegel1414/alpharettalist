About Union Team BJJ

Union Team BJJ is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu academy on Atlanta Highway in north Alpharetta, led by founder and head instructor Grandmaster Ricardo Murgel. He is one of the sport's few 9th-degree grandmasters, has held a judo black belt since 1974, and has trained and competed in Brazil, Europe and Japan.

The academy teaches jiu-jitsu as a martial art rather than sport only, mixing gi and no-gi grappling with takedowns, striking concepts and practical self-defense. Programs cover kids ages 7 to 12, teens and adults, with competition training, private lessons, Modern Taiho Jutsu on Thursdays, and seminars or corporate sessions.

Kids classes start at 5:15pm and teen and adult classes at 6:20pm, Monday through Friday, with open mat on Fridays. New students can book a free trial class.

Union Team BJJ is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more fitness and gyms in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
