About Georgia Obstetrics & Gynecology - Alpharetta

Georgia Obstetrics & Gynecology has cared for women for more than 30 years and is part of Atlanta Women's Health Group. It runs two offices, one in Atlanta and one on Brookside Parkway in Alpharetta, and describes itself as a small, personal practice with access to the larger group's resources.

Services cover pregnancy planning, prenatal care and childbirth, routine gynecology and annual exams, birth control, and care through midlife and menopause. The practice notes that some current patients were delivered by its team and later returned for their own pregnancies. Outpatient surgeries take place at the North Crescent Surgery Center. The practice describes itself as LGBTQIA friendly and sees patients from adolescence through adulthood.

The Alpharetta office is in Suite 103 at 3905 Brookside Parkway, near North Point Parkway and Mansell Road. Fridays end at 1pm, and appointments can be booked online or by phone.

Georgia Obstetrics & Gynecology - Alpharetta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
