About Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar

Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar comes from the same owners as HOBNOB Neighborhood Tavern and takes its inspiration from Charleston. The menu centers on premium steaks and seafood, with charcuterie and cheese boards for the table, and the bar pours more than 200 wines along with wine-infused cocktails.

It is open daily from 11am, until 10pm Tuesday through Saturday and 9pm on Sunday and Monday. Happy hour runs every day from 2:30pm to 6pm, and weekend brunch is served from 11am to 4pm. Weekly specials have included a chef's cut steak dinner for $38 on Mondays, wine flights on Tuesdays and half off select bottles of wine on Wednesdays.

It is the Halcyon restaurant locals pick for anniversaries, birthdays and client dinners, and it fills up on weekend nights. Free parking is in the Halcyon deck.

Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar is at Halcyon, the walkable shopping and dining district at GA 400 exit 12, just north of Alpharetta. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Steakhouse dining scene.

Visiting Halcyon

Halcyon is a 135-acre walkable district at GA 400 exit 12, just over the line in south Forsyth County with an Alpharetta mailing address. Parking is free in the six-level deck, public and family restrooms are in the Market Hall across from the Village Green, and a trail extension near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway connects to the Big Creek Greenway.
