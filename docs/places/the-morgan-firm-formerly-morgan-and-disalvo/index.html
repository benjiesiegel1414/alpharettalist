About The Morgan Firm (formerly Morgan & DiSalvo)

The Morgan Firm is the current name of the Alpharetta estate planning practice long known as Morgan & DiSalvo, P.C. The firm says it is in its 31st year serving clients, and Morgan & DiSalvo was recognized as a Best Lawyers Tier 1 firm in Georgia for Trusts and Estates Law. Richard M. Morgan leads the practice.

Its main areas are estate planning, probate, special needs planning and tax planning. That includes wills, revocable living trusts, financial and healthcare powers of attorney, advance directives, business succession planning, charitable gift planning and trust administration, along with guidance on gift, estate and generation-skipping transfer taxes.

The office is on North Point Parkway, and meetings are set by appointment on weekdays. Clients typically meet with the attorney to review their situation before documents are drafted.

The Morgan Firm (formerly Morgan & DiSalvo) is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more local services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
