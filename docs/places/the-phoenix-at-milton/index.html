About The Phoenix at Milton

The Phoenix at Milton is a Phoenix Senior Living community on Highway 9, just north of Alpharetta in Milton. It offers assisted living, a dedicated memory care neighborhood for residents with Alzheimer's or dementia, and respite stays of up to 60 days.

Services include individualized care plans, daily wellness checks, medication management, therapy, prepared meals, daily housekeeping, personal laundry and complimentary transportation. Memory care floor plans come as companion suites or studios.

Amenities lean social: a bistro and pub, main and private dining rooms, a full-service salon, a game room, a library, an arts and crafts studio, walking trails, an outdoor fireplace, a grill area and a kids' playground for visiting grandchildren. Non-denominational church services are held on site.
