About Hen Mother Cookhouse

Hen Mother Cookhouse is a breakfast, brunch and lunch restaurant at 50 S Main St in downtown Alpharetta. It is the second location of the popular Johns Creek original from chef and owner Soraya Khoury, and it opened here in October 2024. The kitchen is known for homemade comfort food and baked goods served in the morning and early afternoon.

The restaurant fills the historic Jones House, built in 1912 and formerly home to Restaurant Holmes. The Alpharetta dining room is roughly twice the size of the Johns Creek shop, with about 110 seats, which helps with weekend crowds.

Most of the menu mirrors the Johns Creek location, and at opening the Alpharetta kitchen added a few items of its own, including breakfast tacos, oat pancakes and chicken salad and tuna salad sandwiches at lunch. Brunch cocktails have included a rum-based brunch punch and an espresso drink spiked with vodka. The restaurant is closed on Mondays and open Tuesday through Sunday.

Hen Mother Cookhouse is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Brunch dining scene.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • Arrive early on weekends, since brunch is the busiest time.
  • Try the breakfast tacos or oat pancakes, which were added for this location.
  • Plan a visit Tuesday through Sunday, since it is closed Mondays.
