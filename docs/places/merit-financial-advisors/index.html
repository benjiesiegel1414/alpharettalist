About Merit Financial Advisors

Merit Financial Advisors is a wealth management firm founded in 1998 with an office on Lakeview Parkway in Alpharetta. CEO Rick Kent built the firm around a servant-leadership culture, and Merit now reports billions of dollars in advisory, brokerage, retirement plan and ESOP assets.

Services include financial planning, investment management, tax-aware strategies, retirement planning, insurance planning and business planning for owners. Merit puts particular focus on people in transition, such as those going through retirement, divorce, a career change or the loss of a spouse.

Its advisors act as fiduciaries, meaning they are expected to put clients' interests first. Clients also receive monthly market updates and commentary. Office hours run weekdays from 8am to 4:30pm, and new clients can start with a consultation.
