About Kale Me Crazy

Kale Me Crazy is a superfood cafe at 50 Canton Street, Suite 101, in downtown Alpharetta. It is part of a metro Atlanta-founded chain started by Roi Shlomo, whose own change in eating habits in 2012 inspired the concept. The brand now has locations across Georgia as well as in Alabama, Florida and North Carolina.

The menu centers on cold-pressed juices, smoothies made with plant-based milk, acai bowls, power bowls, wraps, salads, flatbreads and avocado toast, with plenty of vegan and gluten-free options and seasonal specials. The company's stated goal is to show that healthy food does not have to give up flavor.

Expect a casual counter-service cafe with outdoor seating, suited to breakfast, a quick lunch or a post-workout snack while walking Canton Street. Online ordering for pickup is available through the chain's website, and the Alpharetta store has its own downloadable menu and email contact for questions.

Kale Me Crazy is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. It is one of 107 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Healthy dining scene.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • Try an acai bowl or vegan wrap, two of the most mentioned items.
  • Order ahead online to skip the line at lunchtime.
  • Ask about gluten-free options if you need them.
