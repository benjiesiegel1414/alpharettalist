About Activate Alpharetta

Activate Alpharetta is an active gaming venue at 3020 Windward Place where teams move through themed game rooms that use the whole body instead of a controller. Rooms include Hoops, Grid, Mega Grid, Climb, Mega Laser, Control, Strike and Scan, with challenges such as a lava floor of colored tiles, a laser maze and a climbing wall with landmines.

Games are built for groups of two to five, and larger groups split into teams and rotate. The activity is physically demanding, with running, jumping, climbing and throwing, and rooms use flashing lights, fog and loud music.

The minimum age is 6, and games can be hard for kids under 10. Hours are noon to 10pm Monday through Thursday, 10am to 11pm on Friday and Saturday, and 10am to 10pm on Sunday.

Activate Alpharetta is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more things to do in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Book online ahead of time; walk-ins are welcome but not guaranteed
  • Solo players cannot play, so bring at least one other person
  • Guests ages 6 to 13 need an adult in the building for the whole session
