About Senergy Fitness

Senergy Fitness's Mansell location sits at the corner of Mansell Road and the Old Alabama Road Connector, next to The Fancy Pantry. It is a full-service training gym rather than a self-guided membership club.

Members choose among one-on-one personal training, semi-private training and group HIIT classes, including a hybrid HIIT format of about 40 minutes. Coaches build customized workout plans and pair them with nutrition help and regular accountability check-ins, and the program is built to work for both beginners and experienced athletes.

First-timers are asked to arrive 10 minutes early to check in and get a tour. Bring athletic clothes, shoes and water. Showers, towels and mats are provided, so it is easy to head straight to work afterward, and there is parking on site.

Senergy Fitness is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more fitness and gyms in our Alpharetta directory.
