About Original ChopShop

Original ChopShop is a better-for-you counter-service restaurant, and its Alpharetta store at 10920 Haynes Bridge Road opened on October 9, 2024. It was the company's third Georgia location and 26th overall.

The kitchen uses whole ingredients across breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner, so you can stop in for an acai bowl in the morning or a bowl or salad later in the day. Menu picks named by the company include the teriyaki chicken bowl, the Citrus Thai Chop salad and the acai bowl, and the lineup also includes wraps, sandwiches and protein shakes.

You can dine in, take food to go or order through the app and website, and catering is available for offices and events. The Chops loyalty program rewards repeat visits.

Original ChopShop is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Healthy dining scene.

Visiting Haynes Bridge

Haynes Bridge Road crosses GA 400 in south Alpharetta, close to the Big Creek Greenway and Old Milton Parkway. Businesses here are mostly in shopping centers and office parks with their own parking.
