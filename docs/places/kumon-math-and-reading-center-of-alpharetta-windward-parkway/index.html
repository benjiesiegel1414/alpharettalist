About Kumon Math and Reading Center of Alpharetta - Windward Parkway

This Kumon center on North Main Street serves families around Windward Parkway and north Alpharetta. Owner and center director Elia Momin has worked with Kumon for more than seven years, starting as a manager at another Atlanta-area center before taking over this location.

Kumon offers two programs, Math and Reading. Students work through individualized worksheets at their own pace, starting at a level set by a placement assessment, and build skills step by step with daily practice. The reading program uses passages drawn from stories, and the director says instruction blends visual, auditory and hands-on methods.

New families can book a free placement assessment with no obligation to enroll. The center posts hours most days of the week, including Saturdays, but some days show more than one time block, so call to confirm when class sessions are held.

Kumon Math and Reading Center of Alpharetta - Windward Parkway is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.

Good to know

  • Book the free placement assessment first.
  • Call to confirm class session times before your first visit.
  • Plan for short daily worksheets at home between visits.
