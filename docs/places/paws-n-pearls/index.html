About Paws N Pearls

Paws N Pearls is an all-breed dog grooming salon in the Village at Crooked Creek shopping center on Atlanta Highway (State Route 9). Owner Missy Crane has groomed both pet and show dogs for decades, including 13 years at Veterinary Medical Center in Alpharetta.

Full grooming includes a bath with a shampoo matched to the coat, a haircut in the style you choose, paw pad trimming, a sanitary trim, nail trim and Dremel, ear cleaning and anal gland expression. A shorter mini groom is also available. Hypoallergenic and flea and tick shampoos can be requested. The salon's show background shows in breed-specific work such as hand stripping terriers and scissoring poodles, Bichons and Shih Tzus.

Grooming is by appointment, so call ahead to book. From GA 400, take the McFarland Road exit and head to Atlanta Highway; the salon is about 0.4 miles down on the right.

Paws N Pearls is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more pets and vets in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
