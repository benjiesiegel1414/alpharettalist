About Alpha Medical Clinic

Alpha Medical Clinic is a full-service internal medicine practice on South Main Street, just south of downtown Alpharetta. Established in 1995, it focuses on primary care for adults and geriatric patients across Alpharetta and North Atlanta, and lists itself as a Wellstar partner.

The clinic is led by board-certified internists Dr. Dhiraj Patel and Dr. Neil Patel. Visits cover annual physicals, chronic conditions such as diabetes, heart health and thyroid disorders, acute illness, lab testing, allergy testing, weight management, gastrointestinal concerns, and support for anxiety and depression. The practice also performs immigration physicals.

Same-day sick appointments may be available, so it is worth calling in the morning. The clinic accepts major commercial plans and Medicare and offers self-pay rates for uninsured patients. A patient portal and texting help patients stay in touch with the office between visits.

Alpha Medical Clinic is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
