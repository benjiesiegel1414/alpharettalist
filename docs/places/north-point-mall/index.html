About North Point Mall

North Point Mall opened on October 20, 1993, and remains Alpharetta's regional enclosed mall, with roughly 1.3 million square feet of space near GA 400. Mall hours are Monday through Saturday 11am to 8pm and Sunday noon to 7pm, though department stores, restaurants and the AMC theater keep their own schedules. Anchors are Macy's, Dillard's, JCPenney and Von Maur, joined by stores such as H&M, Sephora, Aerie, American Eagle and Ann Taylor and the AMC North Point Mall 12 cinema.

Holiday hours for 2026: the mall is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, open 9am to 6pm on Christmas Eve, and closed on Christmas Day. Santa photos at the North Point Pole run from November 21 through December 24, and the mall's holiday kickoff is November 21.

Big changes are planned. In August 2026 the Alpharetta City Council voted 4 to 3 to approve the redevelopment of up to 100 acres of the site by Jamestown, the developer of Ponce City Market, with New York Life and The Machete Group. The plan centers on a 20,000-seat arena meant to host an NHL franchise, surrounded by an entertainment district and tied to a transit station on the GA 400 express lanes. Much of the deal depends on the NHL committing to an Alpharetta team by August 2031, and the mall remains open in the meantime.

North Point Mall is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more shopping in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
