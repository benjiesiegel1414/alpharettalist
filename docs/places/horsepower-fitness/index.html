About Horsepower Fitness

Horsepower Fitness is a boutique training gym in Suite 120 at 12640 Crabapple Road, a short walk from Milton City Hall in the Crabapple area. It focuses on small-group and one-on-one personal training rather than open gym memberships.

Sessions blend strength and conditioning with functional and free-weight training, and the gym also offers HYROX race preparation and nutrition coaching. That mix makes it a fit both for people building general strength and for those training toward a specific event.

Weekday sessions run from 5:30am into the early evening, with a short Saturday morning window, and the gym is closed Sundays. Reservations are required. Amenities include showers, a changing room, towels and on-site parking.

Horsepower Fitness is in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line. Browse more fitness and gyms in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Crabapple

Crabapple sits on Alpharetta's west side near the Milton line, known for its rolling, horse-country feel, larger lots and the historic Crabapple crossroads just over the line in Milton. Most businesses here are in small shopping centers with easy parking.

Good to know

  • Book sessions in advance, since walk-ins are not the model
  • Ask about HYROX training if you are preparing for a race
  • Showers and towels make early sessions before work practical
