Entertainment · Avalon

About Fairway Social

Fairway Social on South Main Street is an entertainment venue built around indoor sports simulators. Groups can play world-class golf courses or a variety of other games, from target challenges to zombie dodgeball, in bays that suit all ages and skill levels.

It also works as a restaurant and bar, with a full-service kitchen serving chef-driven menus and signature cocktails, plus live music and live sports on screens throughout the venue. It comes from the same group behind Roaring Social in downtown Alpharetta.

Walk-ins are welcome, but you can also book a bay online. On arrival, check in with guest services and staff will walk you to your bay with tips and safety basics. Hours run Monday through Thursday 4pm to 10pm, Friday and Saturday 11am to 11pm, and Sunday 11am to 9pm.

Fairway Social is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. Browse more things to do in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.
