About Johns Creek & Alpharetta Counseling

Johns Creek & Alpharetta Counseling, also called JCACounseling, is based in Suite 280 at 5755 North Point Parkway. Licensed therapists see children, teens, adults and couples, and the practice also provides professional supervision for counselors.

Services include individual counseling, couples therapy, play therapy for children and EMDR. Office visits take place in Alpharetta, and online therapy is offered for clients in Georgia, California, Florida and South Carolina.

When you call, you speak with a therapist who helps match you with a counselor trained in what you need. The practice says it will point you to another resource if it is not the right fit. Evening, weekend and weekday appointments are available.

Johns Creek & Alpharetta Counseling is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
