About Amorance

Amorance is a 55+ active adult rental community on Commerce Street in downtown Alpharetta, managed by Greystar. It has 249 one and two bedroom apartments and townhomes and sits beside a five-acre public park and town green, a short walk from City Center, Alpharetta's restaurants and the Alpha Loop.

Homes include wood-style floors, granite counters, kitchen islands, stainless steel appliances, walk-in closets, a washer and dryer, and a private balcony or patio. Shared spaces include a rooftop club room with an outdoor patio, a clubhouse with a catering kitchen, a fireside lounge, a fitness center, a yoga and spin room, a pool with tanning shelves and a courtyard with a grill and fire pit.

Amorance is one of the most walkable senior options in town, with a Walk Score around 77. It is pet friendly. 55places has listed rents starting around $2,600 a month including mandatory fees, so call for current specials.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
