About Alpha Loop

The Alpha Loop is Alpharetta's growing network of urban multi-use trails, designed to link Downtown, Avalon, the North Point area and Northwinds. The paths are 12 feet wide and open to walkers, runners and cyclists.

The best-known segment connects Downtown Alpharetta and Avalon in about a mile, so it is easy to walk between dinner downtown and shopping at Avalon. Another section of roughly a mile, from Old Milton Parkway to Northwinds Parkway, opened on June 1, 2024. It passes through woods and along streams, ducks under Westside Parkway in a tunnel and includes an overlook bridge.

Permanent outdoor sculptures line both trails, adding public art to the route, and the loop continues to grow as the city builds new sections.

Alpha Loop is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more parks and trails in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

  • Look for the outdoor sculptures placed along the trail.
