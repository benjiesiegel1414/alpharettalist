About Comeback Vinyl

Comeback Vinyl is an independent record store at 1 South Main Street in historic downtown Alpharetta, located next to the company's warehouse and offices for its national online shop. The brick-and-mortar store stocks a wide selection of new vinyl across many genres.

A built-in listening station lets you hear most records before you buy, and staff aim for a welcoming, no-judgment feel whether you are a longtime collector or just bought your first turntable. Online orders can be picked up at the store for free.

The shop also buys used LPs in playable condition, mostly music from the 1960s onward. Drop-offs need no appointment, offers usually arrive within a week, and store credit pays 10 to 15 percent more. Free street and deck parking is nearby.

Comeback Vinyl is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more shopping in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
