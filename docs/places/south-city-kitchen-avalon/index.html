About South City Kitchen Avalon

South City Kitchen Avalon is the Alpharetta branch of the long-running South City Kitchen concept, which is known for elevated Southern cooking. The Avalon restaurant sits at The Hotel at Avalon on Avalon Boulevard and serves breakfast, lunch and dinner on weekdays plus brunch on Saturday and Sunday.

The menu pairs familiar South City Kitchen signatures with seasonal, chef-driven Southern dishes that Avalon's directory describes as exclusive to this location. Expect a polished, sit-down setting that works for a hotel breakfast meeting, a business lunch or a weekend brunch with family.

Avalon's directory notes that the bar stays open later on weekends. Because it is part of the hotel, the restaurant is an easy choice for visitors staying at Avalon as well as locals who want a Southern meal before shopping or a show nearby.

South City Kitchen Avalon is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. It is one of 103 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Southern dining scene.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.

Good to know

  • Book brunch ahead on weekends, when it is one of Avalon's busier meals.

  • Park in any of Avalon's free garages and walk over to the hotel.
