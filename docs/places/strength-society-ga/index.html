About Strength Society GA

Strength Society is a boutique small-group strength gym in Suite A100 at 970 North Point Drive. It was founded by former NFL player Jonotthan Harrison, and its motto is There's Strength in Numbers, reflecting a team-style training culture.

Classes are capped at 12 members with two coaches each, which keeps form coaching close. Programming rotates through endurance, hypertrophy and power phases and mixes functional training, free weights, circuits and conditioning. Every movement can be scaled, so beginners and experienced lifters can share a class, and one-on-one coaching is available for people who want individual programming.

Classes run from 5:30am on weekdays, with midday and evening options on some days and a couple of Saturday morning sessions. The gym is closed Sundays. Booking ahead is required, and the facility has showers, parking and accessible restrooms.
