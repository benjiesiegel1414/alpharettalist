About Urban Escape Games

Locally designed escape rooms near North Point where teams have one hour to find clues, crack codes and solve puzzles to get out of a themed room. It bills itself as the first escape game company in Georgia and is popular for family outings, date nights and corporate team building, with debriefs offered for company groups.

Urban Escape Games is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more things to do in our Alpharetta directory.
