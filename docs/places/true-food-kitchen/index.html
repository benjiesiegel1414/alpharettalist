About True Food Kitchen

True Food Kitchen at Avalon serves a seasonal, whole-food menu based on the anti-inflammatory food pyramid developed by founder Dr. Andrew Weil. Bowls, salads and lean proteins sit alongside vegan-friendly and gluten-friendly choices.

The restaurant draws weekday lunch crowds from nearby offices, weekend brunch diners and family groups. A full bar pours craft cocktails and organic wines, and house-made drinks take the place of regular sodas on the menu.

The Alpharetta location has an outdoor patio with seating for more than 120 guests. It is at 2140 Avalon Boulevard in the heart of the development, with valet and self-parking at Avalon. Weekend brunch starts at 10:30am. Reservations are available on OpenTable, and catering, private dining and online ordering are offered.

True Food Kitchen is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. It is one of 111 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Healthy dining scene.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.

Good to know

  • Reserve on OpenTable for weekend brunch.
  • Ask for the patio, which seats more than 120.
  • Use Avalon's valet or self-parking on site.
