About Iron Age Korean Steak House

Iron Age Korean Steak House at Halcyon offers a do it yourself Korean barbecue experience in the Halcyon development off McGinnis Ferry Road. Iron Age opened in 2008 as metro Atlanta's first all you can eat Korean barbecue restaurant, and the Duluth based brand also has locations in Sandy Springs, Kennesaw and Toco Hills, plus Maryland, Virginia and Thailand.

Guests cook beef bulgogi, other marinated meats and vegetables at built in tabletop grills, and staff help with cooking times and food or allergy questions. Side dishes are refilled when you set an empty dish at the edge of the table.

Seating is first come, first served and guests are asked to keep to a two hour dining time. Reservations are taken only for parties of 8 or more, booked at least 6 hours ahead, and tables are not guaranteed on weekends. It works well for birthdays and group dinners.

Iron Age Korean Steak House is a short drive from Alpharetta in Alpharetta. It is one of 111 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Korean BBQ dining scene.
