About Spectrum Dermatology of Atlanta

Spectrum Dermatology of Atlanta opened in Alpharetta in 2021 in The Park at Windward Concourse office park. It was founded by board-certified dermatologists Dr. Susan Spitzler and Dr. Emma Murad, both trained at Emory, and the practice lists itself as a member of the Emory Healthcare Network.

Services span the full range its name suggests: general dermatology for acne, eczema, psoriasis, rosacea, hair loss and moles; skin cancer screening and treatment; XTRAC excimer laser for psoriasis and vitiligo; and cosmetic work such as Botox, fillers, PRP microneedling, sclerotherapy for spider veins and laser treatments. An aesthetic team handles facials, Hydrafacial, chemical peels and dermaplaning. The doctors see both children and adults.

The office is in the 1725 building, first suite on the right inside the double doors, with reserved patient parking out front. It sits just off Windward Parkway near McGinnis Ferry Road.

Spectrum Dermatology of Atlanta is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
