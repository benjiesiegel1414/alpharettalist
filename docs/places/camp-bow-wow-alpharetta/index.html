About Camp Bow Wow Alpharetta

Camp Bow Wow Alpharetta offers dog daycare, overnight boarding, grooming and enrichment from Suite E at 1755 Grassland Parkway in the Windward area. Dogs play in indoor and outdoor yards, and owners can check in on them through live webcams on a computer or phone.

Boarding guests sleep in individual cabins with a cot, a fleece blanket and a nightly treat. Giving medication carries no extra charge, and neither does daycare play during a boarding stay. The Camp app lets owners manage camper profiles, request reservations and view the webcams.

Every new dog has to pass an interview and meet entrance requirements before its first visit. The center opens at 6:45am on weekdays and 7am on weekends, closing at 7pm every day.

Camp Bow Wow Alpharetta is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more pets and vets in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
