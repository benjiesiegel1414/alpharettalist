About Wellstar North Fulton Hospital

Wellstar North Fulton Medical Center, long known as North Fulton Hospital, is the full-service hospital closest to much of Alpharetta. It sits at 3000 Hospital Boulevard in Roswell, just south of the city line, and its emergency department is open 24 hours.

The hospital is a Level II Trauma Center, a Level II Emergency Cardiac Care Center and a Joint Commission Comprehensive Stroke Center. It is also an NAEC accredited Level 3 Comprehensive Epilepsy Center. Wellstar lists emergency check-in, imaging and a range of inpatient and specialty services for the north Fulton area.

Administrative departments such as medical records, imaging scheduling, patient billing and patient experience keep weekday hours from 8am to 5pm, each with its own phone line. The main hospital number is (770) 751-2500.

Wellstar North Fulton Hospital is a short drive from Alpharetta in Roswell. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.
