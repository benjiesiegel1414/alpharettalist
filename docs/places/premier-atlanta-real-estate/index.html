About Premier Atlanta Real Estate

Premier Atlanta Real Estate is a real estate business affiliated with Keller Williams Realty Consultants. Its website lists the Roswell Keller Williams office on Mansell Road as its office, and it works with buyers and sellers across North Atlanta, including Alpharetta, Milton, Johns Creek, Roswell, Sandy Springs, Buckhead and Chastain Park.

Directory information describes full-service buyer and seller representation, investment property analysis and relocation help. The website includes a home search tool that pulls from FMLS listings and lets shoppers filter by price, features and keywords or draw a custom area on a map, which is useful when you are comparing neighborhoods or school zones across several cities.

For families relocating to North Fulton, its coverage of both in-town Atlanta and the northern suburbs means one agent can help weigh options across the region. The website does not name individual agents, so ask who will handle your search or sale, and confirm contact details before arranging a meeting.

Premier Atlanta Real Estate is a short drive from Alpharetta in Roswell. Browse more real estate in our Alpharetta directory.
