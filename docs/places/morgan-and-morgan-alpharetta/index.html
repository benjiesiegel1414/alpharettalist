About Morgan & Morgan Alpharetta

Morgan & Morgan's Alpharetta office is on South Main Street in historic downtown, close to Alpharetta City Center. The national firm describes itself as America's largest injury law firm, with more than 1,100 lawyers and offices in all 50 states and Washington, D.C.

The Alpharetta office's practice pages cover car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian and rideshare accidents, slip and falls, negligent security, construction accidents, brain and spinal cord injuries, wrongful death, medical malpractice, nursing home abuse and product liability. The firm also handles workers' compensation, Social Security disability, employment and insurance claim disputes.

The firm says it is available 24/7 by phone and online. Several attorneys are listed for the Alpharetta office, so ask which lawyer will handle your matter when you call to set up a meeting.

Morgan & Morgan Alpharetta is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more local services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
