About Play Street Museum Alpharetta

Play Street Museum Alpharetta is a small-scale, interactive children's museum at 11770 Haynes Bridge Road, Suites 701 and 702. It is designed like a miniature city for children ages 1 to 8, who can take orders at a pretend cafe, get a checkup at the doctor's office, ride the play fire truck or build with blocks.

Parents can play alongside their kids or sit back while they explore. Playtime is by reservation on weekdays, with five 90-minute sessions each day starting at 8:30am, 10:30am, 12:30pm, 2:30pm and 4:30pm. Weekend playtimes and special events are posted separately.

The museum can also be booked for private birthday parties, playdates and school field trips. Older and younger siblings get in free with a paying child, though older kids are asked to play calmly.

Play Street Museum Alpharetta is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Haynes Bridge

Haynes Bridge Road crosses GA 400 in south Alpharetta, close to the Big Creek Greenway and Old Milton Parkway. Businesses here are mostly in shopping centers and office parks with their own parking.

Good to know

  • Reserve a weekday session online before you go
  • Food is not allowed in the play area, but drinks are welcome
  • Check the events calendar for weekend playtimes
