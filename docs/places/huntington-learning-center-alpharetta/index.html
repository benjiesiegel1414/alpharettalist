About Huntington Learning Center Alpharetta

Huntington Learning Center's Alpharetta location is on Highway 9 North in a shopping center near Windward Parkway. The center offers individualized tutoring for students in kindergarten through 12th grade, both in person and online.

Tutoring covers math, reading, writing, science and study skills, with dedicated programs for students with ADHD and a summer tutoring option. Test prep is a large part of the program, including ACT, SAT, PSAT, AP exams, state tests and private school admissions tests such as the SSAT, ISEE and HSPT, as well as ASVAB and GED preparation. The center also partners with local schools on high-dosage tutoring.

Hours run late into the evening Monday through Thursday, the center is closed on Fridays, and Saturday sessions are held in the morning. Huntington notes that seasonal hours can differ.

Huntington Learning Center Alpharetta is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
