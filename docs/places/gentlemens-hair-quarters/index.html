About Gentlemen's Hair Quarters

Gentlemen's Hair Quarters is a family-owned barber shop that has operated in Alpharetta for more than 15 years, located on Atlanta Highway. Its master barbers work with all hair types and textures, and every service starts with a short consultation so the cut fits the client.

Services include fades and tapers, precision scissor cuts with wash and style, classic gentleman cuts, kids' cuts including first haircuts, beard trims and lineups, straight razor hot towel shaves, style consultations and hair health advice on products and care.

Walk-ins are welcome, though appointments are recommended to secure a preferred time. The shop asks clients to call for current prices and to ask about kids and student pricing. There is plenty of parking in the lot, and the shop serves customers from Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Roswell and nearby areas.

Gentlemen's Hair Quarters is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Ask about kids and student pricing when you call.
  • Saturday hours end at 3pm, so book early in the day.
