About Teddy's Jiu Jitsu Academy

Teddy's Jiu Jitsu Academy is a family-run Brazilian jiu-jitsu school on Holcomb Bridge Road, serving Alpharetta, Roswell, Johns Creek, Peachtree Corners and Norcross. Co-owner and head instructor Jerad "Teddy" Wheeler started grappling at Georgia State University and trained under Master Roberto Traven, and his background includes competition and law enforcement. He runs the academy with his wife, Kelly Wheeler, a coach who placed third at the 2019 IBJJF Masters World Championship.

Kids classes are split into ages 6 to 8 and 9 and up, and adult classes welcome beginners with no martial arts experience. Training focuses on coached practice and partner drills that explain how and why techniques work.

New students start with a phone consultation, then schedule a trial class. Classes run Tuesday through Thursday evenings and Saturday mornings.
