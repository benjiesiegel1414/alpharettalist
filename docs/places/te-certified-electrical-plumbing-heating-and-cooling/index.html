About TE Certified Electrical, Plumbing, Heating & Cooling

TE Certified Electrical, Plumbing, Heating & Cooling has served the Atlanta area since 2003. It runs offices in Roswell, Lawrenceville, Kennesaw and Jonesboro, and the Roswell office on Old Dogwood Road is the one that covers Alpharetta.

The company started with electrical work and still leads with it: repairs to lights, outlets, breakers and GFCI devices, installations, and whole house generators. It also employs licensed plumbers and heating and air technicians, and it lists its state electrical, low voltage, HVAC and master plumber license numbers on its contact page.

TE Certified offers same day service with three hour arrival windows and a courtesy call 30 minutes before the technician arrives. Trucks are stocked so many jobs can be finished on the first visit. The office accepts Zelle, check and major credit cards.
