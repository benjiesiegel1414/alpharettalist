About Sovi Salon

Sovi Salon is a full-service salon that opened in 2010 and works by appointment from a suite on Douglas Road, on the Alpharetta and Johns Creek side of North Fulton. The team focuses on cuts and color that suit each client and are realistic to maintain day to day.

The menu goes beyond hair. Along with haircuts, color and highlights and smoothing treatments, the salon offers waxing, threading, facials, and bridal and makeup services, which makes it a practical stop for event preparation as well as routine upkeep.

Appointments are taken Tuesday through Saturday, and the salon is closed Sunday and Monday. A cancellation policy is posted on the website, so review it before booking. The space is described as bright and modern, and the salon posts recent work on Instagram if you want to see examples of color and styling before your visit.
