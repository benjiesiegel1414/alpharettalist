About Valvoline Instant Oil Change - Cotton Creek

The Cotton Creek Valvoline Instant Oil Change is on Old Milton Parkway about 1.5 miles east of Avalon, near the Brookside Parkway intersection and next to Dunkin'. It serves Alpharetta and nearby neighborhoods toward Johns Creek.

The main service is a stay-in-your-car full service oil change that takes about 15 minutes. Other services include air filters, battery replacement, wiper blades, light bulbs, serpentine belts, tire rotation, radiator, transmission and differential service, fuel system cleaning, air conditioning, state inspections, fleet service and electric vehicle services.

No appointment is needed for any service, so you can simply pull in. Unlike many shops, this location is open seven days a week, including Sunday afternoons. Coupons for new and returning customers are posted on the location page.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.
