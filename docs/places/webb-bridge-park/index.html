About Webb Bridge Park

Webb Bridge Park covers 109 acres off Kimball Bridge Road and mixes busy sports fields with quieter green space. It has three grass soccer fields, a synthetic turf multi-purpose field, four baseball fields and four tennis courts, along with concession stands for game days.

Away from the fields, a 1.5-mile walking trail, a lake and an arboretum with more than 20 tree specimens give the park a natural side. The arboretum represents upland hardwood forest, conifers and trees typical of moist ravines and bottomland.

Kids gravitate to the Webb Zone Playground, and there is outdoor fitness equipment, a Play Me Again piano and a Little Free Library. Dog owners can use the on-site dog park, which has separate areas for large and small dogs. Three picnic pavilions are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

Webb Bridge Park is along McGinnis Ferry Road on Alpharetta's east side, near the Johns Creek line. Browse more parks and trails in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting McGinnis Ferry

McGinnis Ferry Road runs along Alpharetta's northeast edge near the Johns Creek and Forsyth County lines. It's a busy commuter route with shopping centers, medical offices and neighborhoods on both sides.

Good to know

  • Picnic pavilions are first-come, first-served, so arrive early on weekends.
  • Use the separate small-dog area if your pup is shy around big dogs.
  • Walk the 1.5-mile trail past the lake and arboretum.
