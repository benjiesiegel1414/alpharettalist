About Smokejack Southern Grill & BBQ

Smokejack Southern Grill & BBQ is a barbecue restaurant at 29 S Main St in downtown Alpharetta, in a historic brick building on Main Street. It is known for Southern barbecue such as hand-pulled pork, ribs and beef burnt ends.

Its downtown location puts it within walking distance of City Center and the other Main Street restaurants. Check the restaurant's website or call ahead to confirm current hours and menu before visiting.

Smokejack Southern Grill & BBQ is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. It is one of 103 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's BBQ dining scene.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
