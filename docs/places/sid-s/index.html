About Sid's

Sid's Pizza is a family-run pizza joint from husband and wife Stan and Sharon Kurnett, whose original restaurant in Suwanee has a surfer vibe that nods to Stan's Southern California roots. The Alpharetta location at 11770 Haynes Bridge Road, Suite 301, in the Alpharetta Crossing shopping center, is their second, set in the former Mellow Mushroom space with a 1970s basement feel.

Sicilian-style pizza leads the menu, alongside bowls, sandwiches, burgers, salads and wings. It is a relaxed, family-friendly spot for a casual dinner or a game night with friends, and catering is available for events.

The brand marked 20 years in business and has begun franchising. Takeout and delivery are available for nights at home.

Sid's is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. It is one of 107 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Pizza dining scene.

Visiting Haynes Bridge

Haynes Bridge Road crosses GA 400 in south Alpharetta, close to the Big Creek Greenway and Old Milton Parkway. Businesses here are mostly in shopping centers and office parks with their own parking.

Good to know

  • Try the Sicilian-style pizza, the house specialty.
  • Order catering for parties and office events.
  • Parking is easy in the Alpharetta Crossing shopping center lot.
