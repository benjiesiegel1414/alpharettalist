About North Atlanta Spine

North Atlanta Spine is a chiropractic office in Suite 96 at 5755 North Point Parkway. Its team of chiropractors and a massage therapist works with patients who have back pain, neck pain, headaches or muscle tightness, as well as people who want ongoing wellness care.

Services on the practice website include chiropractic adjustments, spinal decompression, massage therapy, soft tissue and myofascial therapy, assisted stretch therapy, and extracorporeal shockwave therapy (ESWT) for problems such as plantar fasciitis and tennis elbow. The office says it aims to make first visits easy for people who have never seen a chiropractor before.

The schedule changes through the week: Monday 8am to 5pm, Tuesday and Thursday 3pm to 6pm, Wednesday 8am to 1pm and Friday 9am to 3pm.

North Atlanta Spine is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
