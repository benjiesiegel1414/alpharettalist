About Fiorenza

Fiorenza Italian is a family-run Tuscan restaurant at 11500 Webb Bridge Way, opened in late June 2026 by Alpharetta couple Jessi and Brooke Qilafi. Jessi grew up in Florence and has worked in restaurants in Italy, New York City and metro Atlanta for more than 15 years.

The menu follows a traditional Italian structure of antipasti, primi, secondi, contorni and dolci, with pasta made by hand. Executive chef Eugene Thompson, whose resume includes Oak Steakhouse and Davio's, leads the kitchen, and the bar pours Italian wines and craft cocktails.

The owners aim for a neighborhood feel with a lively, contemporary room, suited to family dinners and date nights. Reservations are taken through OpenTable or by phone, and walk-ins are welcome.

Fiorenza is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Italian dining scene.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Reserve on OpenTable or call (470) 359-6558 for weekend dinners.
  • Order a handmade pasta as your primi course.
  • It is closed Mondays.
