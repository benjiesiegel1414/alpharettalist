About Noor Studio and Gallery

Noor Studio and Gallery is an art gallery and studio near downtown Alpharetta that doubles as a private event space. Founder Sanaz Noori describes it as a meeting place for art lovers, and the name comes from her last name, which translates from Farsi as light.

Private event packages include sip and paint parties, date nights, birthday painting parties for all ages, brunch and paint, lunch or dinner and paint, tea parties, corporate team building, school events and private exhibitions. Guests gather among the gallery's rotating collection of paintings and mixed-media work. The studio also runs adult and kids' art classes, workshops and summer camps, and it can bring painting and pottery activities to an off-site location.

A gift shop sells prints, jewelry, art supplies and collectible currencies. Full event package details are available on the website, and bookings can be made online or by phone.

Noor Studio and Gallery is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more local services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.

Good to know

  • Best suited to smaller groups and private parties
  • Ask about off-site painting and pottery activities
  • Review the event package PDF on the website before booking
