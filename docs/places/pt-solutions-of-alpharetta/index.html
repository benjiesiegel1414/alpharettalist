About PT Solutions of Alpharetta

PT Solutions calls this clinic Alpharetta Ellard, and it sits in Suite 480 at 8400 Holcomb Bridge Road, near the Ellard area and the Roswell line rather than on North Main Street. It is one clinic in the larger PT Solutions network and is listed as a service of Wellstar Windy Hill Hospital.

The clinic treats patients of all ages for orthopedic pain, sports injuries, pre and post surgery rehab, balance and dizziness problems, and neurological conditions. Across the company, services include dry needling, vestibular therapy, concussion care, pelvic floor therapy and occupational therapy, so it is worth asking which of these are offered at this location.

Hours run 7am to 7pm Monday through Thursday and 7am to 5pm on Friday, with weekends closed. The clinic can be reached by call or text at (770) 640-5470, and appointment requests can also be made online.

PT Solutions of Alpharetta is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.
