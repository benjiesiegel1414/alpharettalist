About Roof Depot

Roof Depot is a roofing and exterior contractor founded in 1998, with its office at 705 Hembree Place in Roswell. The company describes itself as a national partner for homebuilders, general contractors, developers and property managers, with active operations in more than 20 states supported by regional field offices.

Services include re-roofing, tear-offs and new construction roofs in shingle, metal, TPO and modified bitumen, along with flat roof repairs, custom sheet metal flashing, gutters, and fiber cement, engineered wood and vinyl siding for single-family, multifamily and light industrial properties. Property managers can set up preventive maintenance, inspection and emergency leak response programs.

Roof Depot focuses mainly on business and institutional clients. Residential homeowner roof replacements and repairs are handled through America's Best Choice, a company owned by Roof Depot, so homeowners may be directed there for a quote.
