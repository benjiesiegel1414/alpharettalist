About Tonsorial Barber Shop

Tonsorial Barber Shop is a private, upscale grooming parlor for men on Windward Parkway. Founder Luis Morales opened it in 2021 after working at top barbershops around Atlanta, and he comes from a family of barbers and hairdressers. His grandfather owned five shops in Mexico, and Luis is the third generation in the trade.

The shop focuses on men's haircutting, blending old school techniques with modern ones, and offers both hair and shave services. The team stays current on industry standards, and the shop describes its goal as creating an experience rather than simply a service.

Appointments are booked online through the website. The shop is in Suite 204 at 5815 Windward Parkway, so it is convenient for people working in the Windward office corridor, and it opens at 8:30am on weekdays for a cut before work.

Tonsorial Barber Shop is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Book through the website's Book Now link before you go.
  • Weekday hours start at 8:30am, which works for a pre-work cut.
  • The shop is closed on Sundays.
