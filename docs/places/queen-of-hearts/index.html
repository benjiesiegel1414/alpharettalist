About Queen of Hearts

Queen of Hearts Antiques & Interiors is a large antique and home furnishings market at 670 North Main Street in Alpharetta. Alpharetta's visitor bureau describes it as a well-loved stop filled with vintage pieces, collectibles and distinctive home decor.

The business was started by Stacey and Jim Anderson in the late 1990s with just 12 dealers and has grown to more than 550 booth spaces across its Alpharetta, Buford and Marietta stores, according to a City Lifestyle profile. Booths mix antique, vintage and new home interiors: furniture, lighting, area rugs, accessories, collectibles and gifts. Alpharetta dealers specialize in areas such as French and European antiques, farmhouse and rustic pieces, mid-century modern furniture and French Country decor, and some carry Dixie Belle chalk-style paint.

Expect a big space with many separate booths, so allow extra time to browse. The store has hosted frequent DIY workshops, and it has been open seven days a week.

Queen of Hearts is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more shopping in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.
