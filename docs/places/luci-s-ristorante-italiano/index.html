About Luci's Ristorante Italiano

Luci's Ristorante Italiano on Windward Parkway blends Italian cooking with an American steakhouse. The kitchen focuses on fresh ingredients, family recipes and handmade pastas, and steaks are hand-cut and flame-grilled, with a 32-ounce tomahawk as the signature cut.

The menus cover dinner, wines by the glass and from the cellar, cocktails including zero-proof options, and beer. Happy hour runs at the bar on weekdays from 4 to 6:30pm.

Private dining is offered in the Toscana Room, the Roma Room and on an outdoor patio, with space for up to 54 guests, for holiday parties, rehearsal dinners, corporate events and milestone birthdays. The restaurant opens at 4pm Monday through Saturday, is closed on Sundays and recommends reservations on weekends.

Luci's Ristorante Italiano is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. It is one of 103 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Italian dining scene.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Reserve ahead for weekend dinners, as the restaurant recommends.
  • Catch weekday happy hour at the bar from 4 to 6:30pm.
  • Ask about the Toscana or Roma rooms for a private event.
