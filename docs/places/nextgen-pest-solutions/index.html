About Nextgen Pest Solutions

Nextgen Pest Solutions is a veteran-owned pest control and wildlife removal company with an office at 100 Prospect Place, Suite B, in Alpharetta. It serves homes and businesses in Georgia, Florida and Alabama and cites more than 20 years of pest and rodent control experience.

Residential work covers ants, bed bugs, mosquitoes, rodents, cockroaches, spiders and termites. The wildlife side handles bats, birds, raccoons, snakes, squirrels, chipmunks and armadillos. Commercial clients include restaurants, hotels, schools, medical facilities, government buildings and property managers.

The company says it uses kid, pet and eco-friendly treatments and backs its work with a free re-service guarantee if pests return between visits. Same-day or next-day appointments are available in most cases, with 24 hour emergency service for urgent infestations.
