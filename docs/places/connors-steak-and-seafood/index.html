About Connors Steak & Seafood

Connors Steak & Seafood at 5045 Windward Parkway is part of Connor Concepts, a restaurant company established in 1992. The menu pairs prime and choice steaks and chops with mesquite-grilled entrees and fresh seafood, along with a raw oyster bar.

Diners often mention the tuna stack, the chilled seafood tower and the spinach and strawberry salad. The full bar has an extensive wine list, local beers and a large bourbon and whiskey selection, and happy hour runs daily from 3 to 6:30pm in the bar and lounge.

Lunch is served daily until 4pm before the dinner menu takes over, and reservations are available through OpenTable. The restaurant tends to fill up, so booking ahead is a good idea.

Connors Steak & Seafood is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. It is one of 107 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Steakhouse dining scene.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
