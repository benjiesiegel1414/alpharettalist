About Atlanta Eye Group - Alpharetta

Atlanta Eye Group is an independent optometry group with six metro Atlanta offices, each located inside a LensCrafters store. The Alpharetta office is on North Point Parkway and is open seven days a week, with evening hours until 7pm Monday through Saturday.

Exams include comprehensive eye exams, medical eye exams, diabetic exams and digital retinal imaging. The doctors fit standard and specialty contact lenses, including lenses for keratoconus, and offer myopia management for children, dry eye care, glaucoma management and urgent eye care. The office accepts walk-ins and same-day exams, sees children age 5 and up, and staff speak English, Spanish, Hindi, Farsi and Urdu.

The group says patients first meet an optometric assistant for preliminary testing and then get one-on-one time with the doctor, with an emphasis on patient education and prevention. Glasses can be ordered at the LensCrafters store right outside the exam rooms.

Atlanta Eye Group - Alpharetta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
