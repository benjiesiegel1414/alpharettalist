About Secreto Kitchen & Bar

Secreto Southern Kitchen & Bar, now branded on its website as Secreto Kitchen & Bar, is a Southern restaurant at 6195 Windward Pkwy, Suite 102, in the Windward area of Alpharetta. It describes its cooking as farm to table and as an homage to the South, mixing comfort classics with newer creations.

Fried chicken is the dish regulars talk about most, alongside shrimp and grits, fried green tomatoes stuffed with pimento cheese and topped with strawberry jalapeno jam, a fried chicken sandwich, a salmon BLT and an American Wagyu beef burger. Wine and handcrafted cocktails are served, and the bar runs a Community Hour in the late afternoon on weekdays.

The dining room is cozy and dimly lit, which suits a date night, while weekday lunch draws people who work along Windward Parkway. Reservations, private parties, catering and online ordering for pickup or delivery are all handled through the restaurant's website.

Secreto Kitchen & Bar is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Southern dining scene.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Book a table online for dinner, especially on Friday or Saturday.
  • Stop by the bar for Community Hour in the late afternoon.
  • Order the fried chicken, the restaurant's best known dish.
