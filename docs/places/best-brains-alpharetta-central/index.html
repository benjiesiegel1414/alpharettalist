About Best Brains Alpharetta - Central

Best Brains Alpharetta Central is an after-school enrichment center on Century Drive. It serves students from Pre-K through 12th grade with weekly classes in Math, English, Coding, Abacus and General Knowledge, taught by certified teachers using the Best Brains curriculum.

The program focuses on critical thinking, problem solving and building skills ahead of the classroom, with personalized attention in small groups. Families can enroll in one or several subjects, and the site lists monthly subject packages. The center also offers free practice tests during testing season.

Classes run on Tuesday and Thursday evenings and Saturday mornings. New families can book a free assessment online or by phone before choosing subjects, and the wider Best Brains network also offers Pre-K programs, homework help and camps, so ask which run at this location.

Best Brains Alpharetta - Central is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.
