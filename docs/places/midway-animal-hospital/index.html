About Midway Animal Hospital

Midway Animal Hospital opened on Atlanta Highway in 1988, founded by Dr. Rod Hardee with the goal of giving small animals the same standard of care people expect for themselves. It began in a small renovated house and has since grown into a three-story building with a staff of about 20, including four veterinarians.

The hospital has its own surgery center and in-house lab and diagnostic equipment, so many tests and procedures happen on site. Boarding, grooming and bathing are also offered, which lets families handle checkups, care and stays in one place.

Many clients have stayed with the practice for more than two decades. Tours are available for current and prospective clients who want to see the facility, and visitors may meet one of the resident cats.
