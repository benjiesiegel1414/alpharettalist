About Chuan Tasty

Chuan Tasty is a Chinese restaurant in Suite 440 at 5530 Windward Parkway that pairs Sichuan cooking in the fiery Chengdu style with familiar American Chinese dishes.

The menu runs from dumplings, bao buns, stir-fries and soups to Peking duck, Kung Pao chicken and broccoli shrimp. Grilled skewers are a draw, including beef, lamb, beef tendon and tofu skin, and diners who like more traditional Sichuan food can find dishes such as iron plate squid and pork intestine. Reviews on the restaurant's site often mention the steamed pork buns.

You can dine in, order takeout or get delivery, and online ordering is available directly through the website. The restaurant is open every day from 11am to 10pm.

Chuan Tasty is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Chinese, Sichuan dining scene.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
