Arcade, sports bar and restaurant · North Point

About Dave & Buster's Alpharetta

Dave & Buster's Alpharetta combines an arcade, sports bar and restaurant at 6500 North Point Parkway. The game floor has more than 100 games, including newer attractions like High-Tech Darts and Social Shuffle, which groups of up to six can play together.

Sports fans can watch NFL, college football, basketball, hockey and UFC on large HDTVs, including 40-foot screens, with a full-service bar and menu. Game play runs on Power Cards, and combo deals pair an entree with a card or an unlimited play upgrade.

Happy hour runs Monday through Friday from 4pm to 7pm and again Sunday through Thursday from 9pm to 11pm, with discounted cocktails, drafts and house wine. Tables can be reserved by phone for events and parties.

Dave & Buster's Alpharetta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more things to do in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
