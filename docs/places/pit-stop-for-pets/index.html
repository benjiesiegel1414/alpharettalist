About Pit Stop for Pets

Pit Stop for Pets is an independent dog grooming shop in Suite 154 at 12315 Crabapple Road, where it has operated for more than 14 years. It serves dogs from Alpharetta, Milton and Roswell, combining professional grooming, self-serve wash stations and USA-made food and treats under one roof.

Each grooming visit doubles as a coat health treatment: staff work through the coat, skin, ears and body, note things like dry patches or ear buildup, and tell owners what they find. The shop recommends a routine visit every four to six weeks.

Owners who prefer to wash their own dog can use the self-wash stations, which come with tubs, towels, shampoo and dryers. The shop is open Tuesday through Sunday from 10am to 6pm and closed Mondays.

Pit Stop for Pets is in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line. Browse more pets and vets in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Crabapple

Crabapple sits on Alpharetta's west side near the Milton line, known for its rolling, horse-country feel, larger lots and the historic Crabapple crossroads just over the line in Milton. Most businesses here are in small shopping centers with easy parking.

Good to know

  • Self-wash happy hour is a $15 flat rate for any size dog, Tuesday to Thursday 3pm to 5pm
  • Closed Mondays
  • Book grooming on a four to six week routine
