About Coast Dental Alpharetta

Coast Dental Alpharetta is a general dental office on Haynes Bridge Road and part of Coast Dental, a group with offices in Florida, Georgia and Texas. The dentists listed for this office are Dr. Himani Patel, Dr. Michael Quinn and Dr. Anupama Sharma.

The office handles everyday family dentistry such as exams, cleanings, sealants, fillings and oral cancer screenings, along with restorative work like crowns, bridges, root canals, dentures and partials, and implant restorations. Other services include Invisalign and orthodontics, teeth whitening, veneers, periodontal care, extractions including wisdom teeth, sedation dentistry and pediatric dental care. Same-day emergency consultations are offered.

The practice accepts a range of insurance plans and also offers dental savings plans and options for uninsured patients. New patient forms can be downloaded ahead of time, and appointments can be booked online. The office is open weekdays and closed on weekends.
