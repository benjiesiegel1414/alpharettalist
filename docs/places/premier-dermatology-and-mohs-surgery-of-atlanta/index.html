About Premier Dermatology and Mohs Surgery of Atlanta

Premier Dermatology and Mohs Surgery of Atlanta is a dermatology practice on North Point Parkway in Alpharetta. It is led by Dr. Brent Taylor, a board-certified dermatologist and fellowship-trained Mohs surgeon who is also board certified in phlebology. He trained in Mohs and procedural dermatology at Scripps Clinic in La Jolla, California, after residency at the Medical University of South Carolina.

The practice centers on skin cancer: screenings, treatment of basal cell and squamous cell carcinoma and melanoma, Mohs micrographic surgery, surgical excision and photodynamic therapy. It also treats general skin conditions and offers vein care for spider veins and chronic venous insufficiency, plus cosmetic options such as Botox, Kybella and fillers. Kathryn Filipek, PA-C, is also on the team.

The office is in Suite 420 at 3180 North Point Parkway, a short drive from GA 400 and North Point Mall. Early 7am openings run Monday through Wednesday.

Premier Dermatology and Mohs Surgery of Atlanta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
