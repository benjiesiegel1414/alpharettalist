About TRS Painting

TRS Painting, LLC is a painting and remodeling contractor with its office on North Main Street, just north of downtown Alpharetta. It works on both homes and businesses.

Residential services include interior and exterior painting, and the company also handles commercial painting, drywall installation, flooring installation and pressure washing. Additional outdoor work includes custom deck building and fence installation, which makes it an option when a project mixes paint with light carpentry or repairs.

The company says its crews are bilingual and fully insured, and that its two project managers each have more than five years of experience in painting and contracting. Estimates are free, and the office is open weekdays plus Saturday mornings, so homeowners can call before or after a typical workday to set up a visit.

TRS Painting is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more home services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • Bundle drywall repair with painting so one crew handles both.
  • Ask about pressure washing before an exterior repaint.
  • Call on a weekday or Saturday morning to book a free estimate.
