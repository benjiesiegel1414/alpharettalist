About Dominique Dawes Gymnastics & Ninja Academy

Dominique Dawes Gymnastics & Ninja Academy opened in 2026 at 380 North Point Circle, in the North Point area. It is run under the name of Olympic gold medalist Dominique Dawes and is part of her growing group of academies.

Programs cover gymnastics, ninja obstacle training and tumbling for children from beginners through more advanced students. Coaches emphasize safety and let each child progress at their own pace in a low-pressure, supportive setting. Ninja classes give kids who like obstacle courses an alternative to traditional gymnastics.

Families point to the clean, modern facility, convenient location and flexible class scheduling, which helps working parents fit classes around school and other activities.

Dominique Dawes Gymnastics & Ninja Academy is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
