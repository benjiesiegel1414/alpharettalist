About Al's Alpharetta Barber Shop

Al's Alpharetta Barber Shop is a classic men's barbershop on Commerce Street in downtown Alpharetta, near City Center and a short drive from Avalon. The shop cites more than 25 years of experience serving the community and moved into its current modern space in March 2019.

The barbers specialize in classic men's haircuts, precision fades, beard trims, hot towel shaves and straight razor shaves. The shop's stated focus is building long term relationships with clients through quality cuts and easy conversation, and its motto is to come as you are and leave looking your best.

Walk-ins are welcome and appointments can be booked online through Vagaro. The shop is open seven days a week, including Sunday, which makes it easy to fit in a cut before or after work or during a lunch break downtown.

Al's Alpharetta Barber Shop is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
