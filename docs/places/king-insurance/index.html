About King Insurance

King Insurance now operates as King Risk Partners, a second-generation independent agency founded in 1974 by Malcolm C. King Sr. and now owned by his son. The agency has offices from New Hampshire to Florida, including two Alpharetta locations, and ranks among the top 100 brokers in the United States.

As an independent agency it is appointed with hundreds of carriers, some since the 1970s, which lets agents compare options for home, auto, business, employee benefits, aviation and surety coverage. Industry-specific commercial programs are available for businesses with unusual risks.

The Independent Insurance Agents and Brokers of America named King Risk Partners a Best Practices agency each year from 2016 through 2021, and in 2021 recognized it as the top performing Best Practices agency in its revenue class. Agents start with a consultation and then help manage coverage over time.

King Insurance is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. Browse more local services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.
