About J Christopher's

J. Christopher's at Windward Plaza is part of a locally owned breakfast and lunch chain founded by Jay McCann and Chris Brogdon, which opened its first restaurant in Marietta in 1997. The company now runs restaurants across metro Atlanta, Macon, Savannah and Tennessee, including a second Alpharetta location on Old Milton.

The menu features what the company calls familiar food with flair: omelets, breakfast skillets, burgers, sandwiches and salads. House favorites include Blueberry Crunchcakes, Strawberry Waffles, Eggs Christopher and the Club House Burger, and seasonal menus rotate through the year.

The Windward restaurant is open every morning, closing at 2pm on weekdays and 2:30pm on weekends. Online ordering and catering are available.

J Christopher's is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. It is one of 103 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Breakfast dining scene.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Order online for pickup to skip the weekend wait.
  • Try the Blueberry Crunchcakes, a house favorite.
  • Ask about catering for office breakfasts.
