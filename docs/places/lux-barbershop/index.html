About Lux Barbershop

Lux Barbershop on North Main Street blends traditional men's grooming with a modern setting, from authentic barber chairs to hot towel shaves with a straight edge razor. The owner learned the trade from his brother, opened his first shop at 23, and founded Lux after ten years of cutting hair in Alpharetta.

Haircuts range from classic styles to modern fades, with options for long hair and father and son appointments. Shave and beard services include precision shaves, beard trims, hot towels and razor detailing. Add-ons include hair coloring, facials, waxing and shampoo service.

The shop is open seven days a week, including Sunday mornings and afternoons, and appointments are booked through the website. It is in Suite 104 at 631 North Main Street, north of downtown Alpharetta.

Lux Barbershop is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.

Good to know

  • Ask about father and son appointments if you are bringing a child.
  • Saturday opens at 8am for early cuts.
  • Book online through the website's Make Appointment link.
