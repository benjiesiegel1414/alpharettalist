About TruCare Restoration

TruCare Restoration is a family-owned roofing and exterior contractor with an Alpharetta office at 11175 Cicero Drive, Suite 187, and a second office on Bastille Way in Fayetteville. It repairs and replaces shingle and metal roofs, installs gutters and handles storm, hail, wind, tree and fire damage across roughly two dozen metro Atlanta communities, from Acworth to Woodstock.

Beyond residential roofing, the company works on siding, windows, decks, exterior painting and roof coatings, and its commercial crews handle built-up, single-ply, modified bitumen and metal roofs. Each project is assigned a dedicated project manager, and the company advertises a 25-year roof warranty, a satisfaction guarantee and no markup on materials.

The company helps homeowners with insurance claims and offers free next-day inspections and estimates. Hours run Monday through Saturday, 7am to 7pm.
