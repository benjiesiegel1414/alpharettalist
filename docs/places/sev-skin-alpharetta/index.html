About Sev Skin Alpharetta

Sev Skin Alpharetta is part of a national chain of laser hair removal and skin care studios, located in the Stone Walk at North Point shopping center at Haynes Bridge Road and North Point Parkway, near North Point Mall.

The studio offers laser hair removal using dual wavelength Alexandrite and Nd:YAG lasers, which the company says are suited to all skin tones and types. Other services include a quick laser facial, SkinPen microneedling, and cosmetic injections such as Xeomin, Botox and Juvederm. Most appointments are short, and many treatments are listed as having no recovery time.

Sev Skin publishes its pricing on its website before you book and offers single sessions, packages and membership plans. Intro offers are available to clients new to a service on select treatment areas. Appointments can be booked online or by phone, and the studio is closed on Sundays.

Sev Skin Alpharetta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
