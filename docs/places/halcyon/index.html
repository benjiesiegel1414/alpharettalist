About Halcyon

Halcyon is a 135-acre mixed-use district at 6365 Halcyon Way, just across the line in southern Forsyth County off GA 400 at exit 12 (McFarland Parkway). It carries an Alpharetta mailing address and mixes chef-driven restaurants, shops, loft offices, an Embassy Suites hotel and homes for sale or lease around a central Village Green, with a Market Hall at the center that holds public restrooms, family restrooms and an ATM.

Center hours are Monday through Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday 11am to 6pm, with restaurants and entertainment keeping their own schedules. Parking is free in the six-level deck, with 15-minute curbside spots for to-go pickups and premium paid spaces in the event lot and along Halcyon Way. Free Wi-Fi covers the common areas, pets are welcome, and the Village Green has a sandbox and yard games for kids.

Dining is the main draw, from Ocean & Acre, Cattle Shed Wine & Steak Bar and Iron Age Korean Steak House to Gu's Dumplings in the Market Hall, and Trader Joe's anchors the shopping. X-Golf adds indoor golf and a bar. The CMX CinéBistro theater has closed, and the property says a new concept is planned. A 1.5-mile trail extension near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway connects to Forsyth County's Big Creek Greenway.
