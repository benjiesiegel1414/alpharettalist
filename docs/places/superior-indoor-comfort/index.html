About Superior Indoor Comfort

Superior Indoor Comfort is a locally owned and operated heating and cooling contractor on Highway 9 North in Alpharetta that has been in business since 1993. The company describes itself as owner operated and rooted in Georgia.

Services cover the full range of residential and commercial HVAC work, including AC repair, heating repair, new system installations and preventive maintenance. Certified technicians handle both homes and businesses, with an emphasis on keeping systems running efficiently through Georgia's hot summers and cold snaps.

The company says comfort is more than a number on the thermostat, and it aims to make the whole service experience easy, from the first call to the finished job. For repairs, maintenance or an estimate, call 770-664-9098.
