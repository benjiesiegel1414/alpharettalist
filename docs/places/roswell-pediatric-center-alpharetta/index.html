About Roswell Pediatric Center - Alpharetta

Despite its name, Roswell Pediatric Center runs a full office in Alpharetta, in Suite 200 at 11525 Haynes Bridge Road. The group also has offices on Crabapple Road and in Cumming, and all of its pediatricians are board certified in caring for infants, children and adolescents.

Nationally certified nurse practitioners work alongside the physicians on routine sick and well care, and they share after-hours on-call duty. The practice's motto is "Your Child Is Special," and families can view a video tour of each office on the website.

This Alpharetta office is the group's only location with Saturday hours. Providers rotate through Saturday morning sick appointments from 8 to 11:30am, while weekday phone lines are open 8:30am to 4:30pm.

Roswell Pediatric Center - Alpharetta is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Haynes Bridge

Haynes Bridge Road crosses GA 400 in south Alpharetta, close to the Big Creek Greenway and Old Milton Parkway. Businesses here are mostly in shopping centers and office parks with their own parking.
