About Merry Maids North Atlanta Metro

Merry Maids North Atlanta Metro is the local franchise of the national cleaning brand, with an office on Holcomb Bridge Road in Alpharetta. It describes itself as a locally run, woman-led business that has cleaned homes across North Fulton County for more than 25 years.

Services include recurring maid service, one-time cleanings and specialty cleaning, with programs customized to each household's schedule and priorities. Special requests, such as focusing on baseboards or a cluttered room, can be built into a visit. The team suggests a deep clean every 6 to 12 months for homes without regular service.

The office is open on weekdays. The team supports local causes including Cleaning for a Reason, which provides cleanings for people undergoing cancer treatment, and Alzheimer's awareness efforts.

Merry Maids North Atlanta Metro is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more home services in our Alpharetta directory.
