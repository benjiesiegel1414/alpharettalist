About Collage Salon

Colla'ge Salon is a long-running Alpharetta salon on Atlanta Highway that describes nearly 40 years of experience. It is an Aveda Partner Salon and also works with Wella, and it participates in the Aveda Plus rewards program.

Cuts are done with the French hair cutting technique, a precision method meant to create movement and a lasting shape. The menu also includes color and dimensional hi-lites, smoothing treatments for frizz, hair extensions, and styling from blowouts to updos. Waxing and makeup services round out the offering.

The salon has a stylist team page on its website so new clients can pick someone who fits their needs, and there is a new client promotion listed online. Appointments are requested by phone, by email or through an online request form, and the front desk follows up to confirm. Hours vary by day, with early openings on Friday and Saturday.

Collage Salon is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Check the new client promotion on the website before your first visit.
  • Use the online request form after hours; the front desk replies the next business day.
  • Friday and Saturday hours start at 8am, which helps for early appointments.
