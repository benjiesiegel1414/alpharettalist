About Harmony Pediatrics

Harmony Pediatrics is a membership-based pediatric practice at 1800 Abbey Court in Alpharetta led by Dr. Shefali Chheda. Instead of billing visits through insurance, families pay a membership and get unlimited visits with no co-pays or deductibles.

Members receive Dr. Chheda's cell number and can text her directly, with 24/7 access to the practice's board-certified pediatricians. Appointments run longer than at a typical office, same-day sick visits are offered, and home visits are available when needed.

The practice takes an integrative approach that pairs evidence-based medicine with other modalities. It offers nutrition guidance and gut microbiome testing. Its site notes that the model differs from concierge medicine, which often charges more.
