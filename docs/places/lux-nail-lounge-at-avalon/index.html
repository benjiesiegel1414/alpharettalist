About Lux Nail Lounge at Avalon

Lux Nail Lounge at Avalon is a nail salon on Avalon Boulevard, inside the Avalon shopping and dining district. It aims for an upscale, welcoming setting and keeps evening hours most days.

The core menu covers manicures and pedicures, plus nail enhancements such as acrylics, gels and nail art. Beyond nails, the salon offers waxing from brows to legs, semi-permanent eyelash extensions, and permanent shading, a cosmetic tattooing technique also known as ombre or powder brows. The website also lists massage on its services menu.

Appointments can be booked online, and the salon is open seven days a week, until 8pm on weekdays. Its Avalon location makes it easy to pair a nail appointment with shopping or a meal nearby. The salon posts updates and promotions on its social channels, and the website notes when specials are available.

Lux Nail Lounge at Avalon is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.

Good to know

  • Book online to secure a time, especially on weekends at Avalon.
  • Weekday hours run until 8pm.
  • Ask about lash extensions or powder brows if you want to bundle services.
