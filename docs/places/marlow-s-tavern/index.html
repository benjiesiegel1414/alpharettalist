About Marlow's Tavern

Neighborhood tavern known for its prime rib French dip, fish tacos and burgers.

Marlow's Tavern is along McGinnis Ferry Road on Alpharetta's east side, near the Johns Creek line. It is one of 106 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's American dining scene.
