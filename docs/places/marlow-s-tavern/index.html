About Marlow's Tavern

Marlow's Tavern at Avalon is a neighborhood tavern serving scratch-made American food for lunch, dinner and drinks. The menus are led by chef John Metz, and tavern favorites include grilled Atlantic salmon and shrimp and grits.

The room has classic tavern seating and a few flat screens for watching the game without taking over the conversation. A patio offers outdoor dining with handcrafted cocktails, and a gluten-free menu and happy hour menu are posted online.

Marlow's has locations in Georgia and Florida, and the Avalon restaurant is at 3900 1st Street, open daily from 11am. Online ordering and catering are available, and an Insider Club offers rewards and event invitations.

Marlow's Tavern is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. It is one of 108 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's American dining scene.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.
