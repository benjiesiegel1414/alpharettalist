About Su-Tree Service

Su-Tree Service is a family tree company founded in 1989 and now run by owner Jose D. Henriquez, whose brother built the business in Alpharetta. It offers tree removal, trimming, pruning, thinning, storm cleanup, lot clearing and grading for homes and businesses in Alpharetta, Roswell, Marietta, Canton and greater Atlanta.
