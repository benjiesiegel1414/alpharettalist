About Curry Pizza House

Curry Pizza House tops craft pizzas with Indian curries and spices. The concept started in Fremont, California in 2012, created by first-generation Indian Americans who began adding their mother's curry to family pizza nights, and it has grown to several states before arriving in Alpharetta.

Signature pies include Curry Chicken Masala, Shahi Paneer and Tandoori Chicken, alongside classic pizzas. The company says its dough is made in the store and never frozen, and that vegetables are sourced fresh daily.

The restaurant sits at 5530 Windward Parkway and is open every day for lunch and dinner, with later hours on Friday and Saturday. The founders say they travel to European and Asian cities looking for new flavors. Orders can be placed online for pickup.

Curry Pizza House is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. It is one of 107 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Pizza dining scene.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.

Good to know

  • Try the Curry Chicken Masala or Shahi Paneer pizza, the top sellers.
  • Order ahead online for pickup on busy nights.
  • Open until 11pm on Friday and Saturday.
