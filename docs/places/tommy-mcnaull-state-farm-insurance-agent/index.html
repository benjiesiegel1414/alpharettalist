About Tommy McNaull - State Farm Insurance Agent

Tommy McNaull's State Farm office sits on North Point Parkway in Alpharetta, between Haynes Bridge Road and Kimball Bridge Road. The agency builds what State Farm calls a Personal Price Plan for each client, combining coverage with safe driving rewards, bundling and other available discounts.

Coverage options include auto, motorcycle and recreational vehicle insurance, homeowners, renters and personal articles policies, small business insurance, and life, health, Medicare supplement and supplemental health plans. The office also offers annuities, U.S. Bank banking products and investment services such as brokerage and advisory accounts.

Regular hours run Monday through Friday during the business day, and evening or Saturday meetings can be arranged by appointment. The main line also accepts text messages, which is handy for quick policy questions or ID card requests.

Tommy McNaull - State Farm Insurance Agent is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more local services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
