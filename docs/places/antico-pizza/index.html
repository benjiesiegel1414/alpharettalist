About Antico Pizza

Antico Pizza's Avalon shop is part of the Little Italia family of restaurants founded by Giovanni "Gio" Di Palma, whose original Antico opened in Atlanta's West Midtown in 2009. The pizzeria built its name on Neapolitan-style pies baked in wood-fired ovens with traditional, high-quality ingredients.

The Avalon location uses a shared menu with Antico's Battery and Tucker shops. It is a casual, order-and-eat pizzeria rather than a reservation restaurant, and the company only takes special reservations for parties of 10 or more, based on availability.

Pickup and delivery can be ordered through Antico's online partners. Antico also ships frozen pizzas nationwide through Goldbelly, which makes it a common gift choice for out-of-town fans.

Antico Pizza is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. It is one of 103 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Pizza dining scene.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.
