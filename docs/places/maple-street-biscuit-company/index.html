About Maple Street Biscuit Company

Maple Street Biscuit Company serves handcrafted breakfast and lunch from 50 Canton St in downtown Alpharetta. The chain opened its first restaurant in Jacksonville, Florida in 2012, later moved its headquarters to Nashville and now operates in several states.

Biscuit sandwiches built around hand-breaded chicken are the core of the menu. The Squawking Goat, with fried chicken, crispy goat cheese and pepper jelly, was featured on Food Network's "Guilty Pleasures," and other favorites include the Five and Dime with bacon, cheddar, egg and gravy, plus hash bowls, waffles and cold brew.

The restaurant is a daytime spot, opening at 7am every day and closing in the early afternoon. Online ordering and catering are available through the company's website.

Maple Street Biscuit Company is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. It is one of 107 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Breakfast dining scene.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
