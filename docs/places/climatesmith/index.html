About ClimateSmith

ClimateSmith is a family owned and operated heating and air conditioning contractor based on Performance Drive in Cumming. It markets itself as an Alpharetta HVAC company and lists Alpharetta, Milton, Crabapple, Roswell, Johns Creek and Cumming among the areas it serves across Fulton, Forsyth and Gwinnett counties.

Residential work includes AC repair, furnace repair and replacement, heat pump installation and repair, ductless mini splits, new system installation and routine maintenance under a maintenance plan. The company services all brands. On the commercial side it offers HVAC service, repair and maintenance for offices and other businesses.

The office is open on weekdays during business hours and closed on weekends. The company's blog covers common local problems such as a second floor that stays warmer than the first, which is useful reading before a service call.

ClimateSmith is a short drive from Alpharetta in Cumming. Browse more home services in our Alpharetta directory.

Good to know

  • Call on weekdays; the office is closed Saturday and Sunday.
  • Ask about the maintenance plan for regular tune-ups.
  • They service all brands, so you do not need the original installer.
