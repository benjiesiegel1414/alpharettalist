About Little Alley Steak

Little Alley Steak's Alpharetta location occupies the former Salt Factory Pub space at 102 South Main Street, which closed in July 2025 after 15 years. It is the third location for the steakhouse, following the Roswell original and a larger Buckhead restaurant, and it comes from the same restaurant group that ran Salt Factory.

The menu centers on wet-aged and dry-aged steaks, with oysters, shellfish and other seafood, backed by a wine and spirits program. It is an upscale, special-occasion kind of steakhouse, and tables can be booked through OpenTable.

Dinner runs every evening, with later closing on Fridays and Saturdays, and a Sunday lunch service has been listed as well. The downtown address puts it within walking distance of Alpharetta City Center and the Town Green.

Little Alley Steak is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. It is one of 108 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Steakhouse dining scene.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know

  • Book a table on OpenTable for weekend dinners.
  • Ask about the dry-aged cuts, a focus of the menu.
  • Park in the downtown city decks and walk over, since Main Street parking fills up.
