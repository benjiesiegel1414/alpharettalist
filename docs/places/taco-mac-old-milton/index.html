About Taco Mac Old Milton

Taco Mac Old Milton is the Atlanta born sports bar chain's location at 4305 Old Milton Parkway. It is a go to spot for watching games over wings, burgers and the brand's other bar food favorites, and it stays open until midnight most nights and 1am on Fridays and Saturdays.

Craft beer is a big part of the draw. The location's live tap list on Taco Mac's site tracked 73 unique beers in 20 styles when we checked, from domestic lagers to Georgia IPAs, ciders and stouts. Beer fans can join Taco Mac's Brewniversity program, which rewards trying new beers.

The dining room is open, and takeout, delivery, online ordering and catering are available. A lunch menu is offered as well.

Taco Mac Old Milton is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. It is one of 111 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Sports bar dining scene.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.

Good to know

  • Check the live tap list on the location page before you go.
  • Join Brewniversity if you plan to work through the beer list.
  • Order catering for game day parties at home.
