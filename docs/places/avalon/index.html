About Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with more than 570,000 square feet of shops, more than two dozen chef-driven restaurants, a 12-screen all-premium Regal theater, a conference center and The Hotel at Avalon, plus offices and homes. General center hours are Monday through Saturday 10am to 9pm and Sunday noon to 7pm, though individual stores and restaurants set their own schedules. The concierge desk (770-765-1000) can help with directions, bag check, stroller and wheelchair rentals and restaurant reservations.

Parking is easy and mostly free. Self-parking is free every day in the decks and surface lots on the north and south sides of the property, with entrances off Old Milton Parkway and Westside Parkway. Metered street parking along Avalon Boulevard works for quick stops, and valet costs $12 at three stations: Avalon Boulevard and 2nd Street near Concierge, Avalon Boulevard and 3rd Street near Oak Steakhouse, and The Hotel at Avalon. Valet does not accept oversized vehicles or motorcycles.

The store lineup mixes big names and boutiques, including Apple, CHANEL, Sephora, lululemon, Anthropologie, Free People, Madewell, J. Crew, Tory Burch, Kendra Scott, Pottery Barn, Williams Sonoma, Crate & Barrel, Arhaus, The Container Store and a Whole Foods Market.

Much of the appeal is the calendar. Each winter brings the Lighting of Avalon, Santa visits and Avalon on Ice, a Rockefeller-inspired skating rink that runs from November 22 to January 18 this season, and fall Saturdays bring college football on the big screen. The paved Alpha Loop links Avalon to Downtown Alpharetta in about a mile.

Avalon is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. Browse more shopping in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Avalon located?

Avalon is located at 400 Avalon Blvd, Alpharetta, GA 30009, on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown.

What does Avalon offer?

Avalon is listed under shopping (shopping & dining district). Alpharetta's 86-acre upscale mixed-use district: dozens of shops and restaurants along the Boulevard, the Regal Avalon 12 theater, The Hotel at Avalon, and a plaza with bocce, swings and fire pits. Home to a seasonal ice rink, tree lighting and Santa visits each winter.

What are Avalon's hours?

Avalon hours: Mon-Sat 10am-9pm; Sun 12pm-7pm (store and restaurant hours may vary). Hours can change on holidays, so call (770) 765-2000 to confirm.

What is the phone number for Avalon?

You can reach Avalon at (770) 765-2000.

Is Avalon open on Sunday?

Yes. Avalon is open on Sundays, 12pm-7pm (store and restaurant hours may vary).

Is parking free at Avalon in Alpharetta?

Yes. Self-parking is free daily in the parking decks and surface lots on the north and south sides of Avalon. Metered street parking along Avalon Boulevard is available for quick stops, and valet is $12.

Where is valet parking at Avalon?

Valet has three drop-off and pick-up points: Avalon Boulevard at 2nd Street near Concierge, Avalon Boulevard at 3rd Street near Oak Steakhouse, and The Hotel at Avalon. Oversized vehicles and motorcycles are not accepted.

What stores are at Avalon?

Avalon's shops include Apple, CHANEL, Sephora, lululemon, Anthropologie, Free People, Madewell, J. Crew, Tory Burch, Kendra Scott, Pottery Barn, Williams Sonoma, Crate & Barrel, Arhaus, The Container Store and Whole Foods Market, plus local boutiques and the Regal Avalon theater.

What is Avalon's phone number?

Avalon's general line is 770-765-2000 and the concierge desk is 770-765-1000.
