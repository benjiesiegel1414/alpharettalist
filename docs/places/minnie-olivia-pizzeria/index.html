About Minnie Olivia Pizzeria

Minnie Olivia Pizzeria serves wood-fired pizzas, pastas and Italian small plates from the historic Dodd Hotel building at 10 Roswell Street, steps from the Town Green. The restaurant takes its name from Minnie Olivia Dodd, for whom the building is named, and it has a full bar.

Chef Julio Delgado, formerly executive chef at New Realm Brewing and JP Atlanta, opened it in November 2019. The space is intimate, with about 38 seats inside including a small bar, and roughly 40 more on the outdoor patio.

It suits a casual dinner downtown, a lunch break or a pizza before an evening on the green. Pizzas come out of a wood-fired oven, and the full bar pours cocktails, beer and wine to go with them.

Minnie Olivia Pizzeria is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Pizza dining scene.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.

Good to know
