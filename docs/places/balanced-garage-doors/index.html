About Balanced Garage Doors

Balanced Garage Doors is a garage door repair and installation company with its office on Hembree Road in Alpharetta, plus a second location in Lawrenceville. It serves Alpharetta, Atlanta, Marietta, Lawrenceville and the northern metro area.

Common jobs include broken spring replacement, opener repair and installation, routine maintenance and full residential door replacement. The company uses powder-coated, extended-life springs and offers a free 25-point inspection with no service fee. It also says it can often dispatch a technician within a few hours for emergencies.

Regular service hours run weekdays and Saturdays, with the office staffed Monday through Friday and emergency service available around the clock. An adult needs to be present at the start of the visit to approve the work order and at the end to review the job.

Good to know

  • Ask about extended-life springs when replacing a broken one.
