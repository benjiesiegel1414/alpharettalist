About Peachtree Eye Associates

Peachtree Eye Associates, P.C. is an optometry practice located inside the LensCrafters store at Avalon in Alpharetta. It provides eye care for the whole family, from routine exams to medical eye visits.

Comprehensive exams use retinal imaging and computerized vision testing. Contact lens evaluations include soft, gas permeable, hybrid and scleral lenses, plus multifocal lenses and lenses for keratoconus. The doctors also do LASIK evaluations and treat urgent problems such as scratched corneas, foreign bodies in the eye, sudden flashes or floaters, pink eye, eye allergies, severe dry eye and pain after removing contacts, and they try to fit those patients in the same day.

Because the office shares space with LensCrafters, glasses can be picked out right after an exam. Weekend hours, including Sunday afternoons, make it easier to fit an appointment around work or school.

Peachtree Eye Associates is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.

Good to know

  • Sunday appointments are available from noon to 5pm
  • Call the office for same-day help with eye injuries or sudden floaters
  • Patient forms are on the website to fill out ahead of time
