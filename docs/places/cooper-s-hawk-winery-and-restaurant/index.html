About Cooper's Hawk Winery & Restaurant

Cooper's Hawk Winery & Restaurant on North Point Parkway pairs a full-service restaurant with a tasting room pouring the company's own wines. Guests can take a guided tasting at the bar, then sit down for lunch, dinner or weekend brunch.

A Wine Club gives members a wine of the month, dining rewards and discounts, and some membership levels include a monthly tasting for two. Weekday happy hour runs in the afternoon with cocktails, wines by the glass and small bites, and weekend brunch is served Saturday and Sunday mornings.

Carryout and curbside pickup are available, and the restaurant hosts private events. Gift cards and Wine Club gift memberships can be bought online or in the tasting room.

Cooper's Hawk Winery & Restaurant is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. It is one of 103 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's American dining scene.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.

Good to know

  • Book dining reservations ahead; they are highly recommended.
  • Wine tastings are walk-in only and served in order of arrival.
  • Weekday happy hour runs from 3 to 5:30pm.
