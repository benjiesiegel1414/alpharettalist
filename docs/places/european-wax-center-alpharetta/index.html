About European Wax Center Alpharetta

European Wax Center Alpharetta is a location of the national waxing chain, on Old Milton Parkway near Avalon. The center focuses on hair removal for women and men, with services covering the brows, face, underarms, arms, legs, back, chest and bikini areas, including Brazilian waxing.

Facial services include brow waxing and brow tinting, lip, chin, cheek, nose, ear and full face waxing. Men's waxing has its own menu. Pricing for each service is listed on the location page, and the center offers Wax Pass packages that lower the per visit cost for regular clients, along with a new guest offer available in the center.

The brand publishes first visit guidance, prep tips and aftercare advice on its website, which is useful for anyone new to waxing. The center is open seven days a week with evening hours until 8pm on weekdays, and appointments can be booked online.

European Wax Center Alpharetta is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. Browse more beauty and spas in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.

Good to know

  • Read the first visit and prep guidance on the website before your appointment.
  • Ask about a Wax Pass if you plan to come in regularly.
  • Weekday evenings run until 8pm, which helps after work.
