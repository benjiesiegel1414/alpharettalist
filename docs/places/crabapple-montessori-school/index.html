About Crabapple Montessori School

Crabapple Montessori School is a private preschool and elementary school at 12387 Crabapple Road for children ages 15 months to 12 years. It is accredited by Cognia and follows an authentic Montessori curriculum.

Classes are grouped into toddler (15 months to 3 years), primary (3 to 6 years) and elementary (6 to 12 years) levels. The school sits on a three-acre wooded lot, and the building was designed around Montessori principles, with spacious, naturally lit classrooms. Each classroom has its own fenced children's garden for outdoor learning.

Families come from Alpharetta, Milton, Roswell, Cumming, Canton and Woodstock. Admission follows a set path: schedule a tour, submit an application, attend a parent interview, bring your child for a visit, and then receive a decision.

Crabapple Montessori School is in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line. Browse more schools in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Crabapple

Crabapple sits on Alpharetta's west side near the Milton line, known for its rolling, horse-country feel, larger lots and the historic Crabapple crossroads just over the line in Milton. Most businesses here are in small shopping centers with easy parking.
