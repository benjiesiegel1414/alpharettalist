About The Slime Factory Alpharetta

The Slime Factory runs a hands-on slime lab inside North Point Mall, in Suite 1082 at 1000 North Point Circle. Kids put on lab coats and safety goggles, mix their own batches at the lab bench and test how far the slime will stretch.

General admission sessions last 30 to 60 minutes and include two custom slimes, protective gear, a sensory pool, a certificate and photo spots. Birthday parties come in two sizes, small for 5 to 12 slime makers and large for 13 to 25, and run two hours. Field trips for 15 to 50 children are offered on weekdays and last 90 minutes.

The lab is open 11am to 7pm Monday through Thursday, 11am to 8pm on Friday and Saturday, and noon to 7pm on Sunday. Holiday hours may differ.

The Slime Factory Alpharetta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.

Good to know

  • Everyone should sign the online waiver before arriving
  • Tickets are required for ages 2 and up, and kids under 14 need an adult
  • Booking a ticket gets you a coupon toward a birthday party package
