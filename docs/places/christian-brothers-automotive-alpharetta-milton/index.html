About Christian Brothers Automotive Alpharetta Milton

Christian Brothers Automotive Alpharetta Milton is owned and operated by Mike and Cathy Cotter, who first came to the brand as customers. The shop is a locally owned franchise of Christian Brothers Automotive.

It is at 6290 Atlanta Highway, about half a mile from The Crooked Creek Golf Club near the Village at Crooked Creek. From GA-400, take Exit 12B to McFarland Parkway, turn left on Atlanta Highway, and the shop is about a quarter mile on the right. It serves Alpharetta and Milton, including the Windward and Avalon areas.

The shop handles comprehensive auto repair and maintenance with certified technicians and courtesy inspections. Amenities include a free shuttle, after hours key drop off, free Wi-Fi and refreshments, a waiting lounge, special financing and mobile service. The shop is open weekdays and closed on weekends.

Christian Brothers Automotive Alpharetta Milton is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more local services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Windward

The Windward area wraps around Windward Parkway and GA 400 exit 11 on Alpharetta's north side. It mixes office campuses with shopping centers and restaurants along Windward Parkway, and it's an easy hop to Halcyon and Forsyth County to the north. Most businesses here have their own surface parking.
