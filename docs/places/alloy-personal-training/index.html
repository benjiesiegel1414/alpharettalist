About Alloy Personal Training

Alloy Personal Training's Haynes Bridge Village gym is in Suite 600 at 9925 Haynes Bridge Road. Alloy is an Atlanta-based personal training brand that has grown into a national franchise.

The gym focuses on coached workouts rather than open access. Members can choose one-on-one personal training or small group sessions, with programs customized to their experience level. Nutrition coaching and results tracking are part of the offer, making it a fit for busy professionals who want accountability and measurable progress.

Weekday hours run from 8am into the evening Monday through Thursday, with a slightly earlier close on Fridays. Reach out to set up an introductory session and assessment before committing.

Alloy Personal Training is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more fitness and gyms in our Alpharetta directory.
