About Arbor Terrace Crabapple

Arbor Terrace Crabapple is a senior living community on Crabapple Road operated by The Arbor Company. It offers three options: assisted living, the Bridges program for seniors in the early stages of cognitive decline, and memory care in its Evergreen neighborhood for residents living with Alzheimer's disease or other forms of dementia, with care around the clock.

Services and amenities include restaurant-style dining, concierge services, fitness and wellness programs, housekeeping, laundry, accessible transportation and an on-site beauty salon and spa. A monthly social calendar covers classes, hobbies, outings and volunteer activities, and there is a patio with rocking chairs and walking areas. The community offers pet-friendly accommodations.

Starting rates for each apartment type are posted on the website, along with a cost calculator that compares senior living with aging in place. Tours can be scheduled online or by phone.

Arbor Terrace Crabapple is in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Crabapple

Crabapple sits on Alpharetta's west side near the Milton line, known for its rolling, horse-country feel, larger lots and the historic Crabapple crossroads just over the line in Milton. Most businesses here are in small shopping centers with easy parking.
