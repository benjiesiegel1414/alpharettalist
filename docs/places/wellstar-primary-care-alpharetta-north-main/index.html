About Wellstar Primary Care - Alpharetta North Main

This Wellstar Primary Care office sits in Stonewood Village at the corner of North Main Street (Highway 9) and Henderson Parkway, near the Queen of Hearts shopping center. It is part of Wellstar Health System and is accepting new patients.

The providers are internal medicine physicians Dr. Tusharkumar Patel and Dr. Sima Pandey, who see adults 18 and older, and family medicine physician Dr. Anantratn Asthana, who sees patients 12 and older. The office handles preventive care, annual physicals, sick visits and management of chronic conditions, with referrals to specialists when needed. Routine bloodwork is done by on-site lab services, and virtual visits are available.

Early appointments start at 7am. Patients can schedule through Wellstar MyChart, should arrive 15 minutes early to check in, and are asked to give 24 hours notice for cancellations. Parking is on site.

Wellstar Primary Care - Alpharetta North Main is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.
