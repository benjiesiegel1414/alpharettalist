About Piedmont Urgent Care - Milton/Alpharetta

Piedmont Urgent Care on Highway 9 is a walk-in clinic in the Deerfield Place shopping center, across from Cafe Lavande, just past the Deerfield Parkway intersection near the Milton and Alpharetta line. It treats patients of all ages for non-life-threatening illnesses and injuries.

The clinic is open seven days a week from 8am to 8pm, and no appointment is needed. Patients can also book ahead online or schedule a virtual visit. Services include diagnostic testing, allergy testing, COVID-19 testing, sports physicals, treatment for throat and respiratory infections and occupational health care.

From GA-400, take Exit 11 for Windward Parkway, head west, and turn right onto Highway 9. Free parking, including accessible spaces, is in front of the building.
