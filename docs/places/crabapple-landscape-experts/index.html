About Crabapple Landscape Experts

Crabapple LandscapExperts is a locally owned commercial landscape firm whose north branch is listed on Union Center Drive in Alpharetta. The company dates to 1992 and describes itself as a commercial landscaping company serving commercial and mixed-use properties and community associations across Georgia.

Its work falls into four areas: maintenance, including lawn care, irrigation and stormwater management; landscape construction; enhancements such as seasonal color, commercial lighting and tree trimming and removal; and design. Featured projects include City of Alpharetta streetscapes and parks, the McDonough Town Square project, a river restoration at the Deaton Creek community, work at Ponce City Market, a retaining wall at Howell Park and permeable paver installations. The Atlanta Business Chronicle has included it among the region's largest commercial landscape companies.

Office hours are listed as weekdays from 7am to 5pm. The firm is mainly set up for property managers and HOAs rather than individual homeowners.
