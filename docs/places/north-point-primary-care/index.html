About North Point Primary Care

North Point Primary Care is an internal medicine practice on North Point Parkway that has cared for adult patients in Alpharetta and North Fulton for more than 20 years. It is a Northside Hospital network provider.

The practice is led by Dr. Long Le, an internist who lives in the Alpharetta community. Care includes comprehensive annual physicals, management of chronic conditions, treatment of acute illnesses and lifestyle consultations aimed at long-term health. The practice emphasizes building lasting relationships with patients and giving each visit enough time.

The office opens at 7:30am on weekdays, which helps patients who want to be seen before work, and closes at 11:30am on Wednesdays. It accepts most insurance plans and is welcoming new patients. Online scheduling, a patient portal and virtual visits are available through the practice website. The office is in Building 200 of the 3180 North Point Parkway complex.

North Point Primary Care is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
