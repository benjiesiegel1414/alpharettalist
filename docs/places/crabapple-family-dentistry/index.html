About Crabapple Family Dentistry

Crabapple Family Dentistry is a general and family dental office on Crabapple Road, serving Alpharetta, Milton and nearby communities. The practice has been recognized as a Best of Milton winner.

The office is led by Dr. Kathryn Elkins, who earned her biochemistry degree with highest honors from Georgia Tech and her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Medical College of Georgia. She is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry and has done volunteer and mission dental work, including at a clinic in Cusco, Peru.

Care covers routine cleanings and exams, preventive dentistry, fillings, crowns and bridges, restoration of dental implants and cosmetic treatments. The practice says it accepts virtually all major insurance, offers same-day appointments and has monthly payment plans available through Sunbit. Appointments can be booked online. The office is open Monday through Thursday only.

Crabapple Family Dentistry is in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Crabapple

Crabapple sits on Alpharetta's west side near the Milton line, known for its rolling, horse-country feel, larger lots and the historic Crabapple crossroads just over the line in Milton. Most businesses here are in small shopping centers with easy parking.
