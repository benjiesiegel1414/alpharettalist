About Elm3 Financial Group

Elm3 Financial Group is a financial and investment advisory firm in Suite 92 at 5755 North Point Parkway in Alpharetta. Founder and principal Stacey Nickens brings a background in financial planning, investment management, tax preparation and accounting, and the team includes CPAs, enrolled agents, registered investment advisers and CFP professionals.

Services for individuals include investment management, retirement and Social Security planning, insurance evaluations and estate planning. For small businesses the firm handles tax planning and preparation, management consulting and business retirement plans.

The firm's view is that investments, insurance, taxes and business decisions all affect one another, so it tries to look at them together. Its blog covers practical topics like stock compensation and setting up an IRS payment plan.

Elm3 Financial Group is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more local services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
