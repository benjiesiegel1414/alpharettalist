About Ocean & Acre

Ocean & Acre is one of the anchor sit-down restaurants at Halcyon, the walkable district at GA 400 exit 12. It sits in a bright, barn-like building and builds its menu around two ideas: seafood from the ocean and smoked meats and seasonal produce from the land. Expect shareable plates, a raw bar and entrees that work as well for a business lunch as for a weekend dinner with friends.

The restaurant is open every day from 11am, until 10pm Monday through Saturday and 9pm on Sunday. Weekday happy hour runs 3pm to 6pm, weekend brunch is served 11am to 3pm, and a live musician usually plays on Sunday afternoons from 1pm to 4pm.

Parking is free in the Halcyon deck a short walk away. After dinner, the Village Green, Kilwins and the Big Creek Greenway trailhead are all within a few minutes on foot.

Ocean & Acre is at Halcyon, the walkable shopping and dining district at GA 400 exit 12, just north of Alpharetta. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Seafood dining scene.

Visiting Halcyon

Halcyon is a 135-acre walkable district at GA 400 exit 12, just over the line in south Forsyth County with an Alpharetta mailing address. Parking is free in the six-level deck, public and family restrooms are in the Market Hall across from the Village Green, and a trail extension near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway connects to the Big Creek Greenway.
