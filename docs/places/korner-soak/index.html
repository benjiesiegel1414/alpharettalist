About Korner Soak

Korner Soak is a small, locally owned shop at 22 Milton Avenue in downtown Alpharetta. It specializes in aromatic bath and beauty products meant for relaxation and pampering, and it also carries pajamas and loungewear, so it covers both the bath and the cozy night in that follows.

Alpharetta's visitor bureau includes the shop in its Only in Alpharetta list of locally owned businesses. The mix of bath products and loungewear makes it a natural stop when you need a gift for a birthday, a hostess thank you or a self-care treat, and smaller items work well as stocking stuffers or add-ons to a larger present.

The store sits on the same stretch of Milton Avenue as Sis + Moon's, a short walk from Main Street and Alpharetta City Center, so it pairs easily with lunch or coffee downtown. Hours could not be confirmed, so call before a special trip.

Korner Soak is in Downtown Alpharetta, the walkable City Center district around Main Street and the Town Green. Browse more shopping in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Downtown Alpharetta

Downtown is built for walking. City Center, with the Town Green, City Hall and the library, sits a block off Main Street, and free public parking decks are spread around the district, so most people park once and walk. Canton Street and Milton Avenue are lined with restaurants and shops, the Town Green hosts concerts and festivals, and the paved Alpha Loop links downtown to Avalon. On Saturday mornings from spring through fall, the Alpharetta Farmers Market sets up on Old Canton Street.
