About Newpark Orthodontics

Newpark Orthodontics is an orthodontic practice in Suite 2i at 4190 Old Milton Parkway in Alpharetta, led by Dr. Nima Hajibaik, known to patients as Dr. Nima. It serves families from Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Roswell and Cumming.

Treatment options include traditional braces, clear braces and Invisalign for children, teens and adults. The practice also offers early treatment to guide young smiles, care for complex bite and jaw problems, and retainers to protect results. Patients can join a Lifetime Retainer Program.

Dr. Nima is a member of the Roth-Williams International Society of Orthodontists, the American Association of Orthodontists, the Southern Association of Orthodontists and the American Dental Association. The practice reports helping more than 1,000 patients, and reviews often mention a clean, modern office. Hours run weekdays from 8am to 5pm.

Newpark Orthodontics is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.
