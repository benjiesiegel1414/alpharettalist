About Ameris Bank Amphitheatre

Alpharetta's 12,000-seat outdoor concert venue in a wooded setting at Encore Park, hosting major country, rock and pop tours from spring through fall.

Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is at Encore Park on Encore Parkway, home of Ameris Bank Amphitheatre. Browse more things to do in our Alpharetta directory.

Good to know

  • The lawn is first-come; bring a blanket (chairs are usually rentable)
  • Traffic stacks up on Westside Parkway before shows, so arrive early
  • The season generally runs April through October
