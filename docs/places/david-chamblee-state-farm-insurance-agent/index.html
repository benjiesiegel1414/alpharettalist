About David Chamblee - State Farm Insurance Agent

David Chamblee is a second-generation Atlanta-area State Farm agent with 20 years of financial services experience. His office is on Windward Parkway, and he focuses in part on helping people moving to Alpharetta through corporate and state-to-state relocations. He holds a BA from Vanderbilt and an MBA from the University of Texas.

The agency writes auto, motorcycle and recreational vehicle insurance, homeowners and renters coverage, personal articles policies and small business insurance. It also offers life insurance including guaranteed issue final expense, supplemental, Medicare supplement and individual medical plans, pet medical insurance, annuities, U.S. Bank banking products and investment services.

Staff speak English, with Spanish and Chinese also listed. The office stays open until 6pm Monday through Thursday, closes at 5pm on Friday, and meets on Saturdays by appointment. Chamblee also sponsors the city's Alpharetta Touch a Truck event.
