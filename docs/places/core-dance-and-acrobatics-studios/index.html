About Core Dance & Acrobatics Studios

Core Dance & Acrobatics Studios is a performing arts studio on Atlanta Highway in north Alpharetta, near the Milton line. It teaches ballet, tap, jazz, acrobatics, contemporary, hip-hop and musical theater from beginner to advanced levels, with class tracks for ages 3 to 5, 6 to 8, 9 to 11 and 12 and up, plus adult classes.

The facility has three studios with sprung maple floors, one with a Marley surface, and a separate aerial arts room with silks and lyras. Parents can watch class on lobby screens, and dancers have a lounge and homework area between classes. Jayme Leach directs the ballet program, and the studio also runs the Elements Performance Company.

The season includes a holiday show in December, a Nutcracker production and an end-of-season recital in May. The studio also hosts birthday parties.

Core Dance & Acrobatics Studios is in the Windward Parkway area on Alpharetta's north side, near GA 400 exit 11. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.
