About Koi Kaiten Sushi

Koi Kaiten Sushi is a Japanese restaurant at 4180 Old Milton Parkway, Suite 1I, built around a revolving conveyor belt sushi bar, a style known in Japan as kaiten sushi. Diners can pull plates of sushi straight off the belt as they pass or order from the menu.

The belt carries a steady rotation of rolls and small plates, so you can see what looks good before you commit and try several items in one sitting. If something you want is not coming around, ordering from the menu fills the gap. Alpharetta's visitor bureau describes it simply as a place to choose from the menu or pick a plate from the revolving bar.

The format suits a quick, casual meal and is fun for kids and groups who like to sample a variety of rolls and small plates. It also works for a solo lunch, since you can eat at your own pace without waiting on courses. Takeout and delivery are available through delivery apps for nights at home.

Koi Kaiten Sushi is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Sushi dining scene.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.

Good to know

  • Sit at the belt to grab plates as they go by.
  • Order from the menu if you do not see what you want on the belt.
  • Call ahead to confirm current hours.
