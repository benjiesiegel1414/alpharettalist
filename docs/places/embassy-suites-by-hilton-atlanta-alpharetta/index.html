About Embassy Suites by Hilton Atlanta Alpharetta

Embassy Suites by Hilton Atlanta Alpharetta is an all-suite hotel on North Point Parkway, close to North Point Mall and GA 400. Suites have separate living areas and kitchen appliances, which suits families and longer business stays.

A free breakfast buffet and a free evening manager's reception are included. The hotel has an indoor pool open 6 AM to 10 PM, a fitness center, a bar and free self parking. For events there is a ballroom and several smaller meeting rooms, with capacity listed for groups of up to 240 guests.

Check-in runs from 4 PM to midnight and check-out is at 11 AM; late check-out may be available for a fee. Cats and dogs up to 50 pounds are allowed, two per room, for a per-stay fee. Service animals are exempt.

Embassy Suites by Hilton Atlanta Alpharetta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more things to do in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
