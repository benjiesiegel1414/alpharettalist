About Mr. Rooter Plumbing of Atlanta

Mr. Rooter Plumbing of Atlanta is a locally owned and independently operated franchise of the Mr. Rooter brand, part of the Neighborly family of home service companies. Its office sits on Bush Street in Roswell, and Alpharetta is one of the communities on its service list.

The team's licensed plumbers handle residential and commercial plumbing repair, clogged and basement drains, drain cleaning, HydroScrub drain jetting, sewer camera inspections, sewer line repair and emergency plumbing. The company also lists septic drain field installation and service, which can matter for homes on septic in the northern parts of the area.

Scheduling is available around the clock, and the company says its workmanship and parts are guaranteed. Expect a technician to come to your home rather than a storefront visit.

Mr. Rooter Plumbing of Atlanta is a short drive from Alpharetta in Roswell. Browse more home services in our Alpharetta directory.

Good to know

  • Scheduling is available 24/7, so emergencies can be booked after hours.
  • Ask about HydroScrub jetting for recurring sewer or drain clogs.
  • Homes on septic can ask about drain field service as well.
