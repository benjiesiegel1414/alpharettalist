About Atlanta Institute for ENT

Atlanta Institute for ENT is an ear, nose and throat practice in the Northside Hospital network with an Alpharetta office in Suite 520 at 3333 Old Milton Parkway. From GA-400, take Exit 10 and head east about half a mile to the second light, then turn right into the building entrance.

Its physicians treat adults and children for sinus and nasal problems, allergies, sleep and snoring issues, voice and swallowing concerns and ear conditions. Pediatric otolaryngology is among the listed specialties, and audiologists are part of the team for hearing evaluations.

The practice's website includes background on each doctor's training and interests so patients know who they will see before a consultation. Call the office to make an appointment, or ask your doctor to send a referral.

Atlanta Institute for ENT is on Old Milton Parkway, the corridor that links GA 400 with Avalon and downtown. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.
