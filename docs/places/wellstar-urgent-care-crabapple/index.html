About Wellstar Urgent Care - Crabapple

Wellstar Urgent Care in Crabapple occupies Suite 901 at 12460 Crabapple Road in north Alpharetta. Part of the Wellstar network, the clinic sees patients of every age for minor illnesses and injuries, from colds and flu to cuts, strains and small breaks.

The doors are open seven days a week from 8am to 7:30pm. Walk in, or save time by reserving an arrival time online; walk-ins are still accepted when online reservations are full. Wellstar's virtual urgent care is available 24 hours a day for problems that come up after closing.

Beyond sick visits, the clinic offers COVID-19 testing, imaging, sports physicals, and occupational health services like pre-employment drug screens and workers' compensation visits.

Wellstar Urgent Care - Crabapple is in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting Crabapple

Crabapple sits on Alpharetta's west side near the Milton line, known for its rolling, horse-country feel, larger lots and the historic Crabapple crossroads just over the line in Milton. Most businesses here are in small shopping centers with easy parking.
