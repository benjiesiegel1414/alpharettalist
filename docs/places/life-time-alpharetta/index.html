About Life Time Alpharetta

Life Time Alpharetta is a large athletic club at 855 North Point Parkway. It is open from 4am to midnight on weekdays and 6am to 11pm on weekends, and the workout floor and studios host more than 100 group classes a week, from strength and cycle to yoga, Pilates and dance.

Swimmers have an indoor lap pool plus indoor and outdoor recreation pools, and there are indoor pickleball and basketball courts with leagues. Recovery options include a sauna and cold plunge, and LifeSpa offers hair, skin and nail services. LifeCafe serves a nutritionist-designed menu, and a Work Lounge gives members a place to work between sessions.

Families can use Kids Academy, which offers supervised childcare, kids classes, sports and camps. Membership is month to month, and the club offers free guided tours and day passes.

Life Time Alpharetta is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more fitness and gyms in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.

Good to know

  • Schedule a free guided tour online or by phone before joining.
  • Buy a day pass to try the club first.
  • Reserve popular group classes early through the member app.
