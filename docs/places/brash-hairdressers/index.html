About Brash Hairdressers

Brash Hairdressers is a family-owned salon on Webb Bridge Way run by Brian and Ashley Walters, who both started with Toni & Guy in Dallas in the 1990s. They moved to Atlanta in 2000 and opened Toni & Guy locations including North Point, which later became one of the first franchised Toni & Guy salons in the US.

In 2018 the team moved from North Point to a boutique space on Webb Bridge Way, and in 2019 the salon rebranded as Brash. The stylists have taught and demonstrated internationally and have won photographic awards.

Services include wash, cut and blowdry, updos, color, highlights, ombre and balayage, fashion color and Coppola keratin smoothing, which requires a consultation. Pricing is tiered by stylist level, from designer to senior creative director. Walk-ins are welcome and consultations are complimentary. The salon is open seven days except Tuesday.
