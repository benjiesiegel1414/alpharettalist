About Holcomb Bridge Middle School

Fulton County Schools public middle school for grades 6-8 on Holcomb Bridge Road near the Alpharetta and Roswell line. Students have access to counseling, talented and gifted services, school clubs, athletics and fine arts programs.

Holcomb Bridge Middle School is along McGinnis Ferry Road on Alpharetta's east side, near the Johns Creek line. Browse more schools in our Alpharetta directory.
