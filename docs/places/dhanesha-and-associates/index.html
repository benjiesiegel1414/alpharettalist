About Dhanesha & Associates

Dhanesha & Associates is a CPA and financial planning firm at 11175 Cicero Drive in Alpharetta, serving individuals and small businesses. Its team holds CPA, EA, CFP, PFS and CFA credentials along with MBAs, and several members have practiced in tax and public accounting since the early 2000s.

Services fall into three areas. Tax work covers planning and preparation aimed at keeping current and future taxes as low as the law allows. Accounting covers bookkeeping and advisory support for growing businesses. Financial planning covers retirement, investments, taxes and estate planning for households.

The firm works on a fee-only basis and offers year-round consulting rather than seasonal tax help alone. It emphasizes being easy to reach by phone, and new clients can start with a free consultation.
