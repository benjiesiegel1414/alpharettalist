About Kilwins Halcyon

Kilwins at Halcyon is the district's go-to dessert stop for ice cream, fudge, caramel apples and chocolates. It is a natural last stop after dinner at one of Halcyon's restaurants or an afternoon on the Village Green with the kids.

It is open daily from 11am, until 9pm Sunday through Thursday and 10pm on Friday and Saturday. Lines can stretch out the door on warm weekend evenings, so plan on a short wait.

Boxed chocolates and fudge make easy gifts during the holidays. Kilwins also has a shop in Downtown Alpharetta on Market Street.

Kilwins Halcyon is at Halcyon, the walkable shopping and dining district at GA 400 exit 12, just north of Alpharetta. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Dessert dining scene.

Visiting Halcyon

Halcyon is a 135-acre walkable district at GA 400 exit 12, just over the line in south Forsyth County with an Alpharetta mailing address. Parking is free in the six-level deck, public and family restrooms are in the Market Hall across from the Village Green, and a trail extension near Halcyon Way and Ronald Reagan Parkway connects to the Big Creek Greenway.
