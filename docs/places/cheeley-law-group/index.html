About Cheeley Law Group

Cheeley Law Group is a trial-focused personal injury firm based on Old Milton Parkway in Alpharetta, representing injured people and families across metro Atlanta and Georgia. Founder and managing partner Robert D. Cheeley was admitted to the Georgia bar in 1982 after earning his undergraduate and law degrees at the University of Georgia.

The firm is best known for complex truck accident litigation, and it also handles car, motorcycle and pedestrian crashes, autonomous vehicle accidents, defective products, premises liability, medical malpractice, catastrophic injuries, wrongful death and select business tort matters. The firm says it intentionally keeps a limited caseload so clients work directly with an attorney from the start.

The office is in a professional building on Old Milton Parkway. Prospective clients can request a free case analysis by phone or through the firm's website.

Visiting Old Milton Pkwy

Old Milton Parkway is the east-west corridor that ties GA 400 to Avalon and downtown. It's lined with shopping centers, restaurants and offices, and most stops have their own parking lots. Traffic is heaviest at rush hour near the GA 400 interchange.
