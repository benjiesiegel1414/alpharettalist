About Inner Compass Counseling

Inner Compass Counseling is a group therapy practice with an Alpharetta office in Suite 227 at 5755 North Point Parkway, plus offices in Cumming and Marietta. Its therapists work with children ages 5 to 12, teens, young adults ages 18 to 30, individual adults, couples and families.

Specialties listed by the practice include EMDR, Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), and support for anxiety and depression during pregnancy and postpartum. The practice describes therapy as a way to set goals, look at past patterns and build tools for handling life's challenges.

Sessions are available in person or through virtual counseling. Appointments are requested through the website form or by calling (770) 655-2484, and self-pay clients can ask for a Good Faith Estimate of costs.

Inner Compass Counseling is in the North Point corridor along North Point Parkway, near the mall and GA 400 exit 10. Browse more health and medical in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Point

The North Point area runs along North Point Parkway near GA 400, anchored by North Point Mall and lined with shopping centers, restaurants and medical offices. The Big Creek Greenway trail runs nearby, and Ameris Bank Amphitheatre is a short drive west. Expect easy parking but busy traffic around the mall and GA 400 interchanges at rush hour.
