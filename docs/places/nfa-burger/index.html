About NFA Burger

NFA Burger, short for Not Fooling Around, is Billy Kramer's burger brand that first drew a following from a counter inside a Dunwoody Chevron station. The Avalon restaurant at 3180 Avalon Boulevard opened in May 2025 in a space roughly ten times the size of the original.

The draw is the smashed burger, with Billy's Classic stacking two patties, American cheese, pickles, mustard and house Sassy Sauce, and options for more patties and toppings such as hatch green chiles or bacon. There are also hot dogs, fries and tots fried in beef tallow, a kids menu and frozen custard milkshakes, floats and sundaes, plus Liege waffles.

You order at the counter and staff bring food to your table inside or on the outdoor seating facing the courtyard by the Regal theater. A full bar serves drinks, and Avalon's decks offer free parking. There are no reservations.

NFA Burger is at Avalon, Alpharetta's upscale mixed-use shopping and dining district on Old Milton Parkway. It is one of 111 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Burgers dining scene.

Visiting Avalon

Avalon is an 86-acre walkable district off Old Milton Parkway with restaurants, shops, a movie theater, a hotel and a central plaza. Parking is in decks throughout the property, and on busy weekend evenings it pays to arrive a little early. The plaza hosts seasonal events, including an ice rink in winter, and the Alpha Loop connects Avalon with Downtown Alpharetta about a mile away.

Good to know

  • Expect a line at peak weekend lunch and dinner, since there are no reservations.
  • Order a side of tots, a house favorite.
  • Park free in the Avalon decks.
