About Sushi Sumo

Sushi Sumo is a neighborhood sushi and pan-Asian restaurant at Old Alabama Square, the shopping center at 3005 Old Alabama Rd, Suite 440, anchored by The Fresh Market. Its storefront sits at the end of the center, and the dining room inside is small and fills up at lunchtime.

The sushi bar turns out classic rolls such as spicy tuna, rainbow and shrimp avocado, along with a super crunch roll topped with shrimp, tempura flakes and spicy mayo. Lunch from the sushi bar includes a maki lunch of two rolls with miso soup or salad, and the kitchen side of the menu covers Chinese and Thai dishes too. A 2017 Atlanta Journal-Constitution review summed it up as no frills, just good sushi, praising fresh fish and quick, friendly service.

It is a casual pick for sushi in the Old Alabama Road area. Online ordering for pickup and delivery is available through the restaurant's website.

Sushi Sumo is along McGinnis Ferry Road on Alpharetta's east side, near the Johns Creek line. It is one of 119 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Sushi dining scene.

Visiting McGinnis Ferry

McGinnis Ferry Road runs along Alpharetta's northeast edge near the Johns Creek and Forsyth County lines. It's a busy commuter route with shopping centers, medical offices and neighborhoods on both sides.

Good to know

  • Plan around Tuesdays, when it is closed.
  • Sunday service starts mid-afternoon.
  • Try the maki lunch at midday for two rolls with soup or salad.
