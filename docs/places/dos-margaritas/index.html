About Dos Margaritas

Casual, family-friendly Mexican restaurant in Crabapple known for scratch-made dishes and handcrafted margaritas. Popular orders include street tacos, fajitas, carne asada, shrimp bowls and chimichangas, with vegetarian-friendly options, outdoor patio seating, takeout and delivery.

Dos Margaritas is in the Crabapple area on Alpharetta's west side, near the Milton line. It is one of 109 places to eat and drink in our Alpharetta restaurant guide, and part of the city's Mexican dining scene.

Good to know

  • Grab a patio table on nice evenings.
  • Takeout and delivery are available for family dinners at home.
