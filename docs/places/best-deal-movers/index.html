About Best Deal Movers

Best Deal Movers is a family-owned and veteran-owned moving company with its office on Shiloh Road East, in the Alpharetta area near McGinnis Ferry Road. It serves Alpharetta, Milton, Roswell, Johns Creek, Cumming and other North Metro Atlanta communities, plus longer moves across the Southeast.

Services include local moving, long-distance moving, packing and unpacking, office and commercial moves, moving boxes and supplies, and junk removal for appliances, furniture and boxes. The company offers video-based quotes and says its crews are trained, background checked, licensed and insured.

The office keeps weekday hours plus a short Saturday morning window, which is a good time to ask about supplies or schedule an estimate. Quote requests can also be submitted online with move date and home size.

Best Deal Movers is along McGinnis Ferry Road on Alpharetta's east side, near the Johns Creek line. Browse more home services in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting McGinnis Ferry

McGinnis Ferry Road runs along Alpharetta's northeast edge near the Johns Creek and Forsyth County lines. It's a busy commuter route with shopping centers, medical offices and neighborhoods on both sides.

Good to know

  • Request a video-based quote to skip an in-home walkthrough.
  • Buy boxes and packing supplies from the company ahead of your move date.
  • Call on a weekday or Saturday morning; the office is closed Sundays.
