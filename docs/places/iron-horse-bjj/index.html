About Iron Horse BJJ

Iron Horse BJJ is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu gym on Alpharetta Highway led by head instructor Anthony Budion, a 3rd-degree jiu-jitsu black belt and MMA practitioner. He trained in New York under former UFC champion Matt Serra and coach Ray Longo, and he is a U.S. Air Force veteran who also served with the NYPD and FDNY.

The schedule includes adult gi and no-gi classes at all levels, fundamentals classes, wrestling and takedowns, and kickboxing and fitness sessions. Kids can start at age 4 in a short gi class, and children 8 and up can join fundamentals sessions alongside adults.

Classes run Monday through Saturday, with midday options for adults and evening classes for families. Memberships include unlimited and twice-weekly plans with military and family discounts, and a free trial is available.

Iron Horse BJJ is in the Haynes Bridge Road area near GA 400. Browse more fitness and gyms in our Alpharetta directory.
