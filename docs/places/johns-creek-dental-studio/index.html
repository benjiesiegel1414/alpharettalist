About Johns Creek Dental Studio

Johns Creek Dental Studio is a patient-focused dental practice in Alpharetta, GA, led by Dr. Arjun V. Parikh, DMD. We provide preventive, family, cosmetic, restorative, and emergency dental care for children, teens, adults, and families. Our services include dental exams, cleanings, teeth whitening, veneers, Invisalign, dental implants, crowns, and fillings. What makes our practice different is our personalized approach, clear communication, modern dental technology, and welcoming, judgment-free environment. We take time to understand each patient's concerns, explain treatment options, and help them make informed decisions about their oral health. New patients are welcome. We proudly serve Alpharetta and nearby communities, including Johns Creek, Milton, and Roswell.
