About Rock Mill Park

Rock Mill Park is a 20-acre city park on Kimball Bridge Road that serves as one of the main trailheads for the Big Creek Greenway. With on-site parking and restrooms, it is a practical place to start a walk, run or ride along the paved greenway.

The park is known for its green roof pavilion, and a smaller group pavilion is available first-come, first-served for family gatherings and celebrations. An outdoor classroom supports environmental education programs.

Because it is smaller and more natural than the city's sports parks, Rock Mill suits picnics and quiet time outdoors as much as greenway trips. From the trailhead you can head along Big Creek toward other access points such as Haynes Bridge Park or Northpoint Park.

Rock Mill Park is along McGinnis Ferry Road on Alpharetta's east side, near the Johns Creek line. Browse more parks and trails in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting McGinnis Ferry

McGinnis Ferry Road runs along Alpharetta's northeast edge near the Johns Creek and Forsyth County lines. It's a busy commuter route with shopping centers, medical offices and neighborhoods on both sides.
