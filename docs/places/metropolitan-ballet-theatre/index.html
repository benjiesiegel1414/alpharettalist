About Metropolitan Ballet Theatre

Metropolitan Ballet Theatre is a ballet school and performing company based on North Main Street in Alpharetta. It is best known locally for its full-length Nutcracker, set to Tchaikovsky's original score, which marks 20 years as a holiday tradition this season and is staged at Blessed Trinity High School in Roswell.

Students who want to train with MBT can audition year-round, either by attending an open class or by special appointment. Open classes offer barre and center work for dancers 13 and up who want extra training time, held weekday afternoons with no audition required. The school also offers adult ballet and dance-based fitness classes for mature students, including beginners and former dancers.

Nutcracker performances include evening shows, matinees and a Sugarplum Storytime matinee that pairs the ballet with a storytime for young children.

Metropolitan Ballet Theatre is on North Main Street, just up Highway 9 from Downtown Alpharetta. Browse more kids and classes in our Alpharetta directory.

Visiting North Main St

North Main Street (Highway 9) runs north from Downtown Alpharetta toward Milton. Businesses here mostly sit in shopping centers with their own parking, a quick drive from the restaurants and shops of downtown.
