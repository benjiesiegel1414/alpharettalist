About Alpharetta List

An independent local guide to Alpharetta, Georgia.

Alpharetta List is an independent local guide to Alpharetta, Georgia, built for the people who live, work and visit here.

Alpharetta has one of the best downtowns in metro Atlanta, a nationally known dining and shopping district at Avalon, miles of greenway, a packed events calendar and hundreds of technology companies, but there has never been one simple place that brings it all together. That is what we are building: local news in plain English, honest guides, an events calendar and a directory of 455 restaurants, shops, services and places to go across the 30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022 ZIP codes.

Who runs Alpharetta List

Alpharetta List was founded by Ben Siegel, a self-taught entrepreneur who builds and runs a small network of independent websites, including Wellington List, a local guide to Wellington, Florida. He started Alpharetta List to give Alpharetta the same kind of useful, no-nonsense local guide: one place to check what's new, what's happening this weekend and who to call. Ben oversees the news stories, guides and business directory.

What you'll find here

  • Local news and guides on city decisions, new openings, schools, roads, seasonal events and the best places to eat and play.
  • An events calendar with dates, times, venues and prices for festivals, concerts, markets and holiday events.
  • A business directory with addresses, hours, phone numbers and practical tips for restaurants, things to do and local services.

How we research

News stories and guides are based on official sources such as the City of Alpharetta, Fulton County, Fulton County Schools and the State of Georgia, along with public records and local news reporting. We link to our sources at the end of every story. Directory details are checked against each business's own website or reliable public listings, and listing pages show when the details were last checked. We use software tools, including AI tools, to help gather and organize information and draft summaries, and we check facts against the sources before anything is published.

Our editorial standards

  • Sourced facts. If we can't confirm a detail, we leave it out.
  • Both sides. When there's a local debate, we explain what supporters and critics say, using their own words where we can, and we don't take sides.
  • Corrections. When facts change or we get something wrong, we update the page and add a dated note to news stories. Send corrections to hello@alpharettalist.com
  • Independence. Basic listings are free. Advertising and sponsorships are clearly labeled, and advertisers never pay for coverage in our news stories or guides. A listing is not an endorsement.
