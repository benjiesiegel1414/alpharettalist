Avalon Parking, Hours and Directions

Where to park at Avalon for free, how valet works, center hours and how to get there.

OverviewRestaurantsShoppingThings to doParking and hoursChristmas at AvalonWith kidsDog friendlyHotels

Parking at Avalon is free. Self-parking costs nothing every day in the decks and surface lots on the north and south sides of the property, valet is $12, and there are metered spots along Avalon Boulevard for quick stops. Here is how parking works, where to enter, Avalon's hours and the easiest way to get there.

Free self-parking

Avalon's parking decks and surface lots sit on the north and south sides of the property, and self-parking in all of them is free every day. Entrances are off Old Milton Parkway and Westside Parkway. The decks put you a short walk from the Boulevard, so for any visit longer than a quick errand they are the best choice.

Avalon's online map, posted on its website, shows where each deck sits relative to the Boulevard, so check it before you go if you are headed to one specific store or restaurant.

Valet: $12

Valet costs $12 at three stations:

  • Avalon Boulevard and 2nd Street, near the concierge
  • Avalon Boulevard and 3rd Street, near Oak Steakhouse
  • The Hotel at Avalon

Valet does not take oversized vehicles or motorcycles. On rainy nights or when you are dressed up for dinner, it is worth the $12.

Street meters and EV charging

Metered spots along Avalon Boulevard are handy for a quick pickup, but they are paid, so use a deck for anything longer. Avalon also lists EV charging stations in its parking areas among its amenities.

Stores may set their own hours, and restaurants follow their own schedules. Many restaurants open for lunch at 11am and stay open until 10pm or later on Friday and Saturday; Kona Grill and Jeni's stay open until 1am on weekends. See the Avalon restaurant guide for each one's hours. Whole Foods is open daily from 7am to 10pm.

Holiday hours vary. Call the concierge at (770) 765-1000 before visiting on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve or New Year's Day. Avalon on Ice keeps its own hours, with extended 10am to 10pm sessions over Thanksgiving week and winter break.

Curfew for teens

Avalon's code of conduct says visitors under 17 must be with a parent or legal guardian after 8pm, and visitors under 21 are not permitted after 10pm. Skateboards, hoverboards and bike riding on the sidewalks are not allowed, and smoking is limited to designated areas.

Directions

Address: 400 Avalon Blvd, Alpharetta, GA 30009.

  • From Atlanta: take GA 400 north to exit 10, Old Milton Parkway. Turn left (west) onto Old Milton Parkway, and Avalon is on your right. Turn right on 2nd Street for valet, or follow signs to the decks.
  • From Cumming and Forsyth County: take GA 400 south to exit 10 and turn right onto Old Milton Parkway. Avalon is on your right.
  • From Downtown Alpharetta: walk or bike the paved Alpha Loop, about a mile, or drive west on Old Milton Parkway.
  • Rideshare: set your drop-off to 400 Avalon Blvd or the restaurant you are visiting. Rideshare is the easiest choice on event nights.

Parking on busy days

The decks fill quickly on a few nights each year, especially the Lighting of Avalon on Sunday, November 22, 2026. For that event, Avalon recommends carpooling or rideshare, with valet on 3rd Street and shuttles from Gwinnett Technical College across the street. December weekends and Saturday evenings in general are also busy, so arrive early or plan to walk from the far side of a deck.

Concierge services

The concierge desk near 2nd Street lends strollers and wheelchairs for free, checks shopping bags, sells gift cards, charges phones and keeps umbrellas, blankets, water and first aid on hand. Security can help with a dead car battery. Call (770) 765-1000.

Frequently asked questions

Is parking free at Avalon?

Yes. Self-parking is free every day in Avalon's decks and surface lots on the north and south sides of the property. Street meters along Avalon Boulevard are paid.

How much is valet at Avalon?

Valet is $12. Stations are at Avalon Boulevard and 2nd Street near the concierge, Avalon Boulevard and 3rd Street near Oak Steakhouse, and The Hotel at Avalon. Oversized vehicles and motorcycles cannot be valeted.

Does Avalon have EV charging?

Yes. Avalon lists EV charging stations in its parking areas among its amenities.

What exit is Avalon off GA 400?

Take GA 400 exit 10 for Old Milton Parkway. Turn west onto Old Milton Parkway and Avalon is on the right; turn right onto 2nd Street for valet or follow signs to the parking decks.
