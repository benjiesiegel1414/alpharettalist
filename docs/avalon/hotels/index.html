Hotels at and Near Avalon

Stay on the Boulevard or a short walk away, with tips for event weekends.

OverviewRestaurantsShoppingThings to doParking and hoursChristmas at AvalonWith kidsDog friendlyHotels

If you want to stay close to Avalon's restaurants, shops and holiday events, you have three easy choices: The Hotel at Avalon on the Boulevard itself, EVEN Hotel Alpharetta next door on Old Milton Parkway, and the Courtyard Alpharetta Avalon Area a few minutes away. Here is how they compare and when to book.

The Hotel at Avalon

The Hotel at Avalon is the only hotel on the property, with 330 rooms and suites steps from the Boulevard. It connects directly to the Alpharetta Conference Center, which has 44,000 square feet of event space, including 10,000 square feet for exhibits, so it hosts many of the area's conferences, galas and weddings.

South City Kitchen, the Southern restaurant from the Atlanta favorite, is downstairs, serving breakfast on weekdays, a popular weekend brunch and dinner. During the holidays, the hotel hosts Santa Tuck-Ins (a room add-on from November 20 to December 23), the Jingle Jog on December 13 and Noon Year's Eve on December 31. See Christmas at Avalon.

Parking: valet is available at the hotel, and Avalon's self-parking decks are free.

EVEN Hotel Alpharetta

EVEN Hotel Alpharetta is an IHG hotel at 2715 Old Milton Parkway, right next to Avalon. The EVEN brand is built around wellness, with workout space and healthier food options. It is close enough to walk to dinner at Avalon and usually costs less than staying on the Boulevard.

Courtyard Alpharetta Avalon Area

The Courtyard by Marriott on Amber Park Drive has 115 rooms on six floors, The Bistro for breakfast and dinner, an indoor pool and a 24-hour fitness center. It is a short drive to Avalon and convenient to the Westside Parkway office parks.

Staying elsewhere in Alpharetta

  • Downtown Alpharetta: The Hamilton Alpharetta at 25 Milton Ave puts you near Main Street's restaurants and about a mile from Avalon on the Alpha Loop. See our downtown restaurant guide.
  • North Point: several hotels along North Point Parkway, including Embassy Suites Atlanta Alpharetta, are a short drive east across GA 400.
  • Halcyon: Embassy Suites Alpharetta Halcyon sits inside Halcyon at GA 400 exit 12, about 15 minutes north. See our Halcyon guide.

When to book early

  • The Lighting of Avalon weekend (November 22, 2026).
  • December weekends, when Avalon on Ice and Santa visits draw families from across metro Atlanta.
  • Concert nights at Ameris Bank Amphitheatre, just north of downtown, in spring through fall.
  • Graduation and big conference weeks at the Alpharetta Conference Center.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a hotel at Avalon?

Yes. The Hotel at Avalon has 330 rooms and suites on the property, connected to the 44,000-square-foot Alpharetta Conference Center, with South City Kitchen downstairs.

What hotels are walking distance to Avalon?

The Hotel at Avalon is on the property. EVEN Hotel Alpharetta is next door at 2715 Old Milton Parkway, and the Courtyard Alpharetta Avalon Area is a short drive away on Amber Park Drive.
