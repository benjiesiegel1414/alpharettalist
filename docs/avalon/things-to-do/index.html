Things to Do at Avalon

Movies, games, the plaza, spa days, workouts, markets and seasonal events at Avalon.

OverviewRestaurantsShoppingThings to doParking and hoursChristmas at AvalonWith kidsDog friendlyHotels

Avalon is more than a place to shop. Between a 12-screen theater, private game rooms, spa and fitness studios, a plaza with a play area and a dog park, a seasonal ice rink and a trail that runs to Downtown Alpharetta, it is easy to fill an afternoon or a whole day. Here are 20 things to do at Avalon, from free to splurge.

Movies and games

  1. Catch a movie at Regal Avalon. The 12-screen theater at 3950 1st St has an RPX premium auditorium. Buy tickets ahead for opening weekends.
  2. Book a room at Immersive Gamebox. Private, projection-mapped game rooms for groups, with 60-minute sessions that work for birthdays, date nights and team outings. It is open from 11am daily.
  3. Go back to the Boulevard after dark. Avalon is at its liveliest on Friday and Saturday evenings, when restaurant patios fill and the string lights come on.

On the plaza and outdoors

  1. Hang out on The Plaza. Avalon's central plaza has seating and is the stage for many of its events; Palmer Plaza and Ron's Place add more outdoor seating under the lights.
  2. Let kids run at the children's play area and ride the Avalon Express train. See Avalon with kids.
  3. Bring the dog. Avalon is dog friendly, with a fenced dog park behind Building 1000. See Avalon with your dog.
  4. Walk or bike the Alpha Loop to Downtown Alpharetta. The paved loop connects Avalon to City Center in about a mile. Pair it with dinner in our downtown restaurant guide.
  5. Book a picnic in the plaza. The concierge helps arrange Picnic in the Plaza setups for special occasions.
  6. Do the Squirrel Scavenger Hunt. Pick up a free map and clue sheet at the concierge, a simple way to keep kids busy while you stroll.
  7. Book a photo session. Avalon's "Moments on the Boulevard" are 45-minute photo sessions for $50, Friday through Sunday, booked 72 hours ahead through the concierge.

Spa, beauty and fitness

  1. Spend an afternoon at The Woodhouse Day Spa for massage, wraps and an infrared sauna.
  2. Take a class. Club Pilates runs reformer classes for all levels, and [solidcore] teaches 50-minute low-impact resistance classes starting at 5:30am on weekdays.
  3. Recover at Restore Hyper Wellness with cryotherapy, infrared sauna or IV therapy.
  4. Get your nails or hair done at Lux Nail Lounge, Drybar or Van Michael Salon before a night out.

Food and drink experiences

  1. Watch the sunset from The Cape's rooftop. The Cape is one of Avalon's best spots for a drink with a view.
  2. Do a dessert crawl from Five Daughters Bakery to Le Macaron to Jeni's, which stays open until 1am on weekends.
  3. Taste wine and cheese at Crú on its people-watching patio. Full list in our Avalon restaurant guide.

Markets and seasonal events

  1. Shop the Makers Market. Avalon's outdoor local artist fair runs on select dates from March through December, with the last 2026 market on December 12.
  2. Skate at Avalon on Ice. The outdoor rink runs November 22, 2026 through January 18, 2027. Tickets are $18, or $14 for kids 12 and under, skates included. See our Avalon on Ice guide.
  3. See the Lighting of Avalon and visit Santa. The free tree lighting is November 22, and Santa's cottage is open for photos through Christmas Eve. Details in Christmas at Avalon.

Avalon also runs Gameday at Avalon through the fall and Nancy's Flower Truck pop-ups through November. For events across the city, check the Alpharetta events calendar.

Planning tips

  • Parking is free in the decks; see our Avalon parking guide.
  • Teens should know the curfew: under 17 needs a parent after 8pm, and under 21 is not permitted after 10pm.
  • Rain plan: Regal Avalon, Immersive Gamebox, a spa visit or a long lunch.
  • Looking for more? Our things to do in Alpharetta list covers parks, trails and day trips, and Halcyon has indoor golf and a greenway 15 minutes north.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do at Avalon besides shopping?

Avalon has the 12-screen Regal Avalon theater, Immersive Gamebox, a plaza with seating and a children's play area, a dog park, spa and fitness studios, the Makers Market local artist fair, and Avalon on Ice each winter.

Does Avalon have a movie theater?

Yes. Regal Avalon is a 12-screen theater at 3950 1st St with an RPX premium auditorium. Tickets are sold through Regal and Fandango.

Can you walk from Avalon to Downtown Alpharetta?

Yes. The paved Alpha Loop connects Avalon to Downtown Alpharetta in about a mile, making it an easy walk or bike ride.
