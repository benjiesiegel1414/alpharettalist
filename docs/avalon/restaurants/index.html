Avalon Restaurants: Where to Eat at Avalon

All 26 restaurants, cafes and dessert shops at Avalon, with hours, phone numbers and our picks.

Avalon has 26 restaurants, cafes and dessert shops packed along the Boulevard and its side streets, and they range from dry-aged steaks and Persian kabobs to Neapolitan pizza and late-night ice cream. Here is every one, grouped by occasion, with current hours and phone numbers and our picks for each.

Quick picks

  • Special occasion: Oak Steakhouse or Colletta
  • Local favorite: Rumi's Kitchen
  • Rooftop and views: The Cape or the rooftop bar at Barleygarden
  • Brunch: South City Kitchen or Superica
  • With kids: Antico Pizza or Ted's Montana Grill
  • Healthy and quick: True Food Kitchen or Gusto!
  • Dessert: Jeni's, Five Daughters or Cafe Intermezzo

Avalon's dining directory also includes a Chick-fil-A, a Starbucks and Boba Craze for bubble tea. Hours change on holidays, so call ahead on those days.

Special occasions and date night

Oak Steakhouse on Third Street is the Charleston-born steakhouse known for dry-aged beef, Wagyu and an extensive wine list. It serves dinner only, from 5pm on weeknights and 4pm on Sunday, and it is the most-requested anniversary table at Avalon.

Colletta, also on Third Street, brings Northern Italian cooking with wood-fired pizza and house-made pasta to a lively dining room. It adds a weekend brunch on Saturday and Sunday from 11am to 2:30pm.

Rumi's Kitchen helped make Avalon a food destination. The Persian menu centers on juicy kabobs, fragrant rice and hummus, and it stays open until 11pm on Friday and Saturday.

The Cape is a Cape Town-inspired coastal restaurant with South African dishes and wines, a sunset rooftop and a crab dip locals rave about. MF Bar serves sushi, nigiri and robata around a U-shaped bar with a deep sake list, and Thai Candle Seafood & Bar is an upscale but relaxed Thai restaurant built around fresh seafood.

Casual dinners and groups

Antico Pizza is the Avalon outpost of Atlanta's famous Neapolitan pizzeria; it closes when the dough runs out, so do not show up at the last minute. Superica is Ford Fry's Tex-Mex cantina, with queso fundido, sizzling fajitas and a popular weekend brunch. Barleygarden pours more than 60 craft beers with burgers and a dog-friendly patio, and Branch & Barrel is the spot for craft cocktails and its corn fried lobster sandwich.

For familiar crowd-pleasers, Ted's Montana Grill does bison burgers and comfort plates, Marlow's Tavern is a reliable neighborhood tavern, and Kona Grill serves sushi and American dishes until 1am on weekends. Crú Food & Wine Bar is best for a glass and a cheese flight on the patio.

Quick and healthy

True Food Kitchen covers bowls, salads and vegan options, and Gusto! serves grilled bowls and wraps from 10:30am daily. NFA Burger, the cult-favorite burger counter that started as a pop-up, opened its second location here in 2025, and Chick-fil-A is on site for the kids.

Brunch at Avalon

  • South City Kitchen: Saturday and Sunday, 8am to 2pm, inside The Hotel at Avalon. Fried chicken, biscuits and shrimp and grits.
  • Superica: Saturday and Sunday, 10am to 3pm.
  • Colletta: Saturday and Sunday, 11am to 2:30pm.
  • True Food Kitchen: opens at 10:30am on weekends.

For more options around town, see our Alpharetta brunch guide.

Coffee and dessert

Cafe Intermezzo is a Paris-style coffeehouse with towering dessert cases, open until midnight on Friday and Saturday. Caffè Antico, from the Antico team, pours espresso and scoops gelato. Five Daughters Bakery is known for its croissant-style donuts, Le Macaron sells colorful French macarons, and Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams stays open until 1am on weekends.

Tips

  • Reserve for Friday and Saturday dinner at Oak, Colletta, Rumi's, The Cape and South City Kitchen. The Avalon concierge at (770) 765-1000 can also request reservations.
  • Park free in the decks, or pay $12 for valet at 2nd Street or 3rd Street.
  • In winter, pair dinner with a skate at Avalon on Ice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best restaurant at Avalon?

For a special occasion, Oak Steakhouse and Colletta are the most popular reservations. Rumi's Kitchen is a local favorite for Persian kabobs, The Cape has a sunset rooftop, and Antico Pizza is the pick for a casual meal.

Which Avalon restaurants serve brunch?

South City Kitchen serves brunch Saturday and Sunday from 8am to 2pm, Superica from 10am to 3pm, and Colletta from 11am to 2:30pm. True Food Kitchen opens at 10:30am on weekends.

What is open late at Avalon?

Kona Grill stays open until 1am on Friday and Saturday, Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams until 1am on weekends, and Cafe Intermezzo until midnight on Friday and Saturday.

Do I need reservations at Avalon restaurants?

For Friday and Saturday dinner, yes at Oak Steakhouse, Colletta, Rumi's Kitchen, The Cape and South City Kitchen. Avalon's concierge at (770) 765-1000 can also help request reservations.
