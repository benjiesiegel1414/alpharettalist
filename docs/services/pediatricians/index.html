Best Pediatricians in Alpharetta, GA

Local pediatricians serving Alpharetta and North Fulton: well-child visits, vaccines, sick visits and sports physicals.

Looking for the best pediatricians in Alpharetta, Georgia? Here are 7 local providers for well-child visits, vaccines, sick visits and sports physicals in and around Alpharetta, with addresses and phone numbers where available.

Tips for hiring pediatricians in Alpharetta

  • Call ahead to confirm the practice is accepting new patients and takes your insurance
  • Ask about same-day sick visits and after-hours phone lines
  • Schedule back-to-school and sports physicals early in the summer

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best pediatricians in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 7 options for well-child visits, vaccines, sick visits and sports physicals.

Do these pediatricians serve all of Alpharetta?

Most serve all Alpharetta ZIP codes, including 30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022, plus nearby Milton, Roswell and Johns Creek. Call to confirm they cover your address.

Primary care

South Forsyth Family Medicine and Pediatrics

Wellstar-affiliated practice on Lockeway Drive that combines family medicine and pediatrics under one roof, so parents and kids can see doctors in the same office. The team includes family physicians Dr. David Boaz and Dr. Mark Glodener plus pediatricians Dr. Giouzel Glodener and Dr. Jackleen Glodener.

World of Pediatrics

Independent pediatric practice caring for newborns, infants, children and teens, now located on North Point Parkway. Services include well-child and sick visits, immunizations, school physicals, newborn care and chronic condition management, and it serves families from Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Milton, Roswell, Cumming and nearby areas.

North Point Pediatrics

General pediatric practice in Suite 410 at 3180 North Point Parkway, in the office corridor near North Point Mall. It provides routine well visits, sick care and vaccinations for infants, children and teens from Alpharetta and nearby north Fulton communities.

Roswell Pediatric Center - Alpharetta

Longtime pediatric group with an Alpharetta office in Suite 200 on Haynes Bridge Road, despite the Roswell name. The practice also runs offices in Crabapple and Cumming, and its pediatricians and nurse practitioners rotate through Saturday morning sick visits held only at this Alpharetta location.

Harmony Pediatrics

Direct primary care pediatric practice led by Dr. Shefali Chheda. Families pay a membership instead of co-pays or insurance billing, and in return get direct access to the pediatrician, including after-hours contact, for routine and sick care.

Pediatric ENT of Atlanta

Pediatric ear, nose and throat practice in Suite 275 at 3300 Old Milton Parkway, focused on infants, children and teens. Physicians listed for this office include pediatric otolaryngologists Dr. Erik Bauer and Dr. Megan Ballard and otolaryngologist Dr. Paula Harmon.
