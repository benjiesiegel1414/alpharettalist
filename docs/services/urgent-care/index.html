Using an urgent care clinic in Alpharetta: what to know

Urgent care clinics in Alpharetta fill the gap between your regular doctor and the emergency room. They are commonly used for minor illnesses and injuries such as sore throats, ear infections, minor cuts, sprains and rashes, especially in the evening or on weekends when a primary care office is closed. Spring pollen season and the winter cold and flu months tend to be the busiest times. Some clinics treat children of all ages while others have age limits, so check before you go if you are bringing a young child.

Know when urgent care is not the right place. Chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding, serious head injuries or a very sick infant call for 911 or an emergency department. A nearby hospital emergency department is Wellstar North Fulton Medical Center on Hospital Boulevard in Roswell, which is a verified Level II trauma center. If you are unsure, call your doctor's nurse line or ask the urgent care staff by phone before driving over.

Before you need one, find out which clinics near home and work accept your insurance and whether they offer online check in to reduce waiting. Check whether the clinic has on site X-ray and lab testing, since that can save a second trip. Physicians working at urgent care centers are licensed by the Georgia Composite Medical Board, and you can look them up online. After a visit, ask the clinic to send records to your primary care doctor so your file stays complete.

Tips for hiring urgent care clinics in Alpharetta

  • Urgent care handles minor injuries, illnesses, stitches and X-rays

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best urgent care clinics in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 6 options for walk-in care for minor injuries and illnesses, with X-ray and labs.

When should I go to the ER instead of urgent care?

Call 911 or go to an emergency department for chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding or serious injuries. Urgent care is meant for minor illnesses and injuries.

Where is a hospital emergency room near Alpharetta?

Wellstar North Fulton Medical Center at 3000 Hospital Boulevard in Roswell has an emergency department and is a Level II trauma center.

Do these urgent care clinics serve all of Alpharetta?
