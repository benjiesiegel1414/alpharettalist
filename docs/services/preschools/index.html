Best Preschools and Daycares in Alpharetta, GA

Local preschools and daycares serving Alpharetta and North Fulton: preschool, pre-K, Montessori and early learning programs.

Looking for the best preschools and daycares in Alpharetta, Georgia? Here are 6 local providers for preschool, pre-K, Montessori and early learning programs in and around Alpharetta, with addresses and phone numbers where available.

Choosing a preschool or daycare in Alpharetta: what to know

Alpharetta families can choose from licensed child care centers, church affiliated and private preschools, Montessori programs, family child care homes and Georgia's Pre-K classrooms. Start with logistics: hours that match your work schedule, location relative to home or commute, and whether you need full day care or a part day program. Waiting lists are common for infant rooms and popular programs in North Fulton, so many parents begin touring several months before they need care.

Child care in Georgia is overseen by the Department of Early Care and Learning, also called DECAL or Bright from the Start. Its online provider search lets you find licensed programs and review monitoring visit reports, which is one of the most useful checks you can make. Look for Quality Rated status as well. It is a voluntary rating system, and providers that take part earn one to three stars, with three stars being the highest level. Georgia's Pre-K Program is offered at no cost to families for children who turn four on or before September 1 and live in Georgia. It is available in public schools and private centers, runs about 6.5 hours a day on a school calendar, and each provider sets its own application dates.

When touring, ask about teacher to child ratios, staff training and turnover, the daily schedule, how naps, meals and allergies are handled, and how the program communicates with parents. Watch how teachers talk with children. Red flags include programs that discourage unannounced visits, unclear sick child policies, or a monitoring history with repeated issues. Keep immunization and health forms current, since Pre-K requires Georgia DPH Forms 3231 and 3300 soon after enrollment.

Tips for hiring preschools and daycares in Alpharetta

  • Tour at drop-off or pickup time to see the real routine
  • Ask about teacher ratios, turnover and curriculum approach
  • Waitlists are common, so start looking six months or more ahead

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best preschools and daycares in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 6 options for preschool, pre-K, Montessori and early learning programs.

Is Georgia Pre-K free?

Yes. Georgia's Pre-K Program is lottery funded and offered at no cost to families for Georgia children who turn four on or before September 1. Spaces are limited and each provider sets its own application process.

How do I check a daycare's inspection history in Georgia?

Use the provider search on DECAL's website, which shows licensed programs, monitoring visit reports and Quality Rated status.

How old must a child be for kindergarten in Fulton County Schools?

A child must be five years old by September 1 to enroll in kindergarten in Fulton County Schools, with limited exceptions for some students moving from other states.

Do these preschools and daycares serve all of Alpharetta?
