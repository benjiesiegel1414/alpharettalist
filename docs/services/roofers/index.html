Hiring a roofer in Alpharetta: what to know

Roofs in Alpharetta take a beating from summer heat and humidity, heavy thunderstorms, occasional hail and wind, and the odd winter ice storm. In neighborhoods built around the same time, entire streets can reach replacement age together, and storms can bring a wave of door to door contractors. If you live in a community with a homeowners association, check the covenants before you choose shingles, since many HOAs require approval for roof color or material.

Georgia does not issue a separate license for roofing. The Secretary of State's contractor licensing board lists shingle, sheet metal and flat roofing as specialty trades, so a roofer working only within that specialty is not required to hold a state residential or general contractor license. That makes your own checks more important. Ask for a physical business address, proof of liability and workers compensation insurance, and local references from recent jobs. Ask who will pull any required City of Alpharetta permit and arrange inspections, and get the manufacturer's warranty and the contractor's workmanship warranty in writing.

After a hail or wind storm, document the damage with photos and contact your insurance company before signing anything with a contractor. Be wary of anyone who shows up uninvited, offers to waive your deductible, asks you to sign a contract that gives them control of your insurance claim, or wants a large payment before materials are delivered. A good written estimate lists the shingle brand and line, underlayment, ventilation, flashing and how rotted decking will be handled. Replacement work after a major storm can take weeks to schedule, while small repairs are usually quicker.

Tips for hiring roofers in Alpharetta

  • Ask for proof of licensing and insurance before work begins
  • Get photos of any damage and a written scope, especially for insurance claims
  • After hail or wind storms, be wary of door-knocking out-of-town crews

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best roofers in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 5 options for shingle and metal roofs, repairs, replacements and storm damage.

Do roofers need a license in Georgia?

Georgia has no roofing specific license. The state contractor board treats shingle, metal and flat roofing as specialty trades, so check insurance, references and a written warranty instead.

Does my HOA need to approve a new roof in Alpharetta?

Many HOAs require approval for roof color or material. Check your community's covenants and submit any required request before work starts.
