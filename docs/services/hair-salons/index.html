Choosing a hair salon in Alpharetta: what to know

Alpharetta has everything from one chair studios in salon suite buildings to busy full service salons with a dozen stylists. Before you book, decide what you actually need. A precision cut, a big color change, extensions and keratin treatments all call for different skills, so look for a stylist whose portfolio shows recent work on hair like yours, not just polished photos from a product brand.

Georgia requires every salon to be licensed by the Georgia State Board of Cosmetology and Barbers before it opens, and Board rules say the salon license, or a printout of its online verification, must be posted within fifteen feet of the entrance. Each stylist must also display a current license, and the most recent inspection report has to be posted where customers can see it. In buildings divided into salon suites, licenses should make clear which business each stylist works under. If you cannot spot these documents, ask. A licensed professional will not mind.

For color or chemical services, ask whether the stylist does a consultation and a strand or patch test first, how long the appointment will take, and what the policy is if you are unhappy with the result. Red flags include tools that look unwashed, reused disposable items, and pressure to add services you did not request. Popular stylists in North Fulton often book out weeks ahead of holidays, proms and wedding season, so plan early for big events and confirm the cancellation policy in writing.

Tips for hiring hair salons in Alpharetta

  • Book color appointments a week or two ahead, especially before holidays
  • Ask for a consultation before a big color change
  • Check whether the salon charges by stylist level

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best hair salons in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 10 options for haircuts, color, highlights, extensions and blowouts.

Do hair salons need a license in Georgia?

Yes. Salons must be licensed by the Georgia State Board of Cosmetology and Barbers before doing business, and each stylist needs an individual license or permit. Both should be posted in an open area of the salon.

How can I check a salon's inspection record?

Georgia Board rules require salons to post their most recent inspection report in an open area. You can ask to see it if it is not obvious when you walk in.

What should I ask before a major color change?

Ask about a consultation, a strand test, the expected time in the chair, and the salon's policy for corrections. Bring photos of what you want and what you do not want.

Do these hair salons serve all of Alpharetta?
