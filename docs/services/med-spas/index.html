Best Spas and Med Spas in Alpharetta, GA

Local spas and med spas serving Alpharetta and North Fulton: day spas, med spas, massage, facials and recovery treatments.

Looking for the best spas and med spas in Alpharetta, Georgia? Here are 11 local providers for day spas, med spas, massage, facials and recovery treatments in and around Alpharetta, with addresses and phone numbers where available.

Choosing a med spa in Alpharetta

Alpharetta's spas and med spas cluster around Avalon, downtown and the Windward and North Point corridors. Common treatments include facials, injectables, laser treatments, cryotherapy, IV therapy and infrared sauna. Ask who will perform your treatment, what training and licensing they have and which devices they use, and book a consultation before committing to a package.

Tips for hiring spas and med spas in Alpharetta

  • Ask who performs treatments and their credentials
  • Book a consultation before committing to a treatment plan
  • Ask about package pricing for recurring treatments

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best spas and med spas in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 11 options for day spas, med spas, massage, facials and recovery treatments.

What treatments do Alpharetta med spas offer?

Common offerings include facials, injectables, laser treatments, body contouring, cryotherapy, IV therapy and infrared sauna. Menus vary, so check the spa's site or call.

How do I choose a safe med spa?

Ask who performs injectables and lasers and what their medical license and training are, confirm physician oversight, and start with a consultation before buying a package.

Do these spas and med spas serve all of Alpharetta?

Most serve all Alpharetta ZIP codes, including 30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022, plus nearby Milton, Roswell and Johns Creek. Call to confirm they cover your address.
