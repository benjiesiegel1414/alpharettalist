Hiring a handyman in Alpharetta: what to know

A good handyman is ideal for the punch list that piles up in any house: patching drywall, hanging doors, fixing trim, mounting TVs, replacing fixtures and small carpentry jobs. Before you call, write down every task with photos so you can get a clear quote and avoid repeat trip charges. Ask whether the handyman bills by the hour or by the job, who buys materials, and whether they can finish everything in one visit or need to come back.

Georgia's state consumer protection office notes that repair handymen are generally exempt from the state's residential and general contractor license, but utility trades are different. Plumbing, electrical and air conditioning work is regulated by the Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board, so a handyman should not be taking on that work unless properly licensed. Larger projects that go beyond repairs may also require a licensed contractor and building permits. Ask for proof of liability insurance and any city or county business license, and be wary if someone asks you to pull the permits yourself.

The state lists other red flags worth knowing: door to door solicitation, cash only payment, pressure to decide on the spot, and requests to pay for the whole job before work starts. Get the task list, completion date and price in writing, and pay in stages for anything larger than a short visit. Reliable handymen in North Fulton often book out a few weeks, especially before the holidays and in spring, so reach out early if you are preparing to sell or host guests.

Tips for hiring handyman services in Alpharetta

  • Bundle small jobs into one visit to save on trip charges
  • Larger electrical, plumbing or HVAC work needs a licensed trade
  • Ask whether they charge hourly or by the job

Does a handyman need a license in Georgia?

Georgia's consumer protection office says repair handymen are generally exempt from the state residential contractor license. Plumbing, electrical and HVAC work requires a license from the Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board.

Should I pull permits for my handyman?

Georgia's consumer office lists a contractor asking you to get the required permits as a warning sign. The person doing the work should know what permits are needed.

How do I get an accurate handyman quote?

Send a written list of every task with photos and measurements. Ask whether the quote is hourly or fixed and whether materials are included.
