Hiring a landscaping or lawn care company in Alpharetta: what to know

Alpharetta lawns deal with red clay soil, hot humid summers, heavy spring pollen and occasional drought. Many yards are planted with warm season grasses such as Bermuda or zoysia, while shady lots under mature trees often struggle to keep any grass at all. Before getting quotes, decide whether you need weekly mowing, seasonal fertilization and weed control, or design and installation work such as new beds, drainage or hardscaping. If you live in an HOA community, check whether plant choices, fences or new features need approval.

Any business that applies pesticides, including weed control and insect treatments, to someone else's property for a fee must hold a pesticide contractor license from the Georgia Department of Agriculture, and lawn care companies are specifically included. Ask for the license if a company offers chemical treatments. Georgia's statewide outdoor watering rules also matter for new installs. Outside of drought restrictions, irrigation of lawns and plants is allowed daily between 4 p.m. and 10 a.m., while new plantings are exempt during installation and for 30 days after, and drip irrigation, soaker hoses and hand watering are exempt at any time. A good contractor will set irrigation timers within those rules.

Get written proposals that spell out the schedule, what each visit includes, who supplies plants and mulch, and any guarantee on new plants. Ask whether the crew is employed by the company or subcontracted, and confirm liability insurance. Red flags include no written agreement, pressure to prepay a full season, and treatments applied without notice. Spring and fall are the busiest seasons in North Fulton, so book installation projects early.

Tips for hiring landscaping and lawn care companies in Alpharetta

  • Check your HOA's landscaping rules before major changes
  • Ask about irrigation checks heading into summer
  • Get itemized quotes for design-and-install projects

Do lawn care companies need a license in Georgia?

A company that applies pesticides, including weed control, to other people's property for a fee needs a pesticide contractor license from the Georgia Department of Agriculture.

What are the watering rules in Alpharetta?

Under Georgia's non-drought outdoor water use rules, landscape irrigation is allowed daily between 4 p.m. and 10 a.m. New plantings for 30 days, drip irrigation and hand watering are exempt.
