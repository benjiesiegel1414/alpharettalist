Choosing a wedding or event venue in Alpharetta: what to know

Alpharetta and the surrounding North Fulton area offer everything from historic homes and gardens to hotel ballrooms, rustic barns and modern event spaces. Begin with your guest count and the feel you want, then visit venues at the same time of day as your event so you can judge lighting, parking and noise. Ask whether the ceremony and reception can be held in the same place, and what the rain plan is for any outdoor space.

Read the contract closely before you sign. Ask what is included, such as tables, chairs, linens and setup, whether you must use the venue's caterer or an approved vendor list, and whether outside alcohol is allowed and who must serve it. Clarify the deposit and payment schedule, cancellation and postponement terms, the exact hours you have access, and any noise curfew. Find out whether a venue coordinator will be on site during the event and whether liability insurance is required. Popular spring and fall Saturdays book far ahead, so weekday or off season dates can open more options.

Do not forget the marriage license. Georgia marriage licenses are issued by county probate courts. If at least one of you is a Georgia resident, the license can come from any Georgia county, and if neither of you lives in Georgia, it must come from the county where the ceremony will take place. Both partners must be at least 18. Couples who complete a qualifying premarital education program of at least six hours within 12 months before applying are not charged the license fee.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best wedding and event venues in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 7 options for wedding, reception and private event venues.

Where do I get a marriage license for a wedding in Alpharetta?

Marriage licenses come from Georgia county probate courts. If one partner lives in Georgia, any county can issue it; if neither does, apply in the county where the ceremony will be held.

Does Georgia have a premarital education discount?

Yes. Couples who present a certificate from a qualifying premarital education program of at least six hours, completed within 12 months, are not assessed the marriage license fee.

How far ahead should I book a wedding venue?

Popular Saturdays in spring and fall often book many months ahead. Tour early and get your date in a signed contract.

Do these wedding and event venues serve all of Alpharetta?

Most serve all Alpharetta ZIP codes, including 30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022, plus nearby Milton, Roswell and Johns Creek. Call to confirm they cover your address.
