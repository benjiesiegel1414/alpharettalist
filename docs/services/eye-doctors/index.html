Choosing an eye doctor in Alpharetta: what to know

Start by knowing which kind of eye doctor fits your visit. Optometrists handle routine eye exams, glasses and contact lens prescriptions, and many common eye conditions. Ophthalmologists are medical doctors who also perform eye surgery and manage more complex disease. Around Alpharetta you will find optometrists in private practices and inside retail optical stores, while ophthalmology groups usually have dedicated offices and often see patients by referral.

In Georgia, optometrists are licensed through the State Board of Optometry under the Secretary of State, and ophthalmologists, as physicians, are licensed by the Georgia Composite Medical Board, which offers a free online lookup showing license status and any public orders. The optometry board notes that the standard of care for a virtual eye exam is the same as for an in person exam, so ask exactly what an online or kiosk style test includes before relying on it for a prescription.

Vision insurance and medical insurance often work separately, so ask the office which plan will be billed for your visit, especially if you are going for a problem like an infection or floaters rather than a routine exam. Ask whether a contact lens fitting is included or billed separately, and request a copy of your glasses prescription so you can shop around. Back to school season is busy for pediatric exams in North Fulton, so book early in summer. For sudden vision changes, contact a doctor right away rather than waiting for a routine appointment.

Tips for hiring eye doctors in Alpharetta

  • Vision plans and medical insurance cover different things, so ask which applies
  • Bring your current glasses and contact lens boxes to your exam
  • Ask whether dilation is included so you can plan a ride

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best eye doctors in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 2 options for eye exams, contact lens fittings, glasses and eye care.

What is the difference between an optometrist and an ophthalmologist?

Optometrists provide eye exams, prescriptions and care for many common eye conditions. Ophthalmologists are medical doctors who also perform surgery and treat more complex eye disease.

Can I get a copy of my glasses prescription?

Ask the office for a copy after your exam so you can buy glasses wherever you like. Having it on file also helps if you switch providers.

Are virtual eye exams allowed in Georgia?

The Georgia State Board of Optometry says optometrists can perform virtual eye exams, held to the same standard of care as an in person exam. Ask what testing is included before you book.
