Choosing an orthodontist in Alpharetta: what to know

Orthodontic care usually lasts a year or more, so choosing an office in Alpharetta often comes down to convenience as much as anything else. Many visits are short adjustment appointments, and for students that means finding after school or early morning slots near home or school. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have a first check up with an orthodontist by age 7. That visit does not mean treatment will start right away. Often the orthodontist simply monitors growth and recommends a later start.

Orthodontists in Georgia are dentists, and their licenses are issued by the Georgia Board of Dentistry. The board's online license verification shows the license status, expiration date and any public disciplinary actions. At a consultation, ask about the treatment options for your case, such as traditional braces or clear aligners, the expected length of treatment, how often visits will be, and what happens if treatment takes longer than planned. Ask what is included in the quoted fee, including retainers, emergency visits for broken brackets and follow up after the braces come off.

Getting two consultations is common, so compare treatment plans as well as personalities. Ask how the office communicates with your general dentist and how it handles missed appointments during busy school seasons. Red flags include pressure to start treatment the same day, vague answers about what is included, or a plan that does not explain why early treatment is needed for a young child. Keep up regular cleanings with your family dentist throughout treatment.

Tips for hiring orthodontists in Alpharetta

  • Most orthodontists offer a free consultation, so compare two
  • Ask about total treatment cost, payment plans and what retainers cost after
  • Check whether your dental plan includes an orthodontic benefit

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 7 options for braces and clear aligners for kids, teens and adults.

What age should a child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic check up by age 7. Treatment may not begin then, but the orthodontist can monitor growth and spot issues early.

How do I verify an orthodontist's license in Georgia?

Orthodontists are licensed dentists, so you can check their license through the Georgia Board of Dentistry's online license verification.
