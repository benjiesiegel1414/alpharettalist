Choosing a hotel in Alpharetta: what to know

Where you stay in Alpharetta depends on why you are visiting. The city's tourism office lists hotels clustered around Avalon and Old Milton Parkway, along North Point Parkway, and on Windward Parkway. Business travelers often pick a hotel close to their meetings to avoid crossing GA 400 at rush hour, while leisure visitors may prefer to be within walking distance of Avalon's shops and restaurants or a short ride from downtown Alpharetta.

If you are coming for a concert at Ameris Bank Amphitheatre, a tournament or a wedding, book early. Big event weekends can fill rooms across the area, and group blocks usually have a cutoff date. Check whether the hotel offers a shuttle, since some Alpharetta properties do, and confirm parking arrangements, especially near Avalon. Alpharetta's tourism office notes the city is about 30 minutes from Atlanta, but traffic on GA 400 can stretch that considerably, so plan airport trips with extra time.

Before booking, compare the total stay rather than the nightly rate alone, including taxes, resort or parking fees and pet fees. Read the cancellation policy closely, especially on prepaid rates booked through third party sites, which can be harder to change. Ask whether breakfast is included, whether the hotel has meeting space or a pool if you need it, and how far the nearest restaurants are on foot. Booking directly with the hotel can make special requests and changes easier.

Tips for hiring hotels in Alpharetta

  • Weekday rates near the office parks can be higher than weekends
  • Ask whether parking is free or valet only
  • Book early for big Ameris Bank Amphitheatre concert weekends

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best hotels in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 7 options for hotels near Avalon, downtown and the Windward and North Point business districts.

What areas of Alpharetta have hotels?

Alpharetta's tourism office lists hotels around Avalon and Old Milton Parkway, along North Point Parkway and on Windward Parkway. Choose based on whether you need to be near offices, shopping or event venues.

How far is Alpharetta from Atlanta?

Alpharetta's tourism office describes it as about 30 minutes from Atlanta. Rush hour traffic on GA 400 can add significant time.

Are there pet friendly hotels in Alpharetta?

Yes, several Alpharetta hotels accept pets. Confirm the pet policy, weight limits and any fees when you book.
