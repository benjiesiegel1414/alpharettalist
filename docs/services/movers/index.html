Choosing a moving company in Alpharetta: what to know

Whether you are moving across town from Windward to downtown Alpharetta or relocating out of state, the first thing to check is who regulates your mover. For moves within Georgia, moving companies must hold a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity from the Georgia Department of Public Safety, which keeps a list of licensed household goods movers and publishes a maximum rate tariff that licensed movers cannot exceed. For moves that cross state lines, the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration oversees movers, and you can search a company by name or USDOT number on its Protect Your Move site.

Get written estimates from more than one company, ideally after an in person or video walkthrough. Federal guidance lists red flags that apply to any move: a quote given sight unseen, no written estimate, a company that says it will set the price after loading, demands for cash or a large deposit, and a website with no local address or registration information. For interstate moves, your mover should give you the federal booklet Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move before the move.

On moving day, compare your inventory with the mover's and note any damage before you sign at delivery. Standard coverage is limited, so ask about added protection. For an in state move, Georgia requires a written claim to the mover within 90 days, and the Department of Public Safety may help if it is not resolved. Summer and the end of each month book up quickly across North Fulton, so reserve early.

Tips for hiring moving companies in Alpharetta

  • Get at least three in-home or video estimates
  • Book four to six weeks ahead for summer and end-of-month moves
  • Ask whether the quote is binding or an estimate

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best moving companies in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 4 options for local and long-distance moving, packing and storage.

Do movers need a license in Georgia?

Yes. Movers handling household goods within Georgia must hold a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity from the Georgia Department of Public Safety, which publishes a list of licensed movers.

How do I check an interstate moving company?

Use the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration's Protect Your Move site to search a mover or broker by name, USDOT number or MC number and see its complaint history.

How long do I have to file a damage claim on a Georgia move?

For a move within Georgia, submit a written claim to the mover within 90 days. Interstate moves follow federal rules, which allow up to nine months after delivery.

Do these moving companies serve all of Alpharetta?
