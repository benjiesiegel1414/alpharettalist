Choosing a dog groomer or boarding facility in Alpharetta: what to know

Grooming and boarding both mean handing your dog to someone else for hours or days, so trust matters more than convenience. In Georgia, kennel operators, a category the Georgia Department of Agriculture says includes groomers, trainers and boarders, are licensed and inspected by the department's companion animal program. The department offers a free online license search, so check that a groomer or boarding facility appears before your first visit, and use its complaint process if you see problems.

Expect a good facility to ask for proof of vaccinations. Fulton County requires dogs four months and older to be vaccinated against rabies and to carry a county license that runs on the same term as the vaccine, so keep your certificate handy. Ask what other vaccines are required for boarding or daycare, how dogs are grouped during play, how often staff check on boarded dogs overnight, and which veterinarian they call in an emergency. Tour the space, notice the smell and noise level, and ask whether dogs are ever left unattended in dryers or crates.

For grooming, ask how the groomer handles anxious or senior dogs, whether they use cage dryers or hand drying, and how long your dog will be there. Share any health issues, skin sensitivities or behavior concerns at drop off. Red flags include refusing to let you see where dogs are kept, vague answers about supervision, or no vaccination requirements at all. Boarding spots around Alpharetta fill up quickly for Thanksgiving, winter holidays, spring break and summer travel, so reserve well in advance.

Tips for hiring dog groomers and pet boarding in Alpharetta

  • Most groomers and boarders require proof of rabies and other vaccines
  • Book holiday boarding well ahead, since it fills fast
  • Ask whether a trial day is required before boarding

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best dog groomers and pet boarding in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 6 options for dog grooming, boarding, daycare and self-serve dog washes.

Do dog groomers need a license in Georgia?

The Georgia Department of Agriculture licenses kennel operators, which it says includes groomers, trainers and boarders. You can check a business with the department's online license search.

Does Fulton County require a dog license?

Yes. Fulton County requires dogs four months and older to be vaccinated against rabies and licensed, with the license term matching the rabies vaccine.

What vaccines do boarding facilities require?

Rabies is required by law, and many facilities also ask for other vaccines. Ask the facility for its full list before booking.

Do these dog groomers and pet boarding serve all of Alpharetta?

Most serve all Alpharetta ZIP codes, including 30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022, plus nearby Milton, Roswell and Johns Creek. Call to confirm they cover your address.
