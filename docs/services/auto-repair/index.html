Looking for the best auto repair shops in Alpharetta, Georgia? Here are 7 local providers for auto repair, maintenance, tires, brakes and European and Japanese specialists in and around Alpharetta, with addresses and phone numbers where available.

Auto repair in Alpharetta: what to know

Alpharetta has an unusually deep bench of independent shops that specialize in European and Japanese vehicles, which fits a city full of them. Georgia summers are hard on batteries, tires and air conditioning, so many drivers have AC systems checked before the heat arrives.

Ask for a written estimate before work begins, ask whether the shop uses OEM or aftermarket parts, and check whether loaner cars or shuttle service are offered for longer jobs.

Tips for hiring auto repair shops in Alpharetta

  • Ask for a written estimate before repairs begin
  • Ask whether the shop specializes in your make
  • Have the AC and battery checked before summer
