HVAC in Alpharetta: what to know

North Georgia asks a lot of a heating and cooling system: humid 90-degree summers, real winter freezes, and spring pollen that clogs filters fast. Most local companies recommend two tune-ups a year, one for the AC in spring and one for the furnace in fall, plus frequent filter changes during pollen season.

When replacing a system, ask contractors about SEER2 efficiency ratings, proper sizing for your home and any available Georgia Power rebates, and get at least two written quotes. Georgia licenses conditioned air contractors through the Secretary of State, so you can verify a license at sos.ga.gov before hiring.

Tips for hiring hvac and ac repair companies in Alpharetta

  • Book maintenance in spring before the summer rush
  • Ask about maintenance plans, which often include priority service
  • Get a written quote and ask about SEER2 ratings and Georgia Power rebates on new systems

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best hvac and ac repair companies in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 5 options for AC repair, furnace service, new system installation and maintenance plans.

How often should HVAC be serviced in Alpharetta?

Most companies recommend twice a year: an AC tune-up in spring and a furnace check in fall. Change filters more often during spring pollen season.

How long does an HVAC system last in Georgia?

Typically about 12 to 15 years. Frequent repairs, rising power bills or uneven temperatures are signs it may be time to replace.

Do HVAC contractors need a license in Georgia?

Yes. Georgia licenses HVAC contractors as conditioned air contractors through the State Construction Industry Licensing Board. A Class I license is restricted to systems up to 175,000 BTU of heating and 60,000 BTU of cooling, and a Class II license is unrestricted.

How do I check an HVAC contractor's license in Georgia?

Search the contractor's name or license number in the Georgia Secretary of State's online license verification tool.
