Hiring a house cleaning service in Alpharetta: what to know

House cleaning in Alpharetta usually comes in two forms: an independent cleaner who works on their own, or a company that sends a crew. Independent cleaners often offer more consistency because the same person comes every visit, while companies can cover sick days and larger homes. Decide what you want before calling around. A recurring weekly or biweekly clean, a one time deep clean, a move out clean and post construction cleanup are different jobs, and a clear checklist helps you compare quotes fairly.

Ask every service whether it carries liability insurance and, for companies, whether workers are covered if they are injured in your home. Ask whether cleaners are employees or contractors, whether background checks are done, and who will actually show up. Find out what supplies they bring, whether they will use your products if you have pets, allergies or delicate surfaces, and how they handle keys, garage codes and alarm systems. A walkthrough before the first visit is a good sign.

Get the scope, schedule and cancellation policy in writing, along with what happens if something is damaged or a room is missed. Red flags include a service that will not put anything in writing, asks for large payments up front, or sends a different crew every time without notice. Spring cleaning, the weeks before Thanksgiving and the busy summer moving season in North Fulton fill calendars quickly, so book a deep clean or move out clean several weeks ahead.

Tips for hiring house cleaning services in Alpharetta

  • Ask whether cleaners are employees or contractors, and whether they're insured and bonded
  • Schedule a deep clean first, then switch to recurring service
  • Clarify what's included, such as inside the oven or fridge

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best house cleaning services in Alpharetta, GA?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Alpharetta, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 2 options for recurring house cleaning, deep cleans and move-in or move-out cleaning.

Should a house cleaner be insured?

Yes, ask for proof of liability insurance before the first visit. It protects you if something in your home is damaged during cleaning.

What is the difference between a regular clean and a deep clean?

A regular clean maintains surfaces, floors, kitchens and bathrooms. A deep clean adds detailed work such as baseboards, inside appliances and built up grime, and usually takes much longer.

Should I tip my house cleaner?

Tipping is common but optional. Many people tip for deep cleans, at the holidays, or when a cleaner goes beyond the usual checklist.
