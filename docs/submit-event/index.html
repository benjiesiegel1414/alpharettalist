Submit an Alpharetta event

Community events, fundraisers, grand openings, shows and classes in or near Alpharetta. Free to submit.

Hosting something in Alpharetta? Email us the details below and we'll add it to the calendar for free.

Email hello@alpharettalist.com

What to include

  • Event name
  • Date(s) and start and end time
  • Venue name and street address
  • Price, or free
  • A short description of the event
  • Link to the official event page or tickets
  • A photo or flyer, if you have one (attach it)

Send events at least a week ahead when possible. We may lightly edit descriptions for length.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of events do you list?

Community festivals, concerts, markets, fundraisers, holiday events, grand openings, classes and family activities in Alpharetta or close by in Milton, Roswell and Johns Creek. Events should be open to the public.

Does it cost anything to add an event?

No. Adding an event to the Alpharetta events calendar is free.

How far ahead should I send my event?

At least a week ahead is best, and earlier for big seasonal events. We check event details against the official event page before publishing.

Can I update an event after it's listed?

Yes. Email us the event name and what changed, such as a new date, time, venue or price, and we'll update the listing.

Good to know
  • Listing events is free
  • We focus on events in or near Alpharetta
  • Recurring events can go in one email
