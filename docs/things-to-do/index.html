Things to do in Alpharetta, GA

Parks and trails, live music, family fun, shopping districts and easy day trips.

Alpharetta packs a lot into one city: start at Avalon or the Town Green in Downtown Alpharetta, walk or ride the Big Creek Greenway, catch a summer show at Ameris Bank Amphitheatre, and let the kids loose at Wills Park. For what's on this week, see our events calendar.

Avalon

Alpharetta's 86-acre upscale mixed-use district: dozens of shops and restaurants along the Boulevard, the Regal Avalon 12 theater, The Hotel at Avalon, and a plaza with bocce, swings and fire pits. Home to a seasonal ice rink, tree lighting and Santa visits each winter.
